CTAE Udaipur Rankings & Key Highlights

Established in 1962, College of Technology and Engineering (CTAE), Udaipur is one of Rajasthan's oldest and most reputed engineering institutions. As a constituent college of MPUAT, it holds significant recognitions:

NIRF & State Ranking

Ranked among the top government engineering colleges in Rajasthan. One of the oldest technical institutions in the state with over 60 years of academic excellence and a strong alumni network.

AICTE Approved

All B.Tech programs at CTAE are approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE). The parent university MPUAT is recognized by UGC, ensuring full national academic validity for all degrees awarded.

Unique Mining & Agri Programs

CTAE Udaipur is one of the very few colleges in Rajasthan offering B.Tech in Mining Engineering and Agricultural Engineering, catering to Udaipur's mineral-rich region and MPUAT's agricultural mandate.

Academic Departments & Engineering Specializations

CTAE Udaipur hosts several well-established academic departments providing leading-edge technical specializations. Students can opt for key disciplines across technology, core engineering, and specialized sectors:

Computer Science & IT

Focuses on current trends including Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Science, Cyber Security, and Software Development for next-generation tech careers.

Core Engineering

Includes Civil, Mechanical, Electrical Engineering, and Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE) — the backbone of industrial growth and infrastructure development.

Mining Engineering

A unique and high-demand program given Udaipur's proximity to major mineral belts. Graduates are sought by Vedanta-HZL, Hindustan Zinc, mining PSUs, and marble/granite industry companies.

Agricultural Engineering

Offered under MPUAT's mandate, this program covers farm machinery, irrigation systems, and agro-processing technology. Admission via both REAP counseling and ICAR All-India quota.

M.Tech Programs

Postgraduate M.Tech admissions conducted through GATE scores or academic merit, covering major engineering specializations including Computer Science, Electronics, and Civil Engineering.

Ph.D Research Programs

CTAE offers doctoral research programs across engineering disciplines, fostering innovation and advanced research under experienced faculty in collaboration with MPUAT and government research bodies.

CTAE Udaipur Detailed Fee Structure

Tuition fees at CTAE Udaipur are structured based on the allotted seat category (GAS vs SFS). Below is the comprehensive annual cost breakdown for B.Tech students:

Annual Academic Fees

  • GAS Subsidized Seats: ₹38,000 – ₹45,000 / Year (Includes academic tuition fees, university exam charges, and development fees). Exact amount subject to government revision.
  • SFS Self-Financed Seats: ₹70,000 – ₹85,000 / Year (Includes full tuition fees, university development charges, and general amenities).
  • TFWS (Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme): ~₹15,000 / Year (Tuition component waived; candidates pay only basic development and exam charges). Family income below ₹8 LPA required.
  • Total 4-Year Program Cost: Approximately ₹1.5 Lakh – ₹3.4 Lakh total depending on GAS or SFS seat category, making CTAE one of the most affordable engineering colleges in Rajasthan.

Hostel & Mess Accommodation

  • Room Rent (Shared): ₹24,000 – ₹27,000 / Year (Includes electricity, water supply, and security services). Subject to room type and availability.
  • One-Time Admission Charges: Approximately ₹8,800 at the time of hostel admission (includes security deposit and other one-time levies).
  • Mess Charges (Fooding): Approximately ₹3,000 – ₹3,500 / Month (Cooperative mess system, billed monthly based on menu/diet selection).
  • Hostel Caution Money: Refundable deposit collected at admission; returned fully upon check-out as per hostel rules.

CTAE Udaipur Admissions & REAP Guide

B.Tech admissions at CTAE Udaipur are conducted through centralized counseling coordinated by REAP (Rajasthan Engineering Admission Process) based on JEE Main scores. Agricultural Engineering has additional ICAR quota seats.

Eligibility & Seat Quota Rules

  • Home State Quota (85%): Reserved for candidates holding a valid Rajasthan Domicile. Allocations are based primarily on JEE Main CRL ranks via REAP counseling.
  • All India Quota (15%): Open to candidates from all states outside Rajasthan. Allocations are based purely on JEE Main CRL ranks.
  • ICAR Quota (Agricultural Engineering): Approximately 8 seats filled through ICAR-AIEEA (All India Entrance Examination for Admission) conducted by the Indian Council for Agricultural Research separately.
  • Academic Requirements: Minimum 45% aggregate in 10+2 (Class 12th) with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects, alongside Chemistry/Biotechnology/Technical Vocational subject.

Reporting Document Checklist

  • Allotment Letter: REAP Provisional Seat Allotment Letter (printed from the REAP portal).
  • Test Scores: JEE Main CRL scorecard or REAP Merit Certificate (Class 12th based merit).
  • Certificates: Original Domicile, Caste Category (SC/ST/OBC/EWS/MBC), and Income Certificates (for TFWS seats).
  • School Records: Class 10th Certificate (DOB proof), Class 12th Marks Sheet, Transfer Certificate (TC), and Migration Certificate.
  • Photographs: Passport-size photographs as specified in the REAP allotment letter (typically 6–8 copies).

CTAE Udaipur Campus Life & Infrastructure

Set in the scenic Aravalli hills near the "City of Lakes," CTAE Udaipur offers a vibrant campus experience with state-of-the-art academic infrastructure and rich student life:

Campus & Academic Facilities

  • Expansive Campus: Spread over a lush green campus in Pratap Nagar, Udaipur, with dedicated department buildings, well-equipped labs, and green spaces amidst the Aravalli landscape.
  • Advanced Laboratories: State-of-the-art labs including a Mining Simulation Lab, Agricultural Engineering Workshop, Computer Science labs with high-speed internet, and specialized ECE/EE labs.
  • Central Library: A well-stocked library with thousands of engineering reference books, digital journals, IEEE database access, and research archives.
  • MPUAT Research Network: Being part of MPUAT, students benefit from the university's extensive agricultural and engineering research ecosystem and interdisciplinary collaboration.

Fests & Student Activities

  • Annual Techno-Cultural Fest: Features technical project exhibitions, robotics competitions, coding hackathons, cultural performances, and expert guest lectures from industry leaders.
  • Student Technical Clubs: Active chapters including Robotics & Automation Club, Mining Engineers Association, Coding Club, and Electronics Enthusiasts Club.
  • NSS & NCC: Active chapters of National Service Scheme (NSS) and National Cadet Corps (NCC) foster leadership, discipline, and social responsibility among CTAE students.
  • Sports Facilities: Well-maintained grounds for Cricket, Football, Volleyball, Basketball, and indoor facilities for Badminton and Table Tennis. Annual inter-department sports meets.

  • Credit System: Semester-based credit system following MPUAT guidelines, with courses structured as Lecture-Tutorial-Practical (L-T-P) hours.

Frequently Asked Questions about CTAE Udaipur

Are the expected B.Tech seat cutoffs for CTAE Udaipur guaranteed?
No, actual seat allotments are governed entirely by the official REAP counseling algorithm based on your rank, locked choices, vacancy status, and seat reservation rules. The expected cutoff tiers provided on this page represent statistical estimations based on historical REAP opening and closing cutoff ranks.
Can I upgrade from an SFS seat to a GAS seat after admission at CTAE?
Yes. During the REAP upward sliding round and internal sliding phases conducted at the CTAE Udaipur campus, candidates can request upgrading their seat type from SFS (Self-Financing Seat) to GAS (Government Aided Seat) in their branch if seats fall vacant.
What is the family income limit for TFWS seats at CTAE Udaipur?
To qualify for the Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme (TFWS) supernumerary seats (5% per branch), the candidate's family annual income from all sources must be less than ₹8 Lakhs. Candidates must present a valid income certificate issued by a competent authority during REAP counseling.
Are hostel facilities available for all students at CTAE Udaipur?
Yes. Hostel facilities at CTAE Udaipur are provided for both boys and girls on campus. Allocation is based on merit/rank list at the time of admission, irrespective of GAS or SFS seat type. Annual hostel charges are approximately ₹24,000–₹27,000 plus one-time admission charges of around ₹8,800 and monthly mess fees.
Is CTAE Udaipur a government college?
Yes. College of Technology and Engineering (CTAE), Udaipur is a constituent college of Maharana Pratap University of Agriculture and Technology (MPUAT), which is a public state university established and funded by the Government of Rajasthan. All B.Tech programs are approved by AICTE, ensuring national academic validity.
What is the ICAR quota in Agricultural Engineering at CTAE?
Agricultural Engineering at CTAE Udaipur has approximately 8 seats reserved under the Indian Council for Agricultural Research (ICAR) quota. These seats are filled through a separate ICAR-AIEEA (All India Entrance Examination for Admission) exam conducted by ICAR, in addition to the regular REAP counseling seats. Candidates must apply separately on the ICAR portal for these seats.
Why should I choose Mining Engineering at CTAE Udaipur?
Mining Engineering at CTAE Udaipur is a unique and in-demand program. Udaipur is located in the heart of Rajasthan's mineral-rich belt — home to Hindustan Zinc (the world's largest integrated zinc producer), marble quarries, and extensive mineral deposits. Graduates of Mining Engineering find strong placement opportunities with Vedanta Group (HZL), government mining PSUs, and global mining companies.
How can I check my CTAE/MPUAT semester result online?
To check your semester result, visit the official MPUAT university portal at www.mpuat.ac.in or the dedicated CTAE student portal at www.ctae.ac.in. Select your program and semester, enter your enrollment number/roll number, and view your result. You may also check results via the MPUAT examination section directly.
