Frequently Asked Questions

A Rank Predictor is a free tool that estimates your likely rank or position for admissions based on your entrance exam score (JEE Main percentile or board marks), category, and domicile. It compares your score against historical cutoff trends to show expected rank ranges that help with planning choices.

Famral offers free rank predictors for four major state engineering counseling processes: REAP (Rajasthan), MP DTE (Madhya Pradesh), HSTES (Haryana), and ACPC (Gujarat). Each predictor is tailored to the respective state's counseling rules, seat matrix, and historical rank-cutoff data.

JEE Main percentile is accepted as the primary merit criterion for REAP, MP DTE, and HSTES counseling. ACPC (Gujarat) additionally supports GUJCET scores. Each predictor specifies which score to enter for the most accurate results.
