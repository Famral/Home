Understanding WBJEE Marks vs Rank & Counseling Process

The West Bengal Joint Entrance Examinations Board (WBJEEB) conducts WBJEE for admission to Undergraduate Courses in Engineering & Technology, Pharmacy, and Architecture in Universities, Government Colleges, and Self-Financed Institutes across West Bengal. Merit lists are prepared based on total marks obtained in Mathematics (Paper-I) and Physics & Chemistry (Paper-II).

Key Factors Influencing WBJEE General Merit Rank (GMR)

  • Mathematics Weightage: Mathematics accounts for 100 out of 200 total marks (50% weightage). High scores in Mathematics strongly boost your overall GMR rank.
  • Negative Marking & Category-3 Questions: WBJEE features 3 categories of questions. Incorrect answers in Category 1 incur -0.25 negative marks and Category 2 incur -0.5 negative marks, making accuracy critical.
  • State Domicile Reservation (85% HS Quota): State government engineering colleges in West Bengal reserve 100% of their seats exclusively for West Bengal Domicile candidates.
  • Tie-Breaking Rules: In case of equal total score, tiebreaker order is: 1) Higher positive marks in Mathematics, 2) Less negative marks in Mathematics, 3) Higher positive marks in Physics, 4) Less negative marks in Physics.

WBJEE Merit Rules & Category Reservations

Summary of WBJEE state merit compilation rules, domicile requirements, and category-wise reservation quota breakdown for West Bengal admissions.

Eligibility & Merit Compilation

  • Examination Structure: Total 200 marks (Paper 1 Maths = 100 marks, Paper 2 Physics = 50 marks & Chemistry = 50 marks).
  • GMR vs PMR: GMR (General Merit Rank) is compiled on total 200 marks for Engineering/Tech. PMR (Pharmacy Merit Rank) is compiled on 100 marks (Physics + Chemistry) for Pharmacy.
  • State Domicile Rules: State government engineering colleges are reserved 100% for West Bengal Domicile candidates.

Category Reservations

  • OBC-A / OBC-B / SC / ST / EWS: Reserved seats applicable only for West Bengal domicile candidates (OBC-A 10%, OBC-B 7%, SC 22%, ST 6%, EWS 10%).
  • TFW Scheme: 5% additional supernumerary seats in all participating engineering colleges for Tuition Fee Waiver (TFW) eligible candidates (family income < ₹2.5 Lakh/annum).
  • PwD Quota: 5% horizontal reservation for Persons with Disabilities across all vertical categories.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the WBJEE Rank Predictor and how does it work?
The WBJEE Rank Predictor is a free tool that estimates your West Bengal Joint Entrance Examination General Merit Rank (GMR) and Pharmacy Merit Rank (PMR) from your expected marks out of 200. It uses verified score-vs-rank data across ~1.1–1.3 lakh annual examinees to compute accurate rank ranges.
What marks are required for a GMR under 1,000 in WBJEE?
To secure a General Merit Rank (GMR) under 1,000 in WBJEE, a candidate typically needs a total score of approximately 102–108+ out of 200 marks. Scores above 130+ out of 200 generally yield a GMR under 150, making candidates eligible for top CSE branches at Jadavpur University.
What is the difference between GMR and PMR in WBJEE?
GMR (General Merit Rank) is calculated using total marks in Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry (200 marks) and is used for admission into Engineering, Technology, and Architecture courses. PMR (Pharmacy Merit Rank) is calculated using Physics and Chemistry marks (100 marks) for B.Pharm admissions.
Are candidates from outside West Bengal eligible for government colleges?
No, state government engineering colleges in West Bengal (such as KGEC, JGEC, GCELT, GCECT) and reserved category seats are strictly for West Bengal Domicile candidates. However, Jadavpur University offers All India seats in select categories, and non-WB candidates are eligible for All India seats in private engineering colleges.
How does Mathematics score act as a tiebreaker in WBJEE?
If two or more candidates obtain identical total marks in WBJEE, WBJEEB applies tie-breaking rules starting with higher positive marks in Mathematics. If still tied, fewer negative marks in Mathematics, followed by higher positive marks in Physics, are evaluated.
