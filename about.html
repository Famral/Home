Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

We built Famral on the belief that every student deserves equal access to the information they need to choose the right college.

Our Mission

To make college admissions simple, transparent, and accessible for every student in India. We eliminate uncertainty during the counselling process by providing highly accurate college prediction tools, up-to-date admission information, and practical guidance that empowers students to make confident academic decisions.


At Famral, we are constantly expanding our platform with new prediction tools, advanced analytics, and comprehensive counselling resources so that no student has to navigate admissions blindly.

Our Vision

To become India's most trusted and comprehensive platform for college counselling, admission insights, and educational decision-making — one where every student, regardless of their background or location, can access the same quality of guidance that was previously available only to a privileged few.


We envision a future where data-driven tools democratise higher education access across all 28 states and 8 union territories of India.

Everything You Need for Admissions

From predicting your rank to choosing the best college, our suite of free tools covers every step of the Indian counselling journey.

College Predictor

Enter your rank or score and instantly see which colleges you're likely to get admission into — backed by historical cutoff data from JEE, CUET, MHT CET, REAP, and 40+ more exams.

Rank Predictor

Convert your exam score into an estimated rank using normalisation formulas aligned with the official methodology of each exam — JEE Main, JEE Advanced, CUET, GATE, and more.

Percentile Predictor

Cutoff Trends & Analysis

Browse year-wise opening and closing ranks for thousands of college-branch combinations. Understand how cutoffs move and plan your choices accordingly.

College Comparison

Choice Filling Guidance

Counselling Guides & Resources
