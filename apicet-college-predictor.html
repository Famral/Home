AP ICET 2026 Exam Pattern and Marking Scheme

APICET (Andhra Pradesh Integrated Common Entrance Test) is a state-level Computer Based Test conducted by APSCHE for admission to MBA and MCA programs in Andhra Pradesh universities and affiliated colleges.

Sectional Weightage and Question Types

  • Analytical Ability: 75 Questions (75 marks) - Data Sufficiency, Problem Solving, Date and Clock problems, and Analytical Sequences and Series.
  • Mathematical Ability: 75 Questions (75 marks) - Arithmetical Ability, Algebraical Ability, Statistical Ability, covering topics like percentages, profit and loss, statistics, and mensuration.
  • Communication Ability: 50 Questions (50 marks) - Vocabulary, Business Correspondence, Functional Grammar, and Reading Comprehension. This section is only in English.
  • Total Score: 200 Questions for 200 Marks. Duration: 150 Minutes (2.5 hours). No Negative Marking. Computer-Based Test (CBT).

Key Rules and Eligibility

  • Minimum Qualifying Marks: OC candidates must score at least 25% (50 out of 200). There is no minimum qualifying mark for SC and ST categories, though admission depends on available seats.
  • No Negative Marking: Each correct answer carries +1 mark. Incorrect or unattempted answers carry 0 marks - attempt all questions.
  • Eligibility for MBA: Any Bachelor's degree (3 or 4-year) from a UGC/AICTE recognized university with minimum 50% aggregate (45% for BC/SC/ST).
  • Eligibility for MCA: Bachelor's degree with Mathematics as one of the subjects at 10+2 level or graduation. Minimum 50% aggregate marks required.

APICET Admission Process and Counselling

APICET admissions are a centralized process managed by APSCHE. After the entrance test, counselling is conducted on the official portal where candidates fill preferences and seats are allotted based on rank and category.

Step-by-Step APICET Admission Process

  • Step 1: Register and Appear for APICET: Register on cets.apsche.ap.gov.in. Download hall ticket and appear for the 150-minute Computer Based Test. Results are declared by APSCHE.
  • Step 2: Counselling Registration: Qualified candidates register for centralized counselling on the official portal. Submit required documents including category certificate, local status certificate, and qualifying degree marks.
  • Step 3: Fill College and Course Preferences: Select your preferred colleges and courses (MBA/MCA) in order of preference. You can select up to 100+ options from 480+ participating colleges.
  • Step 4: Seat Allotment and Reporting: Seats are allotted based on rank, category, regional status and preferences. Multiple allotment phases are held. Allotted candidates report to college with original documents for verification.

Key Advantages of APICET MBA and MCA Programs

Why APICET is the preferred gateway for MBA and MCA in Andhra Pradesh:

  • University Colleges: State university colleges like Andhra University and SV University offer government-aided MBA/MCA at very affordable fees of Rs 10,000-25,000 per year with strong regional placements.
  • Regional Reservation Benefit: 85% of seats in each college are reserved for local candidates (AU/SVU area). This gives local students a significant advantage in getting admission to colleges in their home region.
  • Wide Choice of Colleges: With 480+ colleges across Andhra Pradesh accepting APICET scores, candidates have an extensive range of options spanning government, autonomous, and private institutions.

Frequently Asked Questions - APICET College Predictor

Common questions about AP ICET college prediction, cutoff ranks, counselling process and MBA/MCA admission in Andhra Pradesh.

What is the APICET College Predictor and how does it work?
The APICET College Predictor is a free tool that uses official AP ICET 2024 closing rank data released by APSCHE to estimate your MBA or MCA admission chances at Andhra Pradesh colleges. Enter your AP ICET rank, category (OC/BC/SC/ST), and course preference. The tool compares your rank against historical closing ranks and shows you which colleges you are likely to get admission into.
What APICET rank is required for Andhra University or top AP colleges?
Based on APICET 2024 official data, AU College of Arts and Science (Andhra University, Visakhapatnam) had an OC closing rank of approximately 584 for MBA. Acharya Nagarjuna University closed at around rank 1,361. SRM University AP and Satya Institute closed around rank 1,897. For top private autonomous colleges, closing ranks are generally within 1,000-5,000 for OC category.
What is the minimum score required to qualify in APICET 2026?
For OC (General) category, candidates must score at least 25% of the total marks i.e., 50 out of 200. For BC category, the minimum qualifying mark is also 25%. For SC and ST categories, there is no minimum qualifying mark requirement. However, actual admission is based on the closing ranks during counselling which depend on the number of applicants and seats available.
What is the difference between AU Region and SVU Region in APICET counselling?
Andhra Pradesh is divided into two university regions for local candidate reservation purposes. AU Region covers districts in the northern part of AP (Visakhapatnam, East Godavari, West Godavari, Krishna, Guntur, etc.) under Andhra University jurisdiction. SVU Region covers the Rayalaseema districts (Kurnool, Kadapa, Anantapur, Chittoor) under Sri Venkateswara University jurisdiction. 85% of seats in each college are reserved for local candidates of the respective region.
How many phases are there in APICET counselling?
APICET counselling is typically conducted in 2-3 main phases by APSCHE. Phase 1 allots seats to the top-ranked candidates. Phase 2 handles vacancies and includes candidates who could not attend Phase 1. A final spot admission round may be conducted by individual colleges for any remaining vacant seats. Closing ranks are generally lower (more competitive) in Phase 1 and increase in subsequent phases.
What documents are required for APICET counselling and college reporting?
Documents required include: APICET 2026 Hall Ticket and Rank Card, Qualifying degree (Bachelor's) marks memos and provisional certificate, 10th and 12th marks memos, Category certificate (BC/SC/ST/EWS) issued by a competent authority, Income certificate (if applicable), Local status certificate (for AU/SVU region eligibility), Transfer certificate, Aadhaar Card, and recent passport-size photographs. Keep originals and attested copies ready.
