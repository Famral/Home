How to Use the IPU CET Predictor for GGSIPU Admissions

Securing a seat in top GGSIPU institutes like USICT, MAIT Rohini, MSIT Janakpuri, VIPS Pitampura, or BVCOE Paschim Vihar requires understanding how JEE Main ranks, IPU CET ranks, 85% Delhi region quotas, and category relaxations work in online counselling rounds. The IPU CET College Predictor matches your rank against official opening-closing cutoffs.

Understanding GGSIPU Counselling & Quota Rules

GGSIPU conducts centralized web-based counselling for B.Tech, BCA, BBA, Law, MCA, and MBA admissions:• 85% Delhi Region Quota: Applies to candidates who completed their qualifying examination (12th Board or Graduation) from a school/institute located inside National Capital Territory (NCT) of Delhi.• 15% Outside Delhi Region Quota: Applies to candidates who completed their qualifying examination from a school/institute located outside NCT of Delhi.• Entrance Exam Channels: B.Tech admissions are based on JEE Main All India Rank. BA LLB/BBA LLB admissions are based on CLAT UG ranks. BCA, BBA, BJMC, B.Ed are based on IPU CET ranks.• Shift 1 vs Shift 2: Top IPU colleges offer Morning (Shift 1) and Evening (Shift 2) sessions. Shift 1 generally has slightly higher closing cutoffs, while degree curriculum and placements are identical.

GGSIPU Counselling Rules, Reservation & Quotas

Overview of regional quotas, reservation percentages across Delhi vs Outside Delhi, and GGSIPU online choice filling mechanics.

Fee Structure & ROI Comparison

  • USS Govt Main Campus (USICT, USCT, USLLS, USMS): Rs. 1.1 - 1.2 Lakhs annual tuition. High ROI with average packages touching Rs. 9.0 - 12.5 LPA.
  • Premier Tier-1 Affiliated (MAIT, MSIT, BVCOE, VIPS): Rs. 1.15 - 1.45 Lakhs annual tuition with average packages ranging Rs. 7.5 - 9.8 LPA.
  • Tier-2 Affiliated (BPIT, ADGITM, GTBIT, JIMS, MSI): Rs. 1.05 - 1.35 Lakhs annual tuition with consistent IT & service placement drives.

Quota & Category Reservation Shifts

  • 85% Delhi Region Quota: Substantially higher closing ranks (easier seat allotment) for candidates completing 12th in Delhi NCR.
  • 15% Outside Delhi Quota: Strictly competitive All India ranks for students passing 12th outside Delhi limits.
  • Delhi OBC (27%): Applicable exclusively to candidates holding valid Delhi OBC certificates recognized by GNCTD.
  • SC / ST / EWS / Defence / PwD: Reserved seats across both Delhi and Outside Delhi regions following GNCTD guidelines.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the IPU CET College Predictor and how does it work?
The IPU CET College Predictor is a free tool that estimates your seat allotment chances in GGSIPU University School of Studies (USICT, USCT, USLLS, USMS) and premier affiliated colleges (MAIT, MSIT, VIPS, BVCOE, BPIT) based on your JEE Main, IPU CET, or CLAT rank, category, and home state quota region (85% Delhi vs 15% Outside Delhi).
How is the 85% Delhi Region Quota determined in GGSIPU?
The 85% Delhi Region quota eligibility is strictly based on the physical location of the school or institute where you completed your qualifying exam (Class 12th for UG courses / Graduation for PG courses). If your school/college is located within NCT Delhi, you belong to the Delhi 85% quota, regardless of your residential address.
Are B.Tech admissions in IPU through JEE Main or IPU CET?
All B.Tech admissions in GGSIPU (USICT, MAIT, MSIT, BVCOE, BPIT, ADGITM, GTBIT) are strictly conducted on the basis of JEE Main Paper-1 All India Ranks. GGSIPU does NOT conduct a separate IPU CET exam for B.Tech. However, IPU CET is conducted for BCA, BBA, MCA, BJMC, and B.Ed.
What JEE Main rank is required for MAIT and MSIT Computer Science (CSE)?
For General category candidates under Delhi Region (85%), MAIT Rohini typically closes around 55,000 JEE Main rank for CSE (Shift 1), while MSIT Janakpuri closes around 58,000 rank. For Outside Delhi candidates (15%), CSE closes around 32,000 to 34,000 rank.
What is the difference between Shift 1 and Shift 2 in IPU colleges?
Shift 1 operates during morning hours (~8:00 AM - 2:30 PM), whereas Shift 2 operates during afternoon hours (~12:30 PM - 7:00 PM). The degree, faculty, syllabus, and placement opportunities are completely identical for both shifts. Shift 1 usually closes at slightly higher ranks.
How does the Sliding Round & Spot Round work in GGSIPU counselling?
After Round 1, 2, and 3, GGSIPU conducts a Sliding Round for candidates who paid the part academic fee without withdrawing. Candidates are upgraded based on preference choices. Spot Rounds are conducted offline/online after sliding rounds to fill remaining vacant seats.
