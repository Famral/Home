How to Use the CAT College Predictor for MBA Admissions

Securing an MBA interview call from top IIMs and premier B-Schools requires understanding how CAT overall percentile, sectional cutoffs, and composite score parameters interact. The CAT College Predictor matches your percentile against official cutoff benchmarks to help you plan your application strategy.

Understanding IIM Call Criteria & Composite Scores

Shortlisting for IIM GD-PI-WAT rounds relies on a composite score formula comprising:• CAT Score (Overall & Sectional Percentiles): Minimum sectional cutoffs (typically 70-85%ile per section) + overall CAT percentile.• Academic Profile: Class 10th & 12th board marks, graduation percentage, and academic discipline.• Diversity Points: Academic diversity (Non-Engineering background) and Gender diversity points.• Work Experience: Quality and duration of full-time work experience (12 to 36 months generally gets maximum points).

Key Features of Top Indian B-School Admissions

  • IIM BLACKI (A, B, C, L, K, I): Top tier IIMs requiring 98.0 to 99.5+ CAT percentile for General category along with strict sectional score cutoffs.
  • CAP (Common Admission Process) IIMs: 10 New/Baby IIMs (Udaipur, Trichy, Ranchi, Raipur, Kashipur, Bodh Gaya, Jammu, Sambalpur, Sirmaur) conduct a single window interview process with cutoffs around 92-93 percentile.
  • FMS Delhi (Best ROI): World-class MBA at under Rs. 2.0 Lakhs total fees with average placements touching Rs. 34.1 LPA. Requires ~98.8 - 99.3+ CAT percentile.
  • IIT B-Schools (SJMSOM, DMS, VGSOM): Excellent ROI B-Schools accepting CAT for 4-year degree / engineering graduates with cutoffs ranging 94 to 98.5+ percentile.

IIM Selection Criteria, Reservation & Fee Structure

Overview of reservation quotas, fee structures across older vs new IIMs and Non-IIMs, and GD-PI-WAT selection mechanics.

Fee Structure & ROI Comparison

  • IIM BLACKI (A, B, C, L, K, I): Rs. 20.5 - 25.0 Lakhs total tuition. Highest average placement packages (Rs. 27.2 - 35.3 LPA).
  • FMS Delhi & IIT B-Schools: Exceptional ROI. FMS Delhi fees under Rs. 2.0 Lakhs (34.1 LPA Avg); IIT B-Schools Rs. 11.0 - 14.0 Lakhs (20.1 - 28.8 LPA Avg).
  • New & CAP IIMs: Rs. 13.0 - 18.9 Lakhs tuition. Average CTC ranging between Rs. 16.0 - 21.0 LPA.
  • Premier Private B-Schools (MDI, SPJIMR, IMT): Rs. 18.0 - 26.0 Lakhs tuition with strong corporate recruiter networks.

Reservation Categories & Cutoff Shifts

  • NC-OBC Quota (27%): Cutoffs typically 5-15 percentile lower than General category (e.g. 94-95 for BLACKI, 77 for CAP IIMs).
  • EWS Quota (10%): Economically Weaker Section cutoffs closely align with NC-OBC percentile benchmarks.
  • SC / ST Quota (15% / 7.5%): Relaxed percentile cutoffs (SC ~80-88 for BLACKI, 60 for CAP; ST ~70-80 for BLACKI, 40 for CAP).
  • PwD Quota (5%): Cutoffs relaxed across all IIMs and management institutes.
  • Sectional Cutoffs: Most top IIMs require minimum sectional percentiles in VARC, DILR, and QA (70%ile - 85%ile).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the CAT College Predictor and how does it work?
The CAT College Predictor is a free online tool that estimates your MBA and PGDM admission call chances across top IIMs and Non-IIM business schools in India based on official CAT overall percentile cutoffs and category benchmarks. Enter your CAT percentile, select your category, and choose your preferred tier or program stream to view instant predictions.
What CAT percentile is required for IIM Ahmedabad, Bangalore, and Calcutta?
For General category candidates with an Engineering background, IIM Ahmedabad, Bangalore, and Calcutta typically require a CAT percentile of 99.2 to 99.5+ along with meeting minimum sectional cutoffs (usually 80-85 percentile in VARC, DILR, QA). Non-engineers and candidates with exceptional academic profiles or work experience may receive calls around 98.0 to 98.5+ percentile.
What is FMS Delhi's CAT cutoff percentile and why is it popular?
Faculty of Management Studies (FMS), Delhi University requires a CAT percentile of approximately 98.8 to 99.3+ for General category. FMS is world-famous for its unmatched Return on Investment (ROI)—charging a total 2-year MBA fee under Rs. 2.0 Lakhs while delivering average placement packages on par with IIM A, B, C (~Rs. 34.1 LPA).
How does CAP (Common Admission Process) work for New & Baby IIMs?
CAP is a single-window centralized interview process conducted for 10 participating New and Baby IIMs (including IIM Udaipur, Trichy, Ranchi, Raipur, Kashipur, Bodh Gaya, Jammu, Sambalpur, Sirmaur). The General category overall CAT percentile cutoff for CAP is typically 92 to 93 percentile, eliminating the need to attend separate interviews for each participating IIM.
Are sectional percentiles mandatory for IIM calls?
Yes, almost all IIMs enforce minimum sectional percentile cutoffs across Verbal Ability & Reading Comprehension (VARC), Data Interpretation & Logical Reasoning (DILR), and Quantitative Aptitude (QA). For older IIMs, sectional cutoffs range between 75 to 85 percentile per section, while for CAP IIMs, sectional cutoffs are generally around 70 percentile per section.
Which top Non-IIM B-Schools accept CAT scores?
In addition to the 21 IIMs, top Non-IIM institutes accepting CAT scores include FMS Delhi, MDI Gurgaon, SPJIMR Mumbai (Profile + Score), IIFT Delhi/Kolkata, SJMSOM IIT Bombay, DMS IIT Delhi, VGSOM IIT Kharagpur, DOMS IIT Madras, IMT Ghaziabad, IMI New Delhi, TAPMI Manipal, FORE School of Management, GIM Goa, and Great Lakes Chennai.
