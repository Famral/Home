Frequently Asked Questions

A College Predictor is a free tool that estimates your B.Tech and degree admission chances in colleges based on your entrance exam score (JEE Main percentile or board marks), category, and domicile. It compares your score against historical counselling cutoffs to show likely, safe, and reach colleges — helping you make smarter choices during counselling.

A Rank Predictor estimates your likely rank or merit position in competitive exams or state counselling processes based on your score, percentile, category, and state domicile quota. It uses official cutoff distributions and mark-vs-rank trends from previous years.

Percentile indicates the percentage of total candidates who scored equal to or below your score in the examination. For example, a 99 percentile in JEE Main or CAT means you scored higher than 99% of all test-takers. Percentage is simply your raw marks divided by the total marks multiplied by 100.
