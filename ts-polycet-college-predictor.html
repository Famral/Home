How to Use the TS POLYCET Predictor for Telangana Admissions

Securing a diploma seat in top Telangana government polytechnics like Govt Polytechnic Masab Tank, JN Govt Poly Ramanthapur, Govt Poly Warangal, Nizamabad, or Karimnagar requires understanding how TS POLYCET state ranks, OU region local quotas (85%), and BC-A/B/C/D/E category reservations work. The TS POLYCET College Predictor matches your rank against official opening-closing cutoffs.

Understanding SBTET Counselling & Quota Rules

SBTET Telangana conducts centralized online web counselling for diploma admissions:• OU Local Region (85% Reservation): Candidates who studied for a minimum of 4 consecutive academic years ending in Telangana State qualify for the 85% Local quota.• Non-Local / Unreserved (15% Quota): Candidates outside Telangana State or those competing in open 15% quota seats.• Backward Class Sub-Categories: Telangana reserves BC seats into BC-A (7%), BC-B (10%), BC-C (1%), BC-D (7%), and BC-E (4%), along with SC (15%), ST (10%), and EWS (10%).• B.Tech Lateral Entry (TS ECET): Completing a 3-year Polytechnic Diploma opens direct admission into 2nd year B.Tech (Lateral Entry) via TS ECET examination.

Top Telangana Government Polytechnics

  • Govt Polytechnic Masab Tank, Hyderabad: The premier government polytechnic in Telangana, established in 1954, famous for CME, ECE, EEE, and Mechanical diploma branches with top industry campus recruitments.

SBTET TS POLYCET Counselling Rules, Reservations & Fee Structure

Overview of regional quotas, reservation percentages across OC, BC-A/B/C/D/E, SC, ST, EWS, and web options mechanics for Telangana polytechnic admissions.

Fee Structure & Fee Reimbursement (Jagananna / TS Post-Matric)

  • Government Polytechnics: Highly subsidized annual tuition fee of approx. Rs. 3,800/- per academic year for all streams.
  • Govt-Aided Polytechnics: Annual fee around Rs. 7,500/- per year with hostel options for girls.
  • Private Self-Financing Polytechnics: Prescribed SBTET tuition fee of Rs. 15,500/- to Rs. 25,000/- per year with full ePASS fee reimbursement eligibility for eligible BC, SC, ST, and EWS candidates.

Local Quota & Category Reservation Ratios

  • OU Local Region (85% Quota): Candidates completing 4 years of education in Telangana receive 85% local seat reservation in all polytechnics.
  • Non-Local / Open Quota (15%): Open to all candidates across India based strictly on TS POLYCET state rank.
  • Backward Classes (BC Sub-Quota): Reserved as BC-A (7%), BC-B (10%), BC-C (1%), BC-D (7%), BC-E (4%).
  • SC / ST / EWS / CAP / PH / NCC: SC (15%), ST (10%), EWS (10%), with horizontal reservations for CAP, PH, and NCC.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the TS POLYCET College Predictor and how does it work?
The TS POLYCET College Predictor is a free tool that estimates your seat allotment chances in Telangana Government & Private Polytechnic Colleges (such as Govt Polytechnic Masab Tank, JN Govt Poly Ramanthapur, Warangal, Nizamabad) based on your SBTET TS POLYCET rank, category (OC, BC-A/B/C/D/E, SC, ST, EWS), and local region quota (OU Local 85% vs Non-Local 15%).
How is the 85% Local Region Quota determined in TS POLYCET?
Local region eligibility is determined if you have studied in educational institutions in Telangana State for a minimum consecutive period of 4 academic years ending with the academic year in which you appeared for the qualifying exam (Class 10th). Eligible students receive 85% local reservation in the OU (Osmania University) local area.
What rank is required for Computer Engineering (CME) in Govt Polytechnic Masab Tank?
For General / OC category candidates under OU Local region, Diploma in Computer Engineering (CME) at Govt Polytechnic Masab Tank typically closes around 1,200 to 1,500 TS POLYCET rank. BC category candidates can get CME around 2,500 to 4,500 rank depending on sub-category.
Can I join B.Tech directly after completing a Polytechnic Diploma?
Yes! After completing a 3-year Polytechnic Diploma from SBTET Telangana, candidates can appear for the TS ECET (Telangana State Engineering Common Entrance Test) to gain direct lateral entry admission into the 2nd year (3rd semester) of B.Tech / B.E. programs in engineering colleges across Telangana.
What are the tuition fees for Government vs Private Polytechnics in Telangana?
Government Polytechnics have a nominal tuition fee of approximately Rs. 3,800 per year. Private self-financing polytechnics charge around Rs. 15,500 to Rs. 25,000 per year. Eligible BC, SC, ST, and EWS students receive full tuition fee reimbursement through Telangana ePASS scheme.
How does SBTET Web Counseling & Choice Option Filling work?
SBTET conducts web counseling after TS POLYCET results. Candidates complete certificate verification at helpline centers, register online, generate password, and submit web options (college and branch priority order). Seat allotment is published based on rank, local quota, and reservation rules.
