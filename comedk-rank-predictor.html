Understanding COMEDK Marks vs Rank & Counseling Process

The Consortium of Medical, Engineering and Dental Colleges of Karnataka (COMEDK) conducts the COMEDK UGET for admission to Undergraduate Engineering (B.E./B.Tech) programmes in over 190 private engineering colleges across Karnataka. The exam comprises 180 multiple-choice questions across Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics — 60 questions each.

Key Factors Influencing COMEDK UGET Rank

  • Equal Subject Weightage: All three subjects — Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics — carry equal weightage of 60 marks each (total 180). Unlike JEE, no subject has extra weight.
  • Negative Marking: COMEDK UGET follows -0.25 marks for every wrong answer. Accuracy is therefore critical to optimize your net score and rank.
  • All-India Competition (No Domicile Requirement): Unlike state board exams, COMEDK admits students from all states. Roughly 1.7–2 lakh students appear annually, competing for ~30,000+ seats.
  • Tie-Breaking Rules: In case of equal total score, tiebreaker order is: 1) Higher marks in Mathematics, 2) Higher marks in Physics, 3) Higher marks in Chemistry, 4) Older age (date of birth).

COMEDK UGET Exam Pattern & Admission Rules

Summary of COMEDK UGET exam structure, marking scheme, eligibility requirements, and key counselling rules for Karnataka private engineering college admissions.

Exam Pattern & Eligibility

  • Examination Structure: 180 questions — Physics (60) + Chemistry (60) + Mathematics (60). Each correct answer = 1 mark; wrong answer = -0.25 marks. Total = 180 marks.
  • Duration & Mode: 3-hour online Computer Based Test (CBT). No sectional time limit — candidates can freely navigate between sections.
  • Eligibility: 10+2 or equivalent with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics as compulsory subjects. Minimum 45% aggregate (40% for SC/ST/OBC Karnataka candidates in participating colleges).

Counselling & Seat Allotment

  • All-India Admissions: No domicile restriction. Students from all states across India can apply for COMEDK UGET and seek admission to Karnataka private engineering colleges.
  • No Caste Reservations: COMEDK is a purely merit-based exam with no caste-based seat reservations. 5% horizontal PwD reservation applies across participating colleges.
  • 190+ Colleges: Over 190 private engineering and architecture colleges across Karnataka participate in COMEDK counselling, offering 30,000+ undergraduate engineering seats.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the COMEDK Rank Predictor and how does it work?
The COMEDK Rank Predictor is a free tool that estimates your COMEDK UGET rank from your expected marks out of 180. It uses verified score-vs-rank data across ~1.7–2 lakh annual examinees to compute accurate rank estimates for Karnataka private engineering college admissions.
What marks are required for a rank under 1,000 in COMEDK?
To secure a rank under 1,000 in COMEDK UGET, a candidate typically needs a total score of approximately 135–140+ out of 180. Scores above 150+ out of 180 generally yield a rank under 300, making candidates eligible for top CSE branches at RV College of Engineering and BMS College of Engineering.
Are there category reservations in COMEDK UGET?
No, COMEDK UGET is a purely merit-based examination with no caste-based reservations. All candidates — from General, OBC, SC, ST, or any other category — compete on an equal footing based solely on their marks. The only special provision is a 5% horizontal reservation for Persons with Disability (PwD) candidates in participating colleges.
Can candidates from outside Karnataka appear for COMEDK?
Yes, COMEDK UGET is an All-India examination. Candidates from any state in India can appear for COMEDK UGET and seek admission to any of the 190+ private engineering colleges in Karnataka. There is no domicile or state residency requirement for COMEDK admissions.
How is the COMEDK tiebreaker resolved?
If two or more candidates obtain identical total marks in COMEDK UGET, tiebreaking rules are applied in the following order: 1) Higher marks in Mathematics, 2) Higher marks in Physics, 3) Higher marks in Chemistry, 4) Older candidate by date of birth gets the better rank.
