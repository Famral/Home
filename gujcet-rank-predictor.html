ACPC Gujarat Composite Merit Rank Formula Explained

In Gujarat, admissions to B.Tech, B.E., and B.Pharm courses via ACPC are determined by combining your Class 12th Board Theory examination performance and your GUJCET examination percentile:

ACPC Merit Calculation Formula

ACPC evaluates the Composite Merit Marks on a 100-point scale:

Merit Score = (0.50 × Class 12 Board PCM Percentile) + (0.50 × GUJCET Percentile)
  • Class 12th Board Weightage (50%): Calculated only on theory marks of Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (or Biology for Group B).
  • GUJCET Weightage (50%): Calculated on your normalized percentile in the state entrance exam.
  • Common Merit List (CML): Candidates across GSEB, CBSE, and ICSE boards are ranked in a single synchronized merit list.

GUJCET Exam Pattern & Subject Marking Scheme

The Gujarat Common Entrance Test (GUJCET) tests candidates on Gujarat Board Class 12 Science syllabus:

Physics & Chemistry Combined

  • Total Questions: 80 Questions (40 Physics + 40 Chemistry)
  • Total Marks: 80 Marks
  • Exam Duration: 120 Minutes (Combined Paper)
  • Marking Scheme: +1 mark for correct, -0.25 mark for incorrect

Mathematics (Group A)

  • Total Questions: 40 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)
  • Total Marks: 40 Marks
  • Exam Duration: 60 Minutes (Separate Session)
  • Syllabus: Class 12th GSEB Mathematics Textbook

Biology (Group B)

  • Total Questions: 40 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)
  • Total Marks: 40 Marks
  • Exam Duration: 60 Minutes (Separate Session)
  • Target Courses: B.Pharm, D.Pharm & Allied Health Sciences

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How is the GUJCET Rank Predictor helpful for ACPC Gujarat Admissions?
The GUJCET Rank Predictor calculates your estimated ACPC Gujarat Composite Merit Rank and GUJCET percentile by simulating official ACPC admission formulas (combining your GUJCET score out of 120 with your Class 12th Board Theory PCM marks). It gives you an accurate forecast of your All Gujarat General Merit Rank and Category Rank before official results are announced.
What is the formula to calculate ACPC Gujarat Merit Marks?
According to ACPC guidelines, the composite merit score is calculated as:
Merit Marks (out of 100) = (0.50 × 12th Board PCM Theory Percentile) + (0.50 × GUJCET Percentile).
This ensures an equitable balance between board conceptual understanding and entrance exam competitive aptitude.
What is considered a good score in GUJCET 2026?
Scoring 100+ marks out of 120 in GUJCET (approx. 98.5+ percentile) combined with 85%+ in Class 12th Board Theory is considered an excellent score, placing you in the top 1,000 ACPC merit ranks in Gujarat.
What is the TFWS (Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme) quota in ACPC?
TFWS offers 5% supernumerary seats in every approved engineering program where the tuition fee is 100% waived off for students whose parent annual income is less than Rs. 8 Lakhs. TFWS cutoffs are usually higher and more competitive than general open category cutoffs.
How is normalization done for CBSE and ICSE students in ACPC?
ACPC converts raw board marks of CBSE, ICSE, and other board students into percentile marks corresponding to the GSEB board distribution to ensure all students are evaluated equitably on a uniform common merit list.
