MHT CET Percentile Formula & CAP Round College Cutoffs Explained

Maharashtra State CET Cell uses the Percentile Score method to calculate the MHT CET merit list, ensuring fairness across multiple exam days and different paper sets:

Official MHT CET Percentile Formula

The MHT CET Percentile Score is calculated separately for each group (PCM / PCB) using:

Percentile = (100 × N≤) / N_total
  • N≤: Number of candidates who scored equal to or less than you in that specific group (PCM or PCB).
  • N_total: Total number of candidates who appeared in that specific group across all exam days.
  • Multi-Day Normalization: Since MHT CET is held across multiple days, raw marks are scaled to a percentile to ensure fairness across different paper difficulty levels.
  • Tie-Breaking: Maths Percentile → Physics Percentile → Chemistry Percentile → Age (older candidate ranks higher).

MHT CET 2026 Exam Pattern & Marking Scheme

MHT CET 2026 is conducted as a Computer-Based Test (CBT) in two separate papers for PCM and PCB groups. There is NO negative marking, making it ideal to attempt all questions:

PCM Group (Engineering)

  • Paper 1 (Maths): 50 Questions — 100 Marks (2 marks each)
  • Paper 2 (Physics + Chemistry): 100 Questions — 100 Marks (1 mark each)
  • Total: 200 Marks across two separate papers
  • Duration: 90 Minutes per paper (3 Hours total)

Marking Scheme & No Negative Marking

  • Maths: +2 marks per correct answer, 0 for incorrect/unattempted
  • Physics & Chemistry: +1 mark per correct answer, 0 for incorrect/unattempted
  • No Negative Marking: Safe to attempt all questions without penalty
  • Question Type: MCQs only (single correct answer)

Subjects & Syllabus Coverage

  • Physics: Class 11 & 12 Maharashtra Board Syllabus
  • Chemistry: Class 11 & 12 Maharashtra Board Syllabus
  • Mathematics: Class 11 & 12 Maharashtra Board (Algebra, Calculus, etc.)
  • Score Validity: Valid for the current academic year CAP only

CAP Eligibility & Admission

  • State Quota (85%): Maharashtra domicile candidates with MHT CET score
  • All India Quota (15%): OMS candidates via CAP (no domicile requirement)
  • CAP Rounds: Multiple rounds (typically 3–4 CAP rounds + institute level)
  • Tie-Breaking: Maths → Physics → Chemistry Percentile → Age

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How does the MHT CET Percentile Predictor calculate my Percentile & State Merit Rank?
The MHT CET Percentile Predictor applies the Maharashtra State percentile normalization method: Percentile = (100 × Candidates scoring ≤ you) / Total candidates in that group (PCM or PCB). The estimated State Merit Rank is derived from historical percentile-to-rank conversion data calibrated with actual MHT CET results across 2022–2025, accounting for a total candidate pool of approximately 3.5–4 lakh PCM group students.
What is the difference between MHT CET Marks, Percentile Score, and State Merit Rank?
MHT CET Marks are the raw marks obtained out of 200 (Paper 1 + Paper 2). The Percentile Score (0–100) reflects the percentage of candidates in your group who scored equal to or less than you — it is a relative score, not an absolute one. The State Merit Rank (State CML) is your overall rank in Maharashtra used for CAP counselling seat allotment.
What MHT CET percentile is needed for COEP Pune or VJTI Mumbai CSE?
For COEP Pune CSE (General category), candidates typically need a percentile of 99.90+ (State Rank within 100–300). For VJTI Mumbai CSE, the cutoff is around 99.85+ (State Rank ~200–500) for General. OBC candidates can secure these branches with approximately 99.0–99.5 percentile. For mid-tier autonomous colleges like Walchand or PICT, a percentile of 98.5–99.5 (General) is typically required for CSE/IT.
How does MHT CET CAP counselling work in 2026?
CAP (Centralized Admission Process) is managed by the State Common Entrance Test Cell, Maharashtra. After MHT CET results, eligible candidates register for CAP, fill college and branch preferences, and receive provisional allotments across multiple rounds (typically CAP Round 1, 2, 3). After CAP rounds, vacant seats are filled via Institute Level processes. Maharashtra domicile candidates compete for 85% State Quota seats while OMS candidates compete for 15% All India Quota seats.
Is there negative marking in MHT CET 2026?
No, there is NO negative marking in MHT CET 2026. Each correct answer in Maths carries +2 marks, and each correct answer in Physics and Chemistry carries +1 mark. Incorrect or unattempted questions carry 0 marks. This makes it advantageous to attempt all 150 questions (50 Maths + 50 Physics + 50 Chemistry) without any risk of losing marks.
Can I use MHT CET score for colleges outside Maharashtra?
MHT CET score is primarily used for Maharashtra state engineering, pharmacy, and agriculture admissions via CAP. Some private universities and institutes outside Maharashtra may accept MHT CET scores, but the main usage is within Maharashtra. For colleges across India, you would need JEE Main or other relevant national/state entrance exams. MHT CET score is valid only for the current academic year.
