TGICET 2026 Exam Pattern & Marking Scheme

TGICET (Telangana State Integrated Common Entrance Test) is a state-level entrance test conducted by Osmania University on behalf of TSCHE for admission to MBA and MCA programs.

Sectional Weightage & Question Types

  • Analytical Ability (Section A): 75 Questions (75 Marks) - Data Sufficiency & Problem Solving.
  • Mathematical Ability (Section B): 75 Questions (75 Marks) - Arithmetic, Algebra, Geometry & Statistics.
  • Communication Ability (Section C): 50 Questions (50 Marks) - English, Comprehension, Vocabulary & Business Correspondence.
  • Total Paper Weightage: 200 Questions for 200 Marks with duration of 2.5 Hours (150 mins). No Negative Marking.

Key Rules & Test Features

  • Mode: Computer Based Test (CBT) conducted at designated examination centres across Telangana.
  • No Negative Marking: All 200 questions carry 1 mark each with no penalty for wrong answers.
  • Separate Ranks: Separate merit lists are prepared for MBA and MCA. Candidates can appear for both.
  • Score Validity: TGICET score is valid for 1 academic year for TSCHE web options counselling only.

TGICET TSCHE Web Options Counselling Process & Eligibility

TGICET admissions are conducted through the online Web Options (TSCHE) counselling portal managed by the Telangana State Council of Higher Education.

Step-by-Step TSCHE Counselling Process

  • Step 1: Registration & Certificate Verification: Submit qualifying degree details, upload documents, and pay counselling fee on the TSCHE web options portal.
  • Step 2: Web Options Exercise: Fill college & program preferences in order of priority on the TSCHE portal. Seat allotment is based on rank, category, and web option order.
  • Step 3: Seat Allotment (Phase-wise): Allotment results declared across multiple phases. Accept or upgrade your allotted seat within the prescribed time.
  • Step 4: Reporting & Enrollment: Report to the allotted college with original documents and pay the required fee for final admission confirmation.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is the TGICET College Predictor and how does it work?
The TGICET College Predictor is a free tool that estimates your MBA and MCA admission probability into universities and colleges across Telangana. Enter your TS ICET State Rank, category, and course preference, and the tool evaluates your chances against official TSCHE historical final phase closing ranks.
Which reservation categories are supported?
TGICET supports multiple reservation categories: OC (Open Category/General), EWS (Economically Weaker Section), BC-A, BC-B, BC-C, BC-D, BC-E (Backward Classes Groups A through E), SC (Scheduled Caste), and ST (Scheduled Tribe). Each category has dedicated seats with separate cutoff ranks per college.
What TGICET rank is required for Osmania University MBA?
Based on official TSCHE web options final phase cutoffs for OC category candidates, MBA at Osmania University PG College of Business Management (PGCBM) typically closes around State Rank 200-400. Kakatiya University MBA closes around rank 400-800. MCA programs usually have higher closing ranks (lower competition) than MBA.
How does the TSCHE Web Options Counselling process work?
Qualified candidates submit a registration form on the TSCHE web options portal, verify their certificates, and fill college & program preferences. Seat allocation is carried out over multiple phases based on merit rank, category quota, seat availability, and preference order. Students can accept or upgrade their allotted seat in subsequent phases.
Are category reservations (BC/SC/ST/EWS) applied in TGICET closing ranks?
Yes, Telangana state government reservation rules are applied in TGICET admissions. Reservations include BC-A (7%), BC-B (10%), BC-C (1%), BC-D (7%), BC-E (4%), SC (15%), ST (6%), and EWS (10%). Our predictor calculates effective cutoff thresholds tailored to your selected reservation category.
