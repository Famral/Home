Understanding ACPC Merit Rank & GUJCET Score

Gujarat engineering admissions via ACPC are not based on raw GUJCET marks alone. The official ACPC merit rank is computed using:• 60% Weightage: Class 12 PCM (Physics, Chemistry & Maths) Theory Percentile Rank.• 40% Weightage: GUJCET Percentile Rank.

ACPC Counselling Rounds & TFWS Scheme

  • 3 Counselling Rounds: ACPC conducts Round 1, Round 2, and a Mop-Up Round. Closing ranks in Round 1 are generally tighter; subsequent rounds may have relaxed cutoffs for unfilled seats.
  • TFWS (Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme): Students with family income below Rs.6 Lakhs/year are eligible for 100% tuition fee waiver on dedicated TFWS seats.
  • Home University Quota: 85% of seats are reserved for Gujarat Board / Home University students. 15% All India quota seats are available for students from other boards/universities.
  • Official ACPC Portal: Register and participate in counselling at gujacpc.admissions.nic.in. Always verify your closing ranks from official ACPC PDF archives for each round.

ACPC Gujarat Admission Process & Fee Structure

Understanding ACPC reservation categories, fee structure for government vs. private colleges, and the online centralized counselling mechanics.

ACPC Category & Fee Structure

  • Government GECs: Rs.30,000-Rs.40,000/year tuition fee. Very affordable; highest competition at top GECs.
  • Grant-in-Aid Institutes (BVM): Rs.40,000-Rs.70,000/year. Semi-govt fee structure with higher autonomy in academics.
  • Private Engineering Colleges: Rs.1.2 Lakhs-Rs.3 Lakhs/year depending on college reputation and ACPC-approved fee.
  • TFWS Scheme: 100% tuition fee waiver for family income below Rs.6 Lakhs/year on designated TFWS seats in all colleges.

Reservation & ACPC Counselling Rounds

  • SEBC (OBC) Quota: 27% reservation. Generally ~30-50% relaxed closing ranks vs. General.
  • SC/ST Quota: SC: 7% reservation; ST: 15% reservation. Most relaxed closing ranks across all colleges.
  • EWS Quota: 10% reservation for Economically Weaker Sections. Closing ranks between General and SEBC category.
  • 3 ACPC Rounds: Round 1, Round 2 (seat upgrades), and Mop-Up / Spot Round for remaining vacant seats.
  • Online Choice Filling: Register at gujacpc.admissions.nic.in and lock choices in priority order before round deadline.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the GUJCET College Predictor and how does it work?
The GUJCET College Predictor is a free online tool that estimates your B.Tech admission chances in Gujarat engineering colleges via ACPC counselling based on official ACPC 2024 closing ranks. Enter your ACPC Merit Rank, select your category (General, SEBC, SC, ST, EWS, TFWS), and choose your preferred branch or college type to get predictions ranked by admission probability.
What is the ACPC Merit Rank formula for GUJCET admissions?
The ACPC composite merit rank is computed using: 60% weightage of Class 12 PCM (Physics, Chemistry & Maths) Theory Percentile + 40% weightage of GUJCET Percentile. This rank - not the raw GUJCET marks - is used for all ACPC seat allotments. Always use your official ACPC merit rank from gujacpc.admissions.nic.in for accurate predictions.
What rank is needed for Computer Engineering at LDCE Ahmedabad?
Based on 2024 ACPC Round 1 official data, the General category closing rank for Computer Engineering at L.D. College of Engineering (LDCE), Ahmedabad was approximately 530. LDCE is the most competitive government engineering college in Gujarat. For SEBC category, the closing rank is typically around 1,200-1,500 for Computer Engineering.
What is the TFWS quota in ACPC Gujarat admissions?
TFWS (Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme) provides 100% tuition fee waiver for eligible students with family income below Rs.6 Lakhs/year on designated TFWS seats. The ACPC merit rank closing for TFWS seats is generally more relaxed than General category. Students must provide a valid income certificate to avail TFWS benefits.
What is the difference between Government GEC and Grant-in-Aid colleges in Gujarat?
Government Engineering Colleges (GEC) are fully funded and managed by the Gujarat state government. They charge the lowest fees (Rs.30,000-Rs.40,000/year) and have the most competitive closing ranks. Grant-in-Aid colleges like BVM (Anand) receive partial government funding and charge slightly higher fees (Rs.40,000-Rs.70,000/year) but operate with more academic autonomy. Private colleges have higher fees (Rs.1.2-Rs.3 Lakhs/year) and generally have more relaxed closing ranks.
How many ACPC counselling rounds are conducted for Gujarat engineering admissions?
ACPC conducts 3 rounds of centralized online counselling: Round 1 (initial seat allotment), Round 2 (seat upgrades & fresh allotments for unoccupied seats), and a Mop-Up / Spot Round for remaining vacant seats. Closing ranks typically get more relaxed from Round 1 to subsequent rounds, but popular branches at top colleges like LDCE, VGEC, and BVM rarely have seats remaining in Round 2.
