CMAT 2026 Exam Structure & Pattern Overview

The Common Management Admission Test (CMAT) is a national-level computer-based entrance examination conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA).

Sectional Weightage & Marking

  • Quantitative Techniques & Data Interpretation: 20 Questions (80 Marks)
  • Logical Reasoning: 20 Questions (80 Marks)
  • Language Comprehension: 20 Questions (80 Marks)
  • General Awareness: 20 Questions (80 Marks)
  • Innovation & Entrepreneurship: 20 Questions (80 Marks)

Key Test Rules & Timing

  • Total Duration: 180 Minutes (3 Hours) without sectional time limits.
  • Total Questions: 100 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) for 400 Total Marks.
  • Marking Scheme: +4 marks for each correct answer; -1 mark for each wrong attempt.
  • Validity: CMAT scorecard is valid for 1 academic year across AICTE institutions.

Maharashtra DTE MAH-CET CAP Counselling Guide (For CMAT Candidates)

For non-Maharashtra (All India) candidates, valid CMAT scores are accepted for 15% All India Quota seats in premier government & university departments like JBIMS, SIMSREE, PUMBA, MET, and Chetana.

Step-by-Step CAP Counselling Flow

  • Step 1: Online Portal Registration: Register on official DTE portal (cap.mahacet.org) under 'All India Candidate' using CMAT details.
  • Step 2: Document Verification (FC): Get documents verified through E-Scrutiny or Physical Facilitation Center.
  • Step 3: Option Form Choice Locking: Enter your preferred college list in strategic order before CAP Round 1 & Round 2.
  • Step 4: Seat Allotment & Freeze/Float: Accept allotted seat, pay seat acceptance fee, and report to institute.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is the CMAT College Predictor and how does it work?
The CMAT College Predictor is a free tool that estimates your MBA and PGDM admission chances in top AICTE-approved management institutions across India. Enter your CMAT percentile or raw score (out of 400), select your category and preferred specialization, and the tool compares your score against previous years' official closing percentiles to show High, Medium, or Low admission probabilities.
What score is required for JBIMS and SIMSREE through CMAT?
Jamnalal Bajaj Institute of Management Studies (JBIMS) and SIMSREE Mumbai accept CMAT scores under the Maharashtra DTE MAH-CET CAP All India (AI) Quota. Cutoffs are extremely competitive, requiring ~99.93+ percentile for JBIMS MMS and ~99.85+ percentile for SIMSREE MMS.
Can non-Maharashtra students apply to JBIMS and SIMSREE via CMAT?
Yes! All India candidates from outside Maharashtra can register on the DTE MAH-CET CAP portal (cap.mahacet.org) and apply for the 15% All India quota MMS seats using their valid CMAT scorecards.
Which top PGDM colleges accept CMAT for shortlisting?
Premier autonomous PGDM colleges accepting CMAT include Goa Institute of Management (GIM Goa), TAPMI Manipal, K J Somaiya Institute of Management, Great Lakes (Chennai/Gurgaon), NIBM Pune, Welingkar (WeSchool), BIMTECH Greater Noida, and NL Dalmia Mumbai.
How does raw score convert to percentile in CMAT?
CMAT raw marks out of 400 are converted to percentile based on normalization across exam slots. Generally, 340+ marks corresponds to ~99.9+ percentile, 310+ marks to ~99.0 percentile, 280+ marks to ~97.0 percentile, 240+ marks to ~92.0 percentile, and 200+ marks to ~85.0 percentile.
