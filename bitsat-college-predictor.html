BITSAT 2026 Exam Pattern & Marking Scheme

BITSAT is a national-level Computer-Based Test (CBT) conducted by BITS Pilani for admission to its Integrated First Degree programs in Engineering, Sciences, and Pharmacy.

Sectional Weightage & Marks

  • Part I - Physics: 30 Questions (90 Marks)
  • Part II - Chemistry: 30 Questions (90 Marks)
  • Part III - English Proficiency (10 Qs) & Logical Reasoning (20 Qs): 30 Questions (90 Marks)
  • Part IV - Mathematics / Biology (B.Pharm): 40 Questions (120 Marks)
  • Bonus Questions Feature: 12 extra questions (36 marks) available if all 130 questions are attempted!

Key Rules & Test Features

  • Total Duration: 180 Minutes (3 Hours) without sectional time limits.
  • Total Questions: 130 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) for 390 Total Marks.
  • Marking Scheme: +3 marks for each correct answer; -1 mark for each wrong attempt.
  • Instant Score Display: Candidates see their raw score immediately upon submitting the CBT exam.
  • 2 Exam Sessions: Session 1 (May) & Session 2 (June). Best score out of the two is considered!

BITSAT Admission Iteration Rounds & M.Sc. Dual Degree Guide

BITS Pilani conducts admission through a transparent multi-round iteration process online at bitsadmission.com.

Step-by-Step BITSAT Counselling Process

  • Step 1: Preference Form Submission: Submit campus (Pilani, Goa, Hyd) and branch choices on bitsadmission.com along with 12th Board marks.
  • Step 2: Iteration Allotments (Iter 1 to Iter 6): Seat allotment results declared across 6 rounds. Cutoffs drop in subsequent iterations.
  • Step 3: Freeze / Slide / Waitlist Option: Pay admission fee to hold seat or remain in waitlist for upgrade to higher choice.
  • Step 4: M.Sc. Dual Degree B.E. Allocation: Dual degree students get a B.E. branch allocated after 1st year based on 1st-year CGPA.

Key BITS Advantages & Features

What sets BITS Pilani apart from traditional engineering institutes:

  • Zero Attendance Policy: No mandatory 75% attendance rule; complete academic freedom for self-driven learning.
  • Practice School (PS-I & PS-II): 6-month corporate internships with top tech, finance, and core MNCs in the 4th/5th year.
  • Merit & Need Scholarships: Up to 80%-100% tuition fee waiver for top BITSAT scorers and high CGPA students.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is the BITSAT College Predictor and how does it work?
The BITSAT College Predictor is a free tool that calculates your admission probability into B.E., M.Sc. Dual Degree, and B.Pharm programs across BITS Pilani, BITS Goa, and BITS Hyderabad. Enter your total BITSAT score out of 390 marks, select your preferred campus and branch filters, and the tool evaluates your choices against official BITS historical final iteration cutoff scores.
What score is required for B.E. Computer Science (CS) in BITS Pilani, Goa, and Hyderabad?
Based on official 2024 final BITSAT iteration cutoffs (out of 390 marks): BITS Pilani B.E. CS required 327 marks, BITS Goa B.E. CS required 301 marks, and BITS Hyderabad B.E. CS required 298 marks.
How does the 5-Year M.Sc. Dual Degree program work at BITS?
Students admitted to an M.Sc. Dual Degree (Economics, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biological Sciences) complete 1st-year common courses alongside B.E. students. At the end of the 1st year, a 4-year B.E. major branch (such as CS, ECE, EEE, Mechanical, etc.) is allocated based purely on 1st-year CGPA. Over 25-30% of dual degree students obtain B.E. Computer Science as their second degree!
Is there any caste reservation (OBC / SC / ST / EWS) in BITSAT admissions?
No. Admissions to BITS Pilani, BITS Goa, and BITS Hyderabad are conducted 100% on merit based strictly on your BITSAT score. There are no reservation quotas for OBC, SC, ST, EWS, or state domiciles.
What is the Board Topper Direct Admission scheme at BITS Pilani?
The 1st rank holder (Topper) in 12th Board examinations of any Central or State Board in India in the PCM stream is guaranteed Direct Admission into the program of their choice at BITS Pilani, irrespective of their BITSAT score.
