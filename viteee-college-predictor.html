How to Use the VITEEE College Predictor for B.Tech Admissions

Securing your desired B.Tech branch at VIT Vellore or Chennai requires clear insights into VITEEE rank cutoffs and the 5 fee category slabs. The VITEEE College Predictor matches your rank against multi-year cutoff trends to help you prepare an optimal choice preference list.

Understanding Category 1 to Category 5 Fee Slabs

VIT conducts seat allocation under 5 tuition fee slabs based on VITEEE rank merit: • Category 1 (Lowest Fee): Reserved for top VITEEE rankers (~₹1.98 Lakhs/year for Group B). • Category 2 to 5 (Higher Slabs): Available for candidates with higher ranks at progressively higher tuition fee rates up to ~₹4.93 Lakhs/year.

Centralized Placements & VIT STARS Scholarship

  • Centralized Campus Placement: VIT conducts single-window placement drives for Vellore, Chennai, AP, and Bhopal students. Over 900+ top recruiters (Amazon, Microsoft, Google, TCS, Deloitte, Intel) visit annually.
  • VIT STARS Scheme: Support the Advancement of Rural Students provides 100% tuition & hostel fee waiver for district toppers from rural government schools in TN & AP.
  • 5 Online Counselling Phases: Phase 1 (Ranks 1-20,000), Phase 2 (20,001-45,000), Phase 3 (45,001-70,000), Phase 4 (70,001-1,000,000), and Phase 5 (1,000,001-1,500,000).

Steps to Build Your VIT Choice List

  • Input Your VITEEE Rank: Enter your official VITEEE overall rank.
  • Select Fee Category & Campus: Choose specific fee categories (Category 1 to 5) and preferred VIT campuses (Vellore, Chennai, AP, Bhopal).
  • Compare & Refine: Filter predictions by admission probability. Select up to 3 options to compare side-by-side on placements, fees, and campus size.
  • Export Preference List: Add preferred options to your Preference List, reorder them, and export to guide your official VIT online choice filling.

VIT Fee Structure & Category Slabs Overview

Understanding Category 1 to Category 5 tuition fee slabs, hostel fee options, and centralized counselling mechanics.

Category 1-5 Tuition Fee Structure

VIT programs are categorized into Group A and Group B fee structures:

  • Category 1 Fee: ₹1,98,000/yr (Group B - CSE, ECE, IT, AI) | ₹1,73,000/yr (Group A - Mech, Civil, Biotech).
  • Category 2 Fee: ₹3,07,000/yr (Group B) | ₹2,82,000/yr (Group A).
  • Category 3 Fee: ₹4,05,000/yr (Group B) | ₹3,79,000/yr (Group A).
  • Category 4 Fee: ₹4,48,000/yr (Group B) | ₹4,22,000/yr (Group A).
  • Category 5 Fee: ₹4,93,000/yr (Group B) | ₹4,65,000/yr (Group A).

Hostel Fees & Counselling Phases

Hostel options & online counselling mechanics:

  • Hostel & Mess Charges: Range from ₹1.05 Lakhs to ₹2.10 Lakhs/year depending on AC/Non-AC and 1/2/3/4/6 bedded room preferences.
  • 5 Online Phases: Choice filling is opened phase-by-phase based on rank bands (Phase 1 to Phase 5).
  • Seat Refund Policy: Strictly as per UGC guidelines with step-wise refund percentages prior to commencement of classes.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the VITEEE B.Tech College Predictor and how does it work?
The VITEEE B.Tech College Predictor is a free online tool that estimates your admission chances across all 4 VIT campuses (Vellore, Chennai, AP, Bhopal) and Category 1 to 5 fee slabs based on official closing rank trends. Enter your VITEEE rank and filter by fee slab or campus to view High, Medium, or Low probabilities.
What is the difference between Category 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5 in VIT?
VIT offers the same curriculum, faculty, and placement opportunities regardless of the category slab. The category slab only determines your annual tuition fee. Category 1 has the lowest fee (~₹1.98L/yr) and gets filled by top rankers, whereas Category 2 to 5 have higher fee structures (~₹3.07L to ₹4.93L/yr) allowing higher rankers to secure seats.
What rank is needed for CSE Core at VIT Vellore in Category 1?
Historically, CSE Core at VIT Vellore in Category 1 closes around rank 7,500. Category 2 closes around rank 14,000, Category 3 around rank 19,000, Category 4 around rank 23,000, and Category 5 around rank 27,000.
Are placements combined for all VIT campuses?
Yes, VIT conducts centralized campus placements for students of VIT Vellore, VIT Chennai, VIT AP, and VIT Bhopal. Top tech recruiters visit the placement drives where eligible students across all campuses participate equally.
Who is eligible for the VIT STARS Scholarship?
The VIT STARS (Support the Advancement of Rural Students) scholarship is awarded to top rankers from rural government high schools in Tamil Nadu and Andhra Pradesh, providing a 100% fee waiver along with free accommodation and mess facilities.
