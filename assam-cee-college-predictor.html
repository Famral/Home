Understanding DTE Assam Counselling Process & Choice Filling

Engineering admissions in Assam state government colleges are conducted by the Directorate of Technical Education (DTE), Assam. Seat allotment is based on the Assam CEE Merit Rank scored in the Assam Combined Entrance Examination (CEE). During online counselling, candidates submit their choice preferences of institute and branch.

Assam Engineering Colleges Fee Structure

  • Government Engineering Colleges (AEC Guwahati, JEC Jorhat): Highly subsidized state government fee structure around Rs 12,000 to Rs 18,000 per year.
  • New State Colleges (JIST, BBEC, DEC, BVEC, GEC): Subsidized tuition fees ranging around Rs 15,000 to Rs 22,000 per year.
  • Post-Matric Scholarship Support: Full post-matric scholarship & fee reimbursement available for SC/ST/OBC/MOBC candidates as per Assam government guidelines.

Assam CEE B.Tech Admissions & Quota Distribution

Understanding Assam's statutory category quotas and merit dynamics for state government engineering admission.

Assam Reservation Rules

DTE Assam implements reservation for key categories:

  • OBC / MOBC Reservation (15%): Includes reserved sub-quotas for Koch Rajbongshi, Tai Ahom, Chutia, Moran, Matak, and Tea Garden Labour communities.
  • SC (7%), STP (10%) & STH (5%): Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribe Plains, and Scheduled Tribe Hills receive designated seat quotas.
  • EWS Reservation (10%): 10% seats reserved for Economically Weaker Section candidates in state government colleges.

Counselling Factors

Closing ranks vary across counselling rounds based on:

  • Assam CEE Merit Score: Scores out of 480 (Physics 120, Chemistry 120, Mathematics 240).
  • Choice Filling Priorities: Allotment is processed strictly in order of filled preferences.
  • Multi-Round Upgradation: Round 1, Round 2, Round 3, and Spot counselling rounds enable rank relaxations.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Assam CEE College Predictor and how does it work?
The Assam CEE College Predictor is a free tool that estimates your B.Tech admission chances in top government engineering colleges in Assam (AEC Guwahati, JEC Jorhat, JIST Jorhat, BBEC Kokrajhar, DEC Dhemaji, BVEC Karimganj, GEC Golaghat). Enter your CEE rank, select your reservation category, and the tool compares your rank against official DTE Assam closing ranks to show High, Medium, or Low admission probabilities.
How does DTE Assam choice filling work?
DTE Assam option entry allows candidates to select college-branch combinations in order of preference. Seat allotment occurs strictly in rank order. Once a choice is allotted, lower preference choices are discarded, making preference ordering critical.
Is the predicted seat guaranteed?
No. Actual seat allotments are determined by DTE Assam based on the candidate's rank, choice list, category seat availability, and actual closing cutoffs in the current year. This tool provides indicative assessments based on live API trends.
What reservation categories are recognized under Assam CEE?
Assam CEE recognizes GENERAL, OBC/MOBC, EWS, SC, STP (Plains), STH (Hills), and special community sub-quotas for Koch Rajbongshi, Tai Ahom, Chutia, Moran, Matak, TGLC (Tea Garden Labour Community), CGE, FF, and PH categories.
Which are the premier government engineering colleges in Assam?
Assam Engineering College (AEC), Guwahati (est. 1955) and Jorhat Engineering College (JEC), Jorhat (est. 1960) are the oldest and most prestigious state government engineering colleges in Assam, followed by JIST Jorhat, BBEC Kokrajhar, DEC Dhemaji, BVEC Karimganj, and GEC Golaghat.
