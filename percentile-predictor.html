Frequently Asked Questions

A Percentile Predictor is a free tool that estimates your likely percentile for competitive exams like JEE Main, MHT CET, CAT, and NMAT based on your raw score or marks. It compares your score against historical score-percentile distribution data from previous years to give you an indicative percentile range that helps with planning and college shortlisting.

Famral offers free percentile predictors for JEE Main (National Engineering), MHT CET (Maharashtra Engineering/Pharmacy), CAT (National MBA/Management), and NMAT by GMAC (NMIMS & Top Business Schools). Each predictor uses exam-specific historical score-percentile data for accurate estimates.

No. Percentile indicates the percentage of candidates who scored equal to or below your score. For example, a 99 percentile in CAT, NMAT, or JEE Main means you scored higher than 99% of all test-takers. Percentage is simply your raw marks divided by total marks multiplied by 100.

No. Percentile Predictors provide indicative estimates based on historical score-to-percentile data from previous exam sessions. Actual percentiles depend on the current year's difficulty level, slot-wise normalization, and the total number of candidates. Always wait for the official result before making any final decisions.

Our predictors are built using slot-wise historical cutoff and normalization trends from previous exam years to provide a close estimate. They serve as a reliable reference tool for planning choices and understanding your admission chances.
