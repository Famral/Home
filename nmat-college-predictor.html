NMAT by GMAC 2026 Exam Pattern & Marking Scheme

NMAT by GMAC (formerly NMAT by NMIMS) is a national-level MBA entrance test administered by the Graduate Management Admission Council (GMAC) for admission to NMIMS and 50+ leading B-schools across India.

Sectional Weightage & Question Types

  • Language Skills: 36 Questions (96 marks) - Reading Comprehension, Para Jumbles, Sentence Correction & Vocabulary. Time: 28 minutes.
  • Quantitative Skills: 36 Questions (144 marks) - Data Interpretation, Arithmetic, Algebra, Geometry & Data Sufficiency. Time: 52 minutes.
  • Logical Reasoning: 36 Questions (120 marks) - Analytical Reasoning, Deductive Logic, Critical Reasoning & Verbal Reasoning. Time: 40 minutes.
  • Total Score: 108 Questions for 360 Marks (scaled). Duration: 120 Minutes total. No Negative Marking.

Key Rules & Test Features

  • 3 Attempts Allowed: Candidates can appear up to 3 times in the testing window (September-December). Best score is considered by most institutions.
  • No Negative Marking: All questions carry positive marks only; incorrect answers receive 0 marks - attempt all questions.
  • Computer-Based Adaptive: NMAT is a CBT conducted at Pearson VUE test centres across India and internationally.
  • Score Validity: NMAT score is valid for the current admission cycle. Scores are shared with all NMAT-accepting institutions you select.

NMAT by GMAC Admission Process & Eligibility

NMAT by GMAC admissions are a multi-stage process involving the entrance test followed by GD/PI rounds at each participating institution.

Step-by-Step NMAT Admission Process

  • Step 1: NMAT Registration & Scheduling: Register on the official nmat.org.in portal. Schedule your exam slot at a Pearson VUE test centre. Up to 3 retakes allowed.
  • Step 2: Appear for NMAT Exam: Take the 120-minute computer-based test. Receive your score immediately after the test session ends.
  • Step 3: Apply to B-Schools: Use your NMAT score to apply to shortlisting institutions. Each college has its own application portal and deadline.
  • Step 4: GD/PI & Final Admission: Shortlisted candidates attend Group Discussion and Personal Interview rounds. Final merit list considers NMAT score, GD/PI, academics, and work experience.

Key Advantages of NMAT MBA Programs

Why NMAT-accepting B-schools are sought after by management aspirants:

  • NMIMS Brand Excellence: NMIMS Mumbai is among India's top 10 B-schools with strong placements in BFSI, consulting, FMCG and technology sectors.
  • 3 Retake Flexibility: NMAT allows up to 3 attempts in a single cycle, giving candidates a significant advantage over single-attempt exams like CAT.
  • Diverse Accepting Institutions: 50+ B-schools accept NMAT scores spanning NMIMS campuses, SPJIMR, XIMB, ISB, Mahindra University and international institutions.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is the NMAT College Predictor and how does it work?
The NMAT College Predictor is a free tool that estimates your MBA admission probability into top B-schools accepting NMAT by GMAC scores. Enter your NMAT total score, reservation category, and program preference, and the tool evaluates your chances against official NMAT cutoff scores from NMIMS, SPJIMR, XIMB, ISB and other participating institutions.
What NMAT score is required for NMIMS Mumbai MBA?
Based on official NMAT by GMAC data, the cutoff score for MBA at NMIMS Mumbai is typically 210-220+ for the General category, with top candidates scoring 230+ securing admission. NMIMS Bangalore and Hyderabad campuses generally accept scores of 200-210+.
How many attempts are allowed in NMAT by GMAC?
Candidates can appear for NMAT up to 3 times during the testing window (September to December). Most institutions consider the best score out of all attempts. There must be a gap of at least 15 days between consecutive attempts.
How does the NMAT admission process work after the exam?
After receiving your NMAT score, you apply separately to each B-school. Shortlisted candidates attend Group Discussion (GD) and/or Case Discussion, followed by Personal Interviews (PI). The final selection is based on a composite score including NMAT score weightage, GD/PI performance, academic record, and work experience.
Are reservation categories applicable in NMAT college admissions?
Yes, central and state government reservation policies are applied at most NMAT-accepting institutions. Reservations include OBC-NCL, SC, ST, and EWS categories as per government norms. Private autonomous institutions like NMIMS may have their own internal diversity policies. Our predictor adjusts effective cutoff thresholds based on your selected reservation category.
