IIT JAM 2026 Exam Pattern & Marking Scheme

IIT JAM is a national-level Computer-Based Test (CBT) conducted annually by one of the IITs for admission to Master of Science (M.Sc.) and doctoral programs.

Sectional Weightage & Question Types

  • Section A - Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs): 30 Questions (50 Marks) with 1/3 and 2/3 negative marking.
  • Section B - Multiple Select Questions (MSQs): 10 Questions (20 Marks) with NO negative marking.
  • Section C - Numerical Answer Type (NATs): 20 Questions (30 Marks) with NO negative marking.
  • Total Paper Weightage: 60 Questions for 100 Marks total duration of 3 Hours (180 mins).

Key Rules & Test Features

  • 7 Official Test Papers: Mathematics (MA), Statistics (MS), Physics (PH), Chemistry (CY), Biotechnology (BT), Geology (GG), Economics (EN).
  • Virtual Calculator: On-screen scientific calculator provided during the CBT examination.
  • Centralized JOAPS Portal: Choice filling & seat allotment managed centrally on jam.iitm.ac.in / JOAPS.
  • Score Validity: IIT JAM score card is valid for 1 academic year for IITs, IISc & CCMN NIT admissions.

IIT JAM JOAPS Admission Counselling Process & Eligibility

IIT M.Sc. admissions are conducted online via the JAM Online Application Processing System (JOAPS).

Step-by-Step JOAPS Counselling Process

  • Step 1: Application for Admission: Submit qualifying degree details and preference list of IIT programs on the JOAPS portal.
  • Step 2: Admission Lists (Rounds 1 to 4): Seat allotment results declared across 4 main rounds. Cutoff ranks expand in subsequent rounds.
  • Step 3: Seat Acceptance & Fee Payment: Choose 'Accept and Freeze', 'Accept with Upgrade', or 'Reject and Quit' options with booking fee.
  • Step 4: Physical Verification & Enrollment: Report to the allotted IIT campus with physical documents for final enrollment.

Key Advantages of Pursuing M.Sc. at IITs

Why IIT M.Sc. programs are highly sought after by science & math graduates:

  • High-Paying Tech & Analytics Jobs: Direct eligibility for campus placements in Data Science, Quant Finance, Software, & R&D.
  • Direct Ph.D. Pathways: Top-performing M.Sc. students get direct Ph.D. conversion or foreign university research admissions.
  • Affordable Fees: Extremely subsidised tuition fee compared to private universities (under Rs 30,000 to Rs 35,000 per year).

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is the IIT JAM College Predictor and how does it work?
The IIT JAM College Predictor is a free tool that calculates your admission probability into M.Sc., Joint M.Sc.-Ph.D., M.Sc.-Ph.D. Dual Degree, and Integrated Ph.D. programs across all 21 IITs and IISc Bangalore. Enter your JAM All India Rank (AIR), category, and test paper, and the tool evaluates your choices against official JOAPS historical final round closing ranks.
Which test papers are available in IIT JAM?
IIT JAM is conducted for 7 test papers: Mathematics (MA), Mathematical Statistics (MS), Physics (PH), Chemistry (CY), Biotechnology (BT), Geology (GG), and Economics (EN).
What JAM rank is required for M.Sc. Applied Statistics & Informatics in IIT Bombay?
Based on official JOAPS final round cutoffs for General category candidates: M.Sc. Applied Statistics & Informatics at IIT Bombay closed around AIR 42, while M.Sc. Mathematics at IIT Bombay closed around AIR 34.
How does the JOAPS Centralized Counselling process work for IIT M.Sc. admissions?
Qualified candidates submit a single admission form on the JOAPS portal with their preferences of IITs and academic programs. Seat allocation is carried out over 4 main rounds based on merit rank, category quota, seat availability, and preference order.
Are category reservations (EWS, OBC-NCL, SC, ST, PwD) applied in IIT JAM closing ranks?
Yes, central government reservation rules are strictly applied in IIT JAM admissions for Gen-EWS (10%), OBC-NCL (27%), SC (15%), ST (7.5%), and PwD (5% horizontal reservation). Our predictor calculates effective cutoff thresholds tailored to your selected category.
