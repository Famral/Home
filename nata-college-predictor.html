How to Use the NATA College Predictor for Admissions

Securing admission in top architecture colleges requires accurate insights into NATA cutoffs and Merit Score calculation. The NATA College Predictor compares your score with multi-year cutoff trends to help you build an optimal preference list.

Understanding NATA Merit Score Calculation & Eligibility

As per Council of Architecture (CoA) guidelines, B.Arch admission merit is calculated using a 50:50 formula in state CAP counselling (such as Maharashtra CAP, KCET Architecture, REAP, TNEA): Merit Score = 50% of (10+2 PCM / Diploma Aggregate %) + 50% of (NATA Score out of 200 scaled to 100) A candidate must secure at least 70 out of 200 marks in NATA 2026 to be qualified for B.Arch seat allocation.

B.Arch Fee Structure & Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme (TFWS)

  • Government Fee Structure: Tuition fee in premier government architecture colleges (Sir JJ, SPA Delhi, MSU Baroda, Jadavpur) ranges from ₹10,000 to ₹60,000 per year.
  • Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme (TFWS): 5% supernumerary seats in state counselling offer 100% tuition fee waiver to candidates with parental income < ₹8.0 Lakhs/year.
  • Private Premier Institutes: Tuition fee in premier private architecture institutions (CEPT, RVCA, BMSCA, Sushant School, MSAP) ranges from ₹1.80L to ₹3.80L per year.

NATA Admissions & Quota Distribution

Understanding category reservation quotas, state domicile seats, and Council of Architecture (CoA) seat allotment dynamics across architecture colleges.

Statutory Reservation & Domicile Structure

B.Arch seat allocation strictly adheres to central and state statutory reservation quotas:

  • OBC-NCL Reservation (27%): 27% seats reserved for Non-Creamy Layer OBC candidates in central & state government architecture institutes.
  • SC (15%) & ST (7.5%): Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe candidates receive reserved seats in merit rank order.
  • EWS Reservation (10%): 10% seats reserved for Economically Weaker Section candidates in government colleges.
  • State Home Domicile Quota (50%-85%): State CAP counselling reserves up to 85% seats for home state domicile candidates (e.g. Maharashtra CAP, KCET, REAP, TNEA).

NATA Counselling Factors

Closing NATA cutoffs across rounds are governed by:

  • Merit Score Calculation: Aggregate score combining 50% 10+2 PCM/Diploma % and 50% NATA Score (out of 100).
  • Preference Choice Hierarchy: Allotment strictly follows candidate choice preference order submitted online.
  • Multiple CAP Rounds: 3-4 central online rounds followed by Spot / Institutional vacancy rounds.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the NATA College Predictor and how does it work?
The NATA College Predictor is a free tool that estimates your B.Arch and B.Plan admission chances across top architecture colleges in India (Sir J.J. College of Architecture Mumbai, CEPT Ahmedabad, SPA New Delhi, CCA Chandigarh, RVCA Bengaluru, BMSCA, MSU Baroda, etc.). Enter your NATA score (out of 200), select your reservation category, and the tool compares your score against official closing cutoffs to show High, Medium, or Low admission probabilities.
How is NATA Merit Score calculated for B.Arch Admissions?
In state CAP counselling (such as Maharashtra CAP, KCET Architecture, REAP, TNEA), admission merit score is calculated with 50% weightage given to your 10+2 PCM / Diploma aggregate percentage and 50% weightage to your NATA score (out of 200, converted to 100).
What is the minimum qualifying score in NATA 2026?
The Council of Architecture (CoA) sets the qualifying cutoff score for NATA at 70 marks out of 200. Candidates must score 70+ in NATA and pass 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (or 10+3 Diploma with Mathematics) to be eligible for B.Arch seat allocation.
Is NATA score accepted in NITs and IITs?
No. NITs, IIEST Shibpur, and SPAs under JoSAA take admission via JEE Main Paper 2 (B.Arch/B.Plan). IITs admit to B.Arch through AAT (Architectural Aptitude Test) after qualifying JEE Advanced. NATA is mandatory for all state government universities, deemed universities, Sir JJ Mumbai, CEPT Ahmedabad, RVCA, BMSCA, CCA Chandigarh, and private architecture institutions across India.
What fee concessions or TFWS schemes are available for B.Arch?
Under the Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme (TFWS) offered in state CAP counselling, 5% supernumerary seats are reserved for meritorious candidates with annual family income below ₹8.0 Lakhs, providing a 100% tuition fee waiver. Additionally, Post-Matric and EBC scholarship subsidies are offered by state governments for eligible categories.
