Frequently Asked Questions

An entrance exam is a standardised test conducted by universities, state boards, or central bodies to assess candidates' aptitude before admission to professional courses like engineering (B.Tech), medicine (MBBS/BDS), management (MBA), law (LLB), and more. Your exam rank or percentile directly determines which colleges and branches you can apply for during counselling.

The most important national-level engineering entrance exams in India are: JEE Main (conducted by NTA for NITs, IIITs, GFTIs and state colleges), JEE Advanced (for IITs), BITSAT (for BITS Pilani campuses), VITEEE (for VIT University), and GATE (for postgraduate M.Tech/M.E. admissions). Many state engineering colleges also accept JEE Main scores for admission.

National-level exams like JEE Main and NEET are conducted across India by a central body (NTA) and are used for admissions to colleges across multiple states. State-level exams like MHT CET (Maharashtra), KCET (Karnataka), WBJEE (West Bengal), TNEA (Tamil Nadu), KEAM (Kerala), and REAP (Rajasthan) are conducted by the respective State Boards and are primarily used for admissions to engineering colleges within that state.

Yes, many states accept JEE Main scores for state-level counselling. For example, Haryana (HSTES), Madhya Pradesh (DTE MP), Rajasthan (REAP), and Uttarakhand accept JEE Main percentile/rank. However, states like Maharashtra (MHT CET), Karnataka (KCET), West Bengal (WBJEE), Tamil Nadu (TNEA), and Kerala (KEAM) have their own separate exams that candidates must additionally appear for to be eligible for state quota seats.

Most major entrance exams follow an annual schedule: JEE Main Session 1 is usually held in January–February, Session 2 in April. JEE Advanced is held in May. NEET UG is held in May. MHT CET is held in April–May. WBJEE is held in April. KCET is held in April. KEAM is held in April. TNEA counselling begins in June based on Class 12 marks. COMEDK UGET is held in May. Exact dates are announced by the respective exam authorities each year.
