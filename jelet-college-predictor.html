How to Use the JELET Predictor for West Bengal Lateral Entry Admissions

Securing a direct 2nd-year B.Tech lateral entry seat in premier West Bengal institutions like Jadavpur University, Calcutta University, KGEC Kalyani, or JGEC Jalpaiguri requires understanding how WBJEEB General Merit Ranks (GMR), West Bengal Domicile rules (85-90% reserved seats), and category reservations (OBC-A, OBC-B, SC, ST, EWS) operate. The JELET College Predictor matches your rank against official opening-closing cutoffs.

Understanding WBJEEB Counselling & Reservation Rules

WBJEEB conducts centralized online e-counselling for JELET rank holders:• West Bengal Domicile Quota (85-90% Reservation): Candidates residing in West Bengal for 10 consecutive years qualify for domicile seats in Government Colleges & Universities.• All India Open Quota: Open to all candidates across India for non-domicile seats in private colleges and unreserved university seats.• Category Reservations: West Bengal reserves seats for OBC-A (10%), OBC-B (7%), SC (22%), ST (6%), and EWS (10%).• Direct 2nd-Year B.Tech Admission: Diploma holders and B.Sc graduates enter directly into the 3rd semester (2nd Year) of 4-year B.Tech / B.E. programs.

WBJEEB JELET Counselling Rules, Reservations & Fee Structure

Overview of West Bengal domicile quota, reservation percentages across OC/GMR, OBC-A, OBC-B, SC, ST, EWS, SVMCM scholarship, and e-counseling mechanics for lateral entry B.Tech admissions.

Fee Structure & SVMCM Scholarship Scheme

  • Government Universities & Colleges: Highly subsidized annual tuition fee: Jadavpur University (~Rs. 2,400/yr), Calcutta University (~Rs. 6,000/yr), and Govt Engg Colleges (~Rs. 12,000/yr).
  • Private Engineering Colleges: Annual tuition fee ranges from approx. Rs. 85,000/- to Rs. 1,20,000/- per year depending on the college tier.
  • Swami Vivekananda Scholarship (SVMCM): Eligible West Bengal domicile diploma holders with ≥60% diploma marks and family income ≤Rs 2.5 Lakhs receive Rs. 60,000/year scholarship for B.Tech. Kanyashree K3 benefits also apply for female candidates.

Domicile Rules & Reservation Quotas

  • West Bengal Domicile (85-90% Quota): Candidates who are permanent residents of West Bengal or living in WB for 10 years qualify for reserved domicile seats in state government universities and colleges.
  • All India / Non-Domicile Quota: Open to candidates from all Indian states for seats in self-financing private colleges and unreserved university seats.
  • Category Sub-Reservations: West Bengal state reservation includes OBC-A (10%), OBC-B (7%), SC (22%), ST (6%), and EWS (10%).
  • General Merit Rank (GMR): All seat allotments in WBJEEB e-counseling are strictly governed by candidate GMR, followed by category merit rank validation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the JELET College Predictor and how does it work?
The JELET College Predictor is a free online tool that estimates your seat allotment chances for direct 2nd-year B.Tech / B.E. admission in West Bengal Government & Private Engineering Colleges (including Jadavpur University, Calcutta University, KGEC, JGEC, IEM, Heritage) based on your WBJEEB JELET General Merit Rank (GMR), category (OC/GMR, OBC-A, OBC-B, SC, ST, EWS), and WB Domicile status.
How does the West Bengal Domicile Quota work in JELET?
WB Domicile eligibility is granted to candidates who have been residing continuously in West Bengal for at least 10 years as of the application date, or whose parents are permanent residents of West Bengal. Domicile candidates receive 85% to 90% seat reservation in all West Bengal State Government engineering colleges and state university departments.
What JELET rank is required for Computer Science (CSE) at Jadavpur University?
For General Merit Rank (GMR) candidates under WB Domicile, lateral entry B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering (CSE) at Jadavpur University usually closes within GMR 120 to 160 rank. ECE closes around GMR 280 to 320, while Electrical Engineering (EE) closes around GMR 450.
Can diploma holders apply for the Swami Vivekananda Scholarship (SVMCM) in lateral B.Tech?
Yes! Diploma engineering students admitted to B.Tech 2nd year through WBJEEB JELET who scored 60% or higher in their diploma examination and have an annual family income of less than Rs. 2.5 Lakhs can apply for the SVMCM scholarship, receiving financial assistance of Rs. 60,000 per year.
What are the tuition fees for Government vs Private Engineering Colleges in West Bengal?
Government Universities and State Engineering Colleges in West Bengal offer highly affordable education: Jadavpur University (~Rs. 2,400/year for CSE), Calcutta University (~Rs. 6,000/year), and Govt Engg Colleges (~Rs. 12,000/year). Private engineering colleges charge between Rs. 85,000 to Rs. 1,20,000 per year.
How does WBJEEB JELET e-Counseling & Web Choice Filling work?
WBJEEB conducts centralized online e-counseling after JELET results. Candidates register online, upload documents for verification, pay the registration fee, fill and lock their college-branch choices in priority order. Allotments are declared in Round 1, Round 2, and Decentralized Mop-Up rounds based on GMR rank and reservation rules.
