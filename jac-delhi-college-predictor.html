How to Use the JAC Delhi College Predictor for B.Tech Admissions

Securing admission in top Delhi engineering universities like DTU, NSUT, and IIIT Delhi requires accurate insights into JAC Delhi closing ranks and regional seat reservation rules. The JAC Delhi College Predictor compares your rank with multi-year cutoff trends to help you build an optimal preference list.

Understanding 85% Delhi Region vs 15% Outside Delhi Quota

JAC Delhi seat matrix is divided based on the location of the school from which you completed Class 12th: • Delhi Region (85% Quota): Candidates who passed 10+2 from any school located within NCT of Delhi. • Outside Delhi Region (15% Quota): Candidates who passed 10+2 from any school outside Delhi.

Fee Structure & Financial Assistance Scheme (FFS)

  • DTU & NSUT Annual Fee: ₹2.19L to ₹2.29L per year covering tuition, examination, student activity, and university facilities.
  • Delhi Govt Fee Waiver (FFS): Family income < ₹2.5L gets 100% tuition waiver; income ₹2.5L-₹6.0L gets 50% waiver; income ₹6.0L-₹8.0L gets 25% waiver.
  • IIIT Delhi Bonus Points: Up to 2 extra bonus percentile points awarded for achievements in Olympiads, Progate, Sports, Chess, Culture, and Innovation.

Steps to Build Your JAC Delhi Choice List

  • Export Preference List: Add preferred choices to your Preference List, reorder them, and export to guide your official JAC Delhi online choice submission.

JAC Delhi Seat Reservation & Domicile Structure

Understanding category reservation quotas, 85% Delhi Region rules, and JAC Delhi online counselling round dynamics.

Statutory Reservation & Domicile Rules

JAC Delhi seat allocation strictly adheres to Delhi state statutory reservation quotas:

  • Delhi Region Quota (85%): 85% of total seats reserved for candidates passing Class 12th from schools located within NCT of Delhi.
  • Outside Delhi Quota (15%): 15% seats open for All-India candidates passing Class 12th from outside Delhi.
  • OBC-NCL Reservation (27%): 27% seats for Non-Creamy Layer OBC candidates (Delhi OBC list mandatory for 85% quota).
  • SC (15%) & ST (7.5%): Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe reservations with significant rank relaxation.
  • EWS Reservation (10%): 10% seats reserved for Economically Weaker Section candidates in DTU, NSUT, and IGDTUW.

JAC Counselling Rounds & Spot Round

Closing JEE Main ranks across counselling stages depend on:

  • 5 Centralized Rounds: Regular online seat allotment rounds based on submitted choice preferences.
  • Upgradation Round: Internal branch sliding within the admitted university after Round 5.
  • Spot Round: Physical round conducted at DTU campus for remaining vacant seats with relaxed closing ranks.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the JAC Delhi College Predictor and how does it work?
The JAC Delhi College Predictor is a free online tool that estimates your B.Tech and B.Arch admission chances across top Delhi government institutions (DTU, NSUT Dwarka, IIIT Delhi, IGDTUW, DSEU). Enter your JEE Main Rank (CRL or Category Rank), select your region quota (Delhi 85% or Outside Delhi 15%) and reservation category to see High, Medium, or Low admission probabilities based on official closing cutoffs.
Who is eligible for the 85% Delhi Region Quota in JAC Delhi?
Candidates who have passed their Class 12th / qualifying examination from a school or college located within the National Capital Territory (NCT) of Delhi qualify for the 85% Delhi Region Quota, regardless of their native home state. Candidates passing 12th outside Delhi come under the 15% Outside Delhi Quota.
How are IIIT Delhi Bonus Points awarded in JAC Delhi?
IIIT Delhi grants up to 2 extra bonus percentile points during seat allocation for accomplishments in National/International Olympiads (Maths, Physics, Informatics), Progate, National Sports, Chess, Cultural events, and Innovation contests.
What fee concession schemes exist for DTU, NSUT, and IIIT Delhi students?
Under the Delhi Government Financial Assistance Scheme (FFS), students with annual family income up to ₹2.5 Lakhs get a 100% tuition fee waiver. Income between ₹2.5L to ₹6.0L gets a 50% tuition waiver, and income between ₹6.0L to ₹8.0L gets a 25% fee concession across DTU, NSUT, IIIT Delhi, IGDTUW, and DSEU.
Can male candidates get admission to IGDTUW through JAC Delhi?
No. IGDTUW (Indira Gandhi Delhi Technical University for Women) is exclusively for female candidates. Male candidates can apply for DTU, NSUT Dwarka, NSUT East/West campuses, IIIT Delhi, and DSEU.
