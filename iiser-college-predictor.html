How to Use the IISER Predictor for IAT Admissions

Securing a seat in premier scientific institutions like IISER Pune, IISER Kolkata, IISER Mohali, IISc Bangalore, or IIT Madras requires understanding how the IISER Aptitude Test (IAT) score, overall All India Rank (AIR), category reservations (OBC-NCL, EWS, SC, ST, PwD, KM), and multi-round JAC e-counselling function. The IISER College Predictor matches your rank against official opening-closing cutoffs.

Understanding IISER Admissions & JAC Rules

The IISER Joint Admissions Committee (JAC) conducts centralized online counselling based on IAT ranks:• Single Entrance Channel (IAT): Starting from recent admission cycles, IISER admissions are streamlined primarily through the IISER Aptitude Test (IAT) across all 7 IISERs.• Category Reservations: Government of India central reservation rules apply: OBC-NCL (27%), SC (15%), ST (7.5%), EWS (10%), PwD (5% horizontal), and Kashmiri Migrants (KM).• IISc Bangalore & IIT Madras via IAT: IISc Bangalore accepts IAT ranks for its 4-Year BS (Research) program, and IIT Madras accepts IAT ranks for its BS in Medical Sciences & Engineering.• Multi-Round Allocation & Freeze/Float: JAC declares seat allotments in up to 8-9 rounds. Candidates can Freeze their seat or opt for Float to upgrade to a higher preference IISER in subsequent rounds.

Premier IISER Campuses & Academic Programs

  • IISER Pune & IISER Kolkata: Established in 2006, these are the flagship premier IISERs. Boasting state-of-the-art supercomputing facilities, NMR centers, clean rooms, and world-class faculty. ~65-70% of graduates secure PhD offers at top global universities like Harvard, MIT, Cambridge, and Max Planck.
  • IISER Mohali & IISER Bhopal: IISER Bhopal is unique in offering 4-Year BS Engineering Sciences (Data Science, Electrical & CS, Chemical Engg) and BS Economic Sciences with high industry placements (Avg 12.5 LPA) alongside the BS-MS program. IISER Mohali excels in Structural Biology & Quantum Physics.
  • IISER TVM, IISER Tirupati & IISER Berhampur: Rapidly expanding campuses with permanent modern infrastructure, specialized research centers in Western Ghats ecology, advanced materials, photonics, and marine sciences.
  • IISc Bangalore (BS Research) & IIT Madras (BS Medical Sciences): Top research institutes accepting IAT AIR ranks for specialized 4-year interdisciplinary undergraduate degrees with stellar career outcomes.

IISER Admission Rules, Fellowships & Fee Structure

Overview of central reservation quotas across GEN, OBC-NCL, EWS, SC, ST, PwD, KM, PM-USP / INSPIRE fellowships, fee structure, and JAC e-counseling mechanics for BS-MS admissions.

Fee Structure & Scholarships

  • IISER Tuition Fees: Affordable fee structure of approx. Rs. 40,000 to Rs. 46,000 per semester (Tuition + Hostel). SC/ST candidates receive 100% tuition fee waiver.
  • PM-USP / INSPIRE Scholarship: Eligible students admitted to 5-Year BS-MS programs can receive the PM-USP / INSPIRE SHE Fellowship offering Rs. 80,000 per year (Rs 60,000 scholarship + Rs 20,000 mentorship grant).
  • Hostel & Campus Facilities: Mandatory residential programs with fully furnished individual/shared hostel rooms, 24/7 high-speed WiFi, library, sports complexes, and dining halls.

Reservation Rules & JAC Rounds

  • Central Reservation Policy: 27% for OBC-NCL, 15% for SC, 7.5% for ST, 10% for EWS, 5% horizontal reservation for Persons with Disability (PwD), and supernumerary seats for Kashmiri Migrants (KM).
  • Overall All India Rank (AIR): All seat allocations in JAC counselling are processed strictly based on the candidate's IAT Overall All India Rank followed by category rank validation.
  • Freeze / Float Mechanism: Candidates receiving a seat allotment can Freeze to accept the seat or Float to remain eligible for higher priority preferences in subsequent JAC rounds.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the IISER College Predictor and how does it work?
The IISER College Predictor is a free online tool that estimates your seat allotment chances for 5-Year BS-MS Dual Degree, 4-Year BS Engineering Sciences, and Integrated MS programs across all 7 IISERs (Pune, Kolkata, Mohali, Bhopal, TVM, Tirupati, Berhampur), IISc Bangalore, and IIT Madras based on your official IISER Aptitude Test (IAT) Overall All India Rank (AIR) and category.
What IAT rank is required for admission to IISER Pune or IISER Kolkata?
For General (GEN-AIR) category candidates, IISER Pune BS-MS dual degree typically closes within Overall AIR 550 to 600 in early JAC rounds. IISER Kolkata closes around AIR 1000 to 1050, IISER Mohali around AIR 1300 to 1350, and IISER Bhopal around AIR 1700 to 1750.
Which IISER offers 4-Year BS Engineering Sciences & Data Science?
IISER Bhopal is the only IISER that offers 4-Year BS degree programs in Engineering Sciences (Chemical Engg, Data Science & Engineering, Electrical Engg & Computer Science) and Economic Sciences admitting students via IAT ranks, with excellent tech placement statistics (Average CTC ~12.5 LPA).
Can I get admission to IISc Bangalore or IIT Madras through IAT score?
Yes! IISc Bangalore accepts IAT ranks for its prestigious 4-Year BS (Research) program (closing within AIR 140-150 for General), and IIT Madras accepts IAT ranks for its 4-Year BS in Medical Sciences & Engineering.
What are the career opportunities and PhD placements after BS-MS from IISER?
IISER graduates enjoy unparalleled global academic recognition. Approximately 60% to 70% of BS-MS graduates secure fully-funded PhD fellowships at top Ivy League & European institutions (Harvard, MIT, Stanford, Oxford, Cambridge, Max Planck). Other graduates pursue R&D roles, data science, biotechnology, and scientific consulting.
How does IISER JAC e-Counseling choice filling & seat Float/Freeze work?
JAC conducts centralized online e-counseling. Candidates register on the portal, upload Class 12 marksheet & category certificates, pay the fee, and rank their IISER campus preferences. Upon receiving an offer in a round, candidates can 'Freeze' to confirm or 'Float' to automatically upgrade to a higher preference campus in subsequent rounds.
