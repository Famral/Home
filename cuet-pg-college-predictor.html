CUET PG Admission & University Counselling Process Guide

CUET PG admission is conducted independently by each participating university through their respective admission portals (such as DU CSAS PG, BHU SAMARTH, JNU Portal).

Step-by-Step Counselling Procedure

  • Step 1: Result Declaration & Scorecard: Download NTA CUET PG scorecard with subject-wise normalized scores and percentile.
  • Step 2: University Portal Registration: Register on individual university portals (e.g., DU CSAS PG, BHU PG Admissions, JNU e-counselling) and fill course preferences.
  • Step 3: Merit List & Cutoff Releases: Universities publish Category-wise Cutoff Lists across Round 1, Round 2, and Spot rounds.
  • Step 4: Document Verification & Fee Payment: Accept allocated seat, upload required undergraduate marksheets/caste certificates, and pay the admission fee to freeze admission.

Key Factors Influencing CUET PG Cutoffs

Key elements that determine the final cutoff for your targeted program:

  • Difficulty Level of Subject Paper: Harder question papers lead to lower qualifying cutoff scores across all categories.
  • Total Candidates Appeared: Higher number of test takers for popular subjects (Economics, Pol Sci, Physics) increases cutoff competition.
  • Category Reservation Quotas: Standard central reservation applies: OBC-NCL (27%), SC (15%), ST (7.5%), EWS (10%), and PwD (5% horizontal).

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is the CUET PG College Predictor and how does it work?
The CUET PG College Predictor is a free tool that calculates your admission probability into postgraduate courses (M.Sc., M.A., M.Com, MBA, MCA, LL.M, B.Ed) across top central universities such as Delhi University (DU), Jawaharlal Nehru University (JNU), Banaras Hindu University (BHU), Jamia Millia Islamia (JMI), and University of Hyderabad (HCU) based on official expected cutoff scores.
What are the CUET PG qualifying scores for General, OBC, SC, and ST?
Depending on university competition and course popularity, approximate CUET PG qualifying scores are: General: 220-230, OBC/OBC-NCL: 190-210, EWS: 190-200, SC: 170-185, ST: 165-175, and PwD: 100-150.
What is considered a good score in CUET PG for admission into top Central Universities?
Scoring 240+ score (or 90+ percentile) is considered an excellent score for top tier programs like M.A. Economics/English, M.Sc. Physics/Chemistry/Maths, and MBA in premier universities like DU, JNU, BHU, and HCU.
Does qualifying the CUET PG cut off guarantee admission?
No. Qualifying the cutoff score makes you eligible for counselling registration. Final seat allotment depends on the merit list rank, category reservation quotas, and seat availability in each university during counselling rounds.
How is the CUET PG Cut Off calculated?
CUET PG Cut off is determined by universities based on factors such as paper difficulty level, total number of candidates who appeared for the exam, total available seats in the department, and reservation criteria as per Government of India guidelines.
