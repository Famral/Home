NMAT Psychometric Scoring & NMIMS Shortlisting Process Explained

NMAT by GMAC employs computer-adaptive psychometric scoring across 3 sections to compute a scaled score (36 to 360). Shortlisting for Competency-based Interview / CD-PI at NMIMS follows rigorous overall and sectional criteria:

GMAC Scaled Scoring & Percentile Formula

The NMAT Percentile is computed relative to the full testing window candidate pool:

Percentile (P) = [(N − R) / N] × 100
  • N: Total number of candidates who appeared in NMAT during the testing window (~90,000).
  • R: Candidate merit rank based on total scaled score (36 to 360).
  • Sectional Scaled Scores: Each section (Language, Quant, Logic) is scored on a scale of 12 to 120.
  • NMIMS First Attempt Rule: NMIMS Mumbai and off-campuses strictly consider only your First (Main) Attempt score.

NMAT 2026 Exam Pattern & Sectional Marking Scheme

NMAT by GMAC is a 2-hour (120 minutes) computer-delivered exam with 108 questions across 3 sections. Candidates have the freedom to choose section order, and there is zero negative marking:

Overall Exam Structure (36 – 360 Score)

  • Total Duration: 2 Hours (120 minutes) with individual section timers
  • Total Questions: 108 Questions (36 in each of the 3 sections)
  • Scaled Score Range: 36 to 360 (12 to 120 per section)
  • Attempts Allowed: Up to 3 attempts (Main Attempt + 2 Retakes)

Sectional Distribution

  • Language Skills: 36 Questions • 28 Minutes (Score: 12–120)
  • Quantitative Skills: 36 Questions • 52 Minutes (Score: 12–120)
  • Logical Reasoning: 36 Questions • 40 Minutes (Score: 12–120)
  • Order Choice: You can select the sequence of sections before starting

Marking & Negative Marks

  • Correct Answer: Scaled psychometric score calculated automatically
  • Negative Marking: NO Negative Marking (0 penalty)
  • Unattempted: 0 Marks — Always attempt all 108 questions
  • Score Instant Delivery: Unofficial score is displayed right after completion

NMIMS Selection Process

  • Stage 1 (NMAT Cutoff): Clearing overall & sectional scaled cutoffs
  • Stage 2 (CD / WAT / PI): Case Discussion, Writing Ability Test & Interview
  • Academic Profile: Class 10th, 12th, and Graduation percentages evaluated
  • Work Experience: Relevant executive experience adds composite weightage

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How does the NMAT Percentile Predictor calculate my Percentile & Merit Rank?
The NMAT Percentile Predictor uses the official GMAC Equipercentile Formula: Percentile = [(N − R) / N] × 100, where N is the total candidate pool (~90,000) and R is your all-India merit rank. The algorithm calibrates your scaled score (36 to 360) against historical GMAC scoring tables and psychometric distributions from previous NMAT cycles to deliver an accurate scaled score vs percentile forecast.
What NMAT scaled score is required for NMIMS Mumbai MBA Core?
For NMIMS Mumbai School of Business Management (SBM) flagship MBA Core, a scaled score of 235+ (around 95–97+ percentile) is typically required on your First Attempt. In addition, candidates must clear sectional cutoffs of approximately 72+ in Language Skills, 72+ in Quantitative Skills, and 71+ in Logical Reasoning.
Does NMIMS accept NMAT Retake scores (Attempt 2 & 3)?
No. NMIMS Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Navi Mumbai, and Indore accept only your First (Main) Attempt score. However, NMAT Retake scores are accepted by other premier business schools such as TAPMI Manipal, XIM University Bhubaneswar, K J Somaiya, SDA Bocconi Asia Center, and Great Lakes.
What are the cutoffs for NMIMS Bengaluru, Navi Mumbai, and Hyderabad?
NMIMS Bengaluru typically cutoffs at 224+, NMIMS Navi Mumbai at 220+, NMIMS Hyderabad at 218+, and NMIMS Indore at 208–212+ on the first attempt, along with qualifying sectional scores around 65+ across sections.
Is there negative marking in NMAT by GMAC?
No negative marking exists in NMAT. There is zero deduction for wrong answers or unattempted questions. Because of this, it is strongly recommended to attempt all 108 questions before the respective sectional timer expires.
Which top non-NMIMS business schools accept NMAT scores?
Prominent institutes accepting NMAT by GMAC include: TAPMI Manipal (MBA programs, 225+ cutoff), XIM University Bhubaneswar (MBA-HRM, MBA-RM, 220+ cutoff), K J Somaiya Institute of Management (225+ cutoff), SDA Bocconi Asia Center (IMB program), Great Lakes Institute of Management, BML Munjal University, ISBR Business School, and Alliance School of Business.
