How to Use the TANCET Predictor for TANCA Admissions

Securing a seat in top institutions such as Anna University CEG Guindy, PSG Tech Coimbatore, MIT Chromepet, GCT Coimbatore, or SSN Chennai requires understanding how the Tamil Nadu Common Entrance Test (TANCET) score, overall state rank, and the 69% Tamil Nadu reservation system function during TANCA single window counselling.

Understanding TANCET Marks, Ranks & 69% Reservation Policy

Anna University conducts TANCET for PG admissions in Tamil Nadu:• Score Normalization: Raw marks in TANCET are normalized using standard deviation formulas to produce a uniform TANCET Score (0 to 100) and State Rank.• 69% Tamil Nadu Reservation Breakdown: Open Competition (OC - 31%), Backward Class (BC - 26.5%), Backward Class Muslim (BCM - 3.5%), Most Backward Class / Denotified Communities (MBC/DNC - 20%), Scheduled Caste (SC - 15%), Scheduled Caste Arunthathiyar (SCA - 3%), and Scheduled Tribe (ST - 1%).• TANCA Single Window Counselling: Directorate of Technical Education (DOTE) conducts online counselling. Candidates register, upload certificates, pay initial fees, fill college options, and receive tentative seat allotment.

TANCET Fee Structure, Scholarships & TANCA Counselling Rules

Overview of Tamil Nadu reservation policy, tuition fee waiver schemes for SC/ST candidates, scholarship schemes, and single-window TANCA counselling mechanics.

Fee Structure & Scholarships

  • Affordable Fees in University Depts & Govt Colleges: Tuition fees in Anna University Depts (CEG/MIT) range between Rs. 20,000 to Rs. 25,000 per semester. Govt-Aided colleges range between Rs. 30,000 to Rs. 45,000 per semester.
  • Full Tuition Fee Waiver for SC/ST/SCA: Government of Tamil Nadu provides 100% tuition fee waiver for eligible SC, ST, and SCA candidates admitted under single window counselling.
  • Post-Matric & BC/MBC Welfare Scholarships: BC, BCM, and MBC/DNC candidates with family annual income under eligible limits receive state welfare scholarship grants.

69% Reservation & TANCA Rules

  • 69% State Reservation: Seat allocation follows strict Tamil Nadu reservation quota: OC (31%), BC (26.5%), BCM (3.5%), MBC/DNC (20%), SC (15%), SCA (3%), ST (1%).
  • State Overall Rank Order: All single-window seat allocations are processed in strict descending order of TANCET State Rank with category reservation mapping.
  • Online Choice Filling & Seat Acceptance: Candidates fill college/branch choices in order of priority. After tentative allotment, candidates confirm, upgrade, or decline allotment.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the TANCET College Predictor and how does it work?
The TANCET College Predictor is a free online tool that estimates your admission seat allotment chances for MBA, MCA, and M.E./M.Tech programs across Anna University Departments (CEG Guindy, ACTech, MIT Chromepet), PSG Tech, GCT Coimbatore, TCE Madurai, SSN, and top Tamil Nadu colleges based on your TANCET State Rank and category reservation (OC, BC, BCM, MBC/DNC, SC, SCA, ST).
What TANCET rank/score is required for MBA in Anna University CEG Guindy?
For Open Competition (OC) candidates, MBA at Anna University Department of Management Studies (DOMS CEG Guindy) typically closes within TANCET State Rank 120-150 (approx 99+ percentile / 65+ marks). For BC category, it closes around rank 250, MBC/DNC around rank 380, and SC around rank 850.
Which are the top colleges for MCA through TANCET in Tamil Nadu?
Top MCA colleges via TANCET include Anna University CEG Guindy, MIT Campus Chromepet, PSG College of Technology Coimbatore, Government College of Technology (GCT) Coimbatore, Thiagarajar College of Engineering Madurai, SSN College of Engineering Chennai, and Coimbatore Institute of Technology (CIT).
How does Tamil Nadu 69% reservation work in TANCA / TANCET counselling?
Tamil Nadu enforces a 69% statutory reservation: Open Competition (OC - 31%), Backward Class (BC - 26.5%), Backward Class Muslim (BCM - 3.5%), Most Backward Class / Denotified Communities (MBC/DNC - 20%), Scheduled Caste (SC - 15%), Scheduled Caste Arunthathiyar (SCA - 3%), and Scheduled Tribe (ST - 1%).
What is the fee structure for MBA & MCA in Govt and Self-Financing colleges?
University Departments and Government Colleges charge approximately Rs. 15,000 to Rs. 25,000 per semester. Government-Aided colleges charge around Rs. 30,000 to Rs. 45,000 per semester. Autonomous and Self-Financing colleges charge between Rs. 45,000 to Rs. 65,000 per semester under single window quota. SC/ST candidates get 100% tuition fee waiver.
How does TANCA online choice filling and seat allotment work?
DOTE Tamil Nadu conducts single-window online counselling. Candidates log in to the TANCA portal, select college and branch preferences in order of choice, lock preferences, and view tentative seat allotment results based on TANCET rank and category reservation rules.
