BITSAT 2027 Examination & Admission Overview

Administered annually by BITS Pilani (an Institution of Eminence deemed-to-be-university), the BITS Admission Test is widely regarded as India's most prestigious private engineering benchmark, evaluating over 3.2 lakh aspirants across two online computer-based sessions for approximately 3,000 highly coveted undergraduate seats.

Full Examination Name Birla Institute of Technology and Science Admission Test (BITSAT 2027)
Conducting University Birla Institute of Technology & Science (BITS Pilani) — Institution of Eminence (IoE)
Participating Campuses BITS Pilani (Main Campus), BITS Pilani - K. K. Birla Goa Campus, and BITS Pilani - Hyderabad Campus (Separate merit admissions for BITS Dubai Campus based on 12th Board marks)
Degree Programs Offered B.E. (Hons.): Computer Science, Mathematics & Computing (MnC), ECE, EEE, ENI, Mechanical, Chemical, Civil, Manufacturing.
M.Sc. (Hons.) Dual Degree: Economics, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biological Sciences (plus secondary B.E. allotted after Year 1).
B.Pharm (Hons.): 4-Year Bachelor of Pharmacy.
Examination Mode & Window Online Computer Based Test (CBT) conducted in two independent nationwide phases (Session 1 in May 2027; Session 2 in June 2027). Best of two scores considered.
Test Duration & Total Marks 3 Hours (180 Minutes), 130 Multiple Choice Questions totaling 390 Marks (+12 Bonus Questions worth 36 Marks upon answering all 130 Qs).
Marking Scheme +3 Marks for each correct response, -1 Mark for each incorrect response, 0 Marks for unattempted questions. No sectional time restrictions.
Reservation & Quotas Zero Quotas / Pure 100% Merit: No caste, state domicile, or management reservation. First-rank Board Toppers receive direct admission.
Official Admission Portal bitsadmission.com / bits-pilani.ac.in
⚡

Zero Attendance Policy

BITS Pilani upholds a world-renowned culture of academic freedom. Students are not penalized for classroom absence, empowering them to pursue deep open-source coding, robotics, high-impact research, and venture-backed startup building.

💼

Practice School (PS-I & PS-II)

Every engineering undergraduate undergoes structured industrial training: 2 months of PS-I in Year 2 and a full-time, 5.5 to 6-month corporate internship in PS-II during Year 4/5, yielding an exceptional 70%+ Pre-Placement Offer (PPO) conversion rate.

🌐

Unified Pan-BITS Placement Drive

All three domestic campuses (Pilani, Goa, Hyderabad) participate in a synchronized central placement drive. The degree certificate awarded does not differentiate campus prestige, guaranteeing identical tier-1 corporate recruiter exposure.

Eligibility Criteria & Direct Admission for Board Toppers

BITS Pilani maintains strict academic prerequisites for admission. Review the twin requirements of PCM aggregate and individual subject percentages, along with the prestigious Board Toppers direct admission policy.

📋

75% Aggregate in PCM/PCB

Candidates must have obtained a minimum aggregate of 75% marks in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (or Physics, Chemistry, and Biology for B.Pharm) in their Class 12 board examination or its equivalent.

🎯

60% Minimum Subject Rule

In addition to the 75% aggregate, candidates must score at least 60% marks in each individual subject—Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics/Biology separately. No rounding off is permissible.

⏳

Qualifying Year Limit

Only students who are appearing in Class 12 examination in 2027 or who have passed Class 12 in 2026 are eligible. Students who passed in 2025 or earlier are strictly ineligible to attempt BITSAT 2027.

🏆

Direct Admission for First Rank Holders (Board Toppers)

In keeping with BITS Pilani's tradition of recognizing pure academic excellence, the first rank holders (Toppers) of all recognized Central and State Education Boards in India in the PCM stream are guaranteed direct admission to the program and campus of their choice (Pilani, Goa, or Hyderabad), completely bypassing the BITSAT score requirement!

Note: The first rank holder in the PCB stream from each board is likewise granted direct admission to the 4-year B.Pharm program. Candidates must fulfill the basic 75% aggregate and 60% per-subject eligibility rules.

The Legendary 12 Bonus Questions: Strategy & Rules

BITSAT is the only engineering entrance examination in India featuring an optional "Bonus Questions" booster that can elevate your maximum possible score from 390 to 426 marks.

⚠️
The Strict Golden Rule: To unlock the 12 Bonus Questions, you must have marked an answer for all 130 questions in the regular paper. Once you confirm the prompt to view bonus questions, you cannot return to or edit any of the previous 130 answers!

Structure of the 12 Bonus Questions

The 12 bonus questions are drawn in equal proportion across the core engineering subjects:

  • 3 Questions from Physics (+9 Marks)
  • 3 Questions from Chemistry (+9 Marks)
  • 3 Questions from Mathematics (or Biology) (+9 Marks)
  • 3 Questions from Logical Reasoning (+9 Marks)
  • Total Additional Marks: 36 Marks (Total Ceiling = 426 Marks)
  • Marking follows the exact same +3 / -1 scheme.

Strategic Decision Matrix: To Unlock or Not?

Before clicking "Unlock Bonus Questions", evaluate this mathematical threshold:

  • Unlock if: You have 15–20 minutes remaining, you have solved 120+ questions with high certainty, and you made calculated guesses on the remaining 5–10 questions.
  • Do NOT Unlock if: You blindly guessed 15+ questions just to reach the 130-question mark. Revising your marked doubts is statistically far more rewarding than taking on 12 new questions with -1 risk.

BITS Admission Iterations & Centralized Counselling Roadmap

Admission to Pilani, Goa, and Hyderabad is coordinated through a single centralized preference portal. Learn how the 6 Iteration rounds operate and how to secure your seat.

1️⃣

Stage 1: Preference Choice Submission

In June, qualified candidates log into bitsadmission.com and submit an exhaustive ranked preference list of degree programs across all 3 campuses. You can list as many programs as you wish in descending order of true preference.

2️⃣

Stage 2: Iteration 1 to 3 Allotments

The system computes cutoff thresholds and generates the Iteration 1 allotment list. If allotted a program, candidates must pay the requisite admission fee and advance semester fees to confirm their seat or remain in the sliding pool.

3️⃣

Stage 3: Floating, Upgrades & Iteration 4–6

Candidates who pay the advance fee are automatically considered for upward movement to higher-ranked choices in Iteration 2, 3, 4, 5, and 6. Once allotted a higher preference, previous lower allotments are permanently vacated.

Key Rules of BITS Counselling Iterations

  • Single Consolidated List: There is no separate counselling for Goa or Hyderabad. All campuses and programs are handled on a single preference sheet.
  • Payment of Advance Fees: If an offer is made in any iteration and you fail to pay the required admission fees by the specified deadline, you are permanently removed from all subsequent iteration rounds.
  • Waitlist Status: Candidates on the waitlist who pay a nominal refundable advance fee will continue to be evaluated through subsequent iterations as seats fall vacant.
  • Campus Reporting & Spot Allotments: Candidates must physically report to their designated campus in early August for document verification, biometric capture, and orientation.

The BITS 5-Year M.Sc. Dual Degree: How B.E. Branch Allotment Works

The M.Sc. Dual Degree is one of BITS Pilani's most popular pathways. Secure an M.Sc. degree in year 1, and earn a prestigious B.E. engineering degree (such as Computer Science) based purely on your first-year CGPA!

How the Dual Degree Mechanism Operates

1. Year 1 (Common Curriculum): All first-year students—whether in 4-Year B.E. or 5-Year M.Sc.—study the exact same foundational engineering math, physics, chemistry, coding, and workshop courses.

2. End of Year 1 Allotment: Based entirely on your 1st-year Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA), a secondary B.E. branch is allotted. There is zero BITSAT score consideration at this stage!

3. Graduation with Two Degrees: In 5 years, you graduate with two distinct degrees: e.g., M.Sc. Economics + B.E. Computer Science. Dual degree graduates enjoy identical recruitment rights and placements as single-degree students.
