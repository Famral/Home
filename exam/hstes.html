HSTES 2027 Overview & Key Highlights

The Haryana State Technical Education Society (HSTES), functioning under the aegis of the Department of Technical Education, Government of Haryana, administers centralized online web counselling for undergraduate engineering admissions across prestigious state universities and colleges.

Admission Process Name Haryana State Technical Education Society Centralized Online Counselling (HSTES 2027)
Organizing Body Haryana State Technical Education Society (HSTES), Panchkula, Haryana
Official Web Portals Counselling & Choice Filling: techadmissionshry.gov.in
Society & Notification Portal: hstes.org.in
Courses Offered B.Tech (1st Year), B.Tech Lateral Entry (2nd Year LEET), B.Arch, B.Pharmacy
Admission Channel (B.Tech 1st Year) JEE Main 2027 All India Common Rank List (CRL) — No separate entrance examination
State Quota & Residency Verification 85% Haryana State Quota dynamically validated using Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP) Family ID; 15% All India Category (AIC)
Counselling Mode 100% Online Off-Campus Web Counselling (2 Centralized Rounds + Institute-Level Physical Spot Rounds)
Counselling Fee ₹1,000 for General Category; ₹700 for Girls and Reserved Categories of Haryana (Non-refundable)
Undergraduate 4-Year

B.Tech 1st Year Admission Pathway

Admissions to 4-year degree programs across state universities and colleges based entirely on JEE Main All India CRL.

  • Single Merit Rank: NTA JEE Main 2027 All India Common Rank List (CRL).
  • Institutions: YMCA Faridabad, DCRUST Murthal, UIET KUK, UIET MDU, GJUS&T Hisar.
  • Academic Cutoff: 10+2 with Physics & Mathematics ≥ 45% (40% for reserved Haryana categories).
Lateral Entry 3-Year

B.Tech Lateral Entry (2nd Year LEET)

Direct admission into the 3rd semester / 2nd year of B.Tech for Diploma in Engineering and B.Sc degree holders.

  • Selection Metric: Qualifying examination merit (Diploma / B.Sc marks inter-se-merit).
  • Supernumerary Intake: 10% lateral entry supernumerary seats in addition to vacant seats.
  • Eligibility: Minimum 45% aggregate (40% for reserved category) in 3-year AICTE diploma or B.Sc.

Eligibility Criteria & Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP) Norms

Haryana mandates specific academic cutoffs, domicile norms, and electronic integration with the state Parivar Pehchan Patra (Family ID) database for all 85% Home State seats.

10+2 Academic Requirements

Mandatory
Candidates must have passed 10+2 (Senior Secondary) examination conducted by the Board of School Education Haryana (BSEH) or CBSE/ICSE/any recognized equivalent board with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects along with Chemistry, Biotechnology, Biology, or Technical Vocational subjects. Minimum aggregate: 45% marks (40% for SC, DSC, BCA, BCB, and PwD candidates of Haryana).

Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP)

Haryana Only
Haryana Government mandates that all candidates claiming Haryana Resident Quota (85%) and category reservations must provide an active Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP) Family ID. Annual family income, caste classification, and local domicile verification are pulled directly via API from the PPP registry without requiring manual paper attestations.

All India Category (15%)

Open Quota
Candidates who do not possess a Haryana Domicile or PPP Family ID are eligible under the 15% All India Category (AIC) in all state universities (YMCA, DCRUST, UIET) and 100% of seats in private self-financing engineering colleges. AIC applicants compete purely on JEE Main All India CRL merit.

Haryana Reservation Matrix & Quota Distribution

The Government of Haryana administers statutory vertical and horizontal reservations across state universities and government institutions according to official technical education norms.

All India Category (AIC)

15% Sanctioned
15% of sanctioned intake is open to all Indian citizens based strictly on JEE Main CRL without domicile restriction. Includes a 10% EWS allocation within AIC.

Haryana Open General (HOGC)

50% of State Quota
50% of the 85% Haryana State Quota seats (approximately 42.5% of total college intake) are unreserved for bona fide Haryana resident candidates on open merit.

SC & Deprived SC (DSC)

20% of State Quota
20% of state quota is reserved for Scheduled Castes, subdivided into 10% for Deprived Scheduled Castes (DSC) and 10% for general SC.

Backward Classes BCA & BCB

27% of State Quota
27% total reservation: 16% for Backward Classes Block 'A' (BCA) and 11% for Backward Classes Block 'B' (BCB). Non-Creamy Layer verified via PPP.

25% Women Horizontal Quota

Horizontal
25% horizontal reservation is carved out across all categories (HOGC Female, SC Female, DSC Female, BCA Female, BCB Female, EWS Female) in all government/aided colleges.

Tuition Fee Waiver (TFW)

5% Supernumerary
5% additional supernumerary seats in every branch for Haryana residents whose family income is below ₹8,00,000 / annum. 100% waiver of institutional tuition fees.

Step-by-Step Haryana B.Tech Admission Roadmap

A comprehensive 6-stage lifecycle guide ensuring a smooth journey from JEE Main results to final campus orientation.

1

JEE Main Qualification

Appear in JEE Main 2027 and secure an All India CRL rank. Retain official scorecards and application credentials.

2

PPP Family ID Validation

Haryana candidates must ensure their Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP) has updated income and category status before registration.

3

Online HSTES Registration

Log in to techadmissionshry.gov.in, pay the non-refundable registration fee, and verify credentials online.

4

Strategic Choice Filling

Fill choices in descending preference order (top dream branches first). Don't limit choices to just 2-3 options.

5

Seat Allotment & Decision

Download provisional allotment letter and pay the mandatory seat acceptance token fee to retain the seat.

6

College Physical Reporting

Report in person with original documents, character certificate, medical certificate, and institutional balance fees.

For All India Category (AIC), CSE closes around 25,000 to 30,000 CRL. Reserved categories like BCA close around 65,000 - 75,000, BCB around 55,000 - 62,000, and SC/DSC up to 1,20,000 - 1,50,000.
The Government of Haryana bifurcates the 20% Scheduled Caste (SC) state reservation into two equal halves: 50% of the SC quota (i.e. 10% of total state seats) is reserved exclusively for Deprived Scheduled Castes (DSC) as notified by the Haryana Government, and the remaining 50% (10% of state seats) is available for other Scheduled Castes (SC). If eligible DSC candidates are unavailable, the unfilled seats revert to the general SC category.
Under the AICTE TFW scheme, 5% supernumerary seats are created in every approved branch of engineering. These seats are available strictly to Haryana domicile candidates whose total annual family income from all sources is less than ₹8,00,000 (verified electronically through PPP Family ID). Candidates admitted under TFW are 100% exempt from paying tuition fees for the entire 4-year degree course.
In all government and government-aided engineering colleges in Haryana, 25% horizontal reservation is maintained for female candidates possessing bona fide Haryana residency across each vertical category (HOGC Female, SC Female, DSC Female, BCA Female, BCB Female, EWS Female).
In HSTES online counselling, if you are allotted a seat in Round 1 and wish to upgrade to a higher preferred branch or college in Round 2, you must pay the token seat acceptance fee and complete online provisional formalities. In Round 2 choice filling, you can enter higher choices. If upgraded, your previous seat is automatically cancelled and reallocated; if not upgraded, your Round 1 seat remains protected.
After two centralized online counselling rounds, participating colleges (including YMCA Faridabad, DCRUST Murthal, and UIETs) publish vacant seat matrices on their official websites. Candidates must attend physical on-campus spot counselling rounds where vacant seats are allotted on merit first to registered JEE Main CRL holders, and subsequently based on qualifying 10+2 PCM merit if seats remain vacant.
