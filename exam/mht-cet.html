MHT CET 2027 Highlights & Examination Framework

Essential facts, governing bodies, courses offered, entrance exam structure, and centralized admission architecture for Maharashtra engineering admissions.

Admission Process Centralized Admission Process (CAP 2027) for Engineering & Technology (First Year B.E. / B.Tech)
Conducting Body State Common Entrance Test Cell, Government of Maharashtra (CET Cell Mumbai)
Official Portals https://cetcell.mahacet.org/ & https://mahacet.org/
Academic Courses Bachelor of Engineering (B.E.) & Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) across Computer Science, AI/ML, Data Science, EnTC, IT, Mechanical, Electrical, Chemical, Civil & 30+ emerging branches
Exam Pattern (PCM) Computer-Based Test (CBT): 150 Multiple Choice Questions across 200 Marks (Mathematics: 50 Qs × 2 Marks = 100 Marks; Physics: 50 Qs × 1 Mark = 50 Marks; Chemistry: 50 Qs × 1 Mark = 50 Marks). No negative marking.
Scoring System Multi-session normalization using Percentile Scores up to 7 decimal places (e.g. 99.8732104) to eliminate session difficulty differences.
Merit Basis Maharashtra State Candidates: 100% based on MHT CET Percentile Score.
All India Candidates: JEE Main Paper 1 Percentile gets first preference over MHT CET.
Facilitation Modes Hybrid Scrutiny: Option to choose between online E-Scrutiny (no physical travel required) or in-person Physical Scrutiny at designated Facilitation Centres (FCs).
Key Seat Categories Home University (HU), Other Than Home University (OHU), State Level (SL - Autonomous Institutes), All India (15% in unaided colleges), TFWS (5% tuition fee waiver), EWS (10%), and Institutional/Management Quota.
Premier Institutes COEP Technological University (Pune), VJTI (Mumbai), SPIT (Mumbai), PICT (Pune), Walchand College of Engineering (Sangli), DJSCE (Mumbai), VIT (Pune), PCCOE (Pune), Cummins College for Women, and Government Engineering Colleges (Amravati, Aurangabad, Karad, Yavatmal, Chandrapur).

MHT CET Seat Matrix, University Quotas & Reservations

Understanding the distribution between Home University (HU), Other than Home University (OHU), State Level (SL), Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme (TFWS), and All India quota seats.

🏛️ Home University (HU) Seats

For non-autonomous colleges affiliated to a specific regional university (e.g. SPPU Pune, Mumbai University, DBATU), candidates passing 12th from that district hold Home University priority (historically 70% of state seats).

Regional Priority
🌐 Other Than Home Univ (OHU)

Maharashtra candidates passing 12th from outside the college's university jurisdiction compete for OHU seats (historically 30% of state seats). Cutoffs are typically 1–3% higher than HU.

Cross-University
⚡ State Level (SL) Seats

Premier Autonomous institutes like COEP Technological University, VJTI Mumbai, SPIT, and Walchand have 100% State Level seats. There is NO HU/OHU quota — all Maharashtra candidates compete on equal merit.

Autonomous Institutes
🎓 TFWS (100% Tuition Waiver)

5% extra supernumerary seats available in all engineering branches. 100% tuition fees waived for candidates with annual family income < ₹8,00,000. Cutoffs for TFWS are generally higher than open merit.

Step-by-Step MHT CET CAP Counselling Roadmap

A comprehensive walk-through of the six critical stages of Maharashtra Centralized Admission Process from initial registration to physical college joining.

Stage 1

Online Registration & Document Upload

Visit cetcell.mahacet.org, create your candidate login, and enter your personal, academic (10th/12th marks), and MHT CET / JEE Main roll numbers. Upload scanned copies of qualifying certificates, domicile, category proofs, and photo/signature.

Stage 2

E-Scrutiny or Physical FC Verification

Choose your verification mode. Under E-Scrutiny, CET Cell verification officers audit your uploaded documents digitally; if queries arise, you resolve them via your dashboard. Under Physical Scrutiny, you book an appointment slot and visit an FC in person with original documents.

Stage 3

Merit List & State Rank Verification

CET Cell releases the Provisional Merit List displaying your State General Merit (SML) rank, Home University rank, and Category rank. If any error exists (e.g. category not recognized), submit a grievance online with supporting documents before the Final Merit List is published.

Stage 4

Option Form Filling (Up to 300 Choices)

Carefully construct your preference list. The combination of Institute Code (e.g. 6006 for COEP) and Branch Choice Code (e.g. 10 for Computer Engineering) represents a single option. Fill choices in strict descending order of preference. Remember: Choice #1 triggers Auto-Freeze!

Stage 5

Seat Allotment & Acceptance Fee

Upon allotment announcement, log in to verify your allocated college and branch. Choose Auto-Freeze (mandatory if Choice #1), Self-Freeze, or Betterment. Pay the mandatory ₹1,000 online Seat Acceptance Fee to secure the seat.

Stage 6

Institute Reporting & Fee Payment

Candidates who freeze their seat must download their Seat Allotment Letter and report to the allotted engineering college within the notified schedule with original documents and college tuition fee demand draft / online payment to finalize admission.

Frequently Asked Questions (MHT CET 2027 CAP)

Clear answers to the most common questions about percentile normalization, counselling rules, seat allotment, and reporting.

What is the difference between Auto-Freeze and Self-Freeze in MHT CET? ▾
Auto-Freeze happens automatically when you receive your 1st Preference choice in any CAP round. Because your highest dream choice was granted, the CET Cell system permanently locks your seat and excludes you from subsequent CAP rounds. You must pay the ₹1,000 Seat Acceptance Fee and report to the college. Self-Freeze occurs when you are allotted choice #2 or lower, and you voluntarily decide that you love this seat and wish to confirm admission right now without trying for higher choices in further rounds.
What happens if I choose 'Betterment' (Not Freeze) and do not get a higher seat in Round 2? ▾
If you choose Betterment and pay the ₹1,000 online Seat Acceptance Fee, your Round 1 allotted seat remains 100% safe and reserved for you. If Round 2 allocates you a higher-preference choice, your previous seat is automatically cancelled and offered to someone else. However, if you are NOT allotted any higher choice in Round 2, your Round 1 seat continues to be safely retained.
Can outside Maharashtra students get admission in COEP or VJTI through JEE Main? ▾
No. 100% of seats in Government and Government-Aided engineering colleges in Maharashtra (including COEP Technological University, VJTI Mumbai, and Government Colleges of Engineering at Amravati, Aurangabad, Karad, etc.) are strictly reserved for Maharashtra State Candidates based on MHT CET merit. Outside Maharashtra candidates can only apply for 15% All India Quota seats in unaided private engineering colleges (such as SPIT, PICT, DJSCE, VIT Pune, PCCOE) via their JEE Main Paper 1 score.
How is MHT CET Percentile calculated across multiple shifts? ▾
MHT CET is conducted across multiple days and morning/afternoon shifts. To ensure that students in tougher shifts are not penalized, CET Cell uses a mathematical Normalization Formula:

Percentile = (100 × Number of candidates in the shift with raw score ≤ Candidate's raw score) ÷ (Total number of candidates who appeared in that shift)

Percentile scores are calculated to 7 decimal places to avoid ties in state ranks.
What is the TFWS Quota and how do I apply for it? ▾
The Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme (TFWS) reserves 5% supernumerary seats in all approved engineering colleges in Maharashtra. Selected candidates receive a 100% waiver on their annual tuition fee. To be eligible, the candidate must hold Maharashtra State Domicile and submit an annual Family Income Certificate below ₹8,00,000 issued by the Tehsildar / SDO. During option form filling, TFWS choices have separate choice codes (ending with 'T').
What is the difference between E-Scrutiny and Physical Scrutiny? ▾
Under E-Scrutiny, you upload digital copies of all certificates through your candidate login; an allotted verification officer examines your documents remotely. If discrepancies are found, you receive an online query and can re-upload corrections from home. Under Physical Scrutiny, you must book an appointment slot and visit a designated Facilitation Centre (FC) in person with original documents for on-the-spot physical verification. Both modes have identical merit validity.
What is an ACAP / Spot Admission Round? ▾
ACAP stands for Against CAP Vacancy. After the conclusion of Centralized CAP Round 3, seats that remain vacant due to non-reporting or cancellations are returned to the respective colleges. The institutions then conduct institutional-level open merit Spot Rounds. All registered candidates who have an MHT CET or JEE Main merit rank can participate directly at the college campus.
Is there any negative marking in MHT CET? ▾
No. There is no negative marking in MHT CET. In the PCM group, Mathematics consists of 50 questions awarding 2 marks each (100 marks), while Physics and Chemistry consist of 50 questions each awarding 1 mark (100 marks total), bringing the total test score to 200 marks.
