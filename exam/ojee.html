OJEE 2027 Overview & Key Highlights

The Odisha Joint Entrance Examination (OJEE) is the state-level statutory authority governed by the Skill Development and Technical Education (SDTE) Department, Government of Odisha. OJEE oversees unified centralised web counselling for undergraduate 4-year B.Tech admissions across government and private institutions, and conducts the state-wide CBT entrance examination for Lateral Entry B.Tech, B.Pharm, MCA, MBA, and Special OJEE.

Exam & Counselling Name Odisha Joint Entrance Examination — OJEE 2027 & Centralised Web Counselling
Conducting Authority Odisha Joint Entrance Examination Committee (OJEE Board) under the Skill Development and Technical Education (SDTE) Department & DTET, Government of Odisha
Official Portals Examination Portal: ojee.nic.in • Board Portal: odishajee.com
Admissions Pathway for B.Tech 1st Year B.Tech (4-Year Regular): 100% based on JEE Main 2027 CRL Rank through OJEE Web Counselling.
Lateral Entry B.Tech (2nd Year Direct): Based on OJEE 2027 CBT Examination conducted across Odisha.
Participating Colleges 10+ Government Universities & Colleges (OUTR Bhubaneswar, VSSUT Burla, IGIT Sarang, PMEC Berhampur, GCE Keonjhar, GCE Kalahandi) plus 65+ Top Private Universities and AICTE-Approved Engineering Colleges in Odisha.
Seat Reservation Structure 85% Odisha State Domicile Quota • 15% All India Quota (Open to all Indian citizens via JEE Main) • 5% Tuition Fee Waiver (TFW) Supernumerary Seats • 5% Green Card (GC) Quota • 30% Women Horizontal Reservation.
Counselling Registration Fee ₹450 (Non-refundable web counselling registration fee payable online on ojee.nic.in)
Seat Acceptance Fee (SAF) ₹10,000 for General / OBC / EWS candidates • ₹5,000 for SC, ST, and PC (Physically Challenged) candidates

🔄 Dual Pathway to B.Tech

Fresh 10+2 science graduates secure 1st year B.Tech seats exclusively using their JEE Main CRL rank. Diploma holders and B.Sc graduates appear for the OJEE CBT exam to secure 2nd-year Lateral Entry admission directly into engineering disciplines.

  • No separate state exam needed for 1st year B.Tech (JEE Main CRL used)
  • OJEE CBT specifically designed for Diploma & B.Sc Lateral Entry
  • Both streams participate through the unified ojee.nic.in counselling portal

⭐ Special OJEE (2nd OJEE)

To ensure no engineering seat remains unfilled, the OJEE Board conducts a dedicated Special OJEE (2nd OJEE) examination in June/July for non-JEE candidates seeking admission into vacant seats across Odisha colleges.

  • Separate computer-based test conducted across major state district centres
  • JEE Main candidates retain 1st merit priority over Special OJEE ranks
  • Enables lateral and spot-round entries for private & government colleges

🎓 100% Tuition Fee Waiver (TFW)

Under AICTE guidelines, 5% supernumerary seats in all approved disciplines in both government and private institutes offer complete tuition fee exemption to economically underprivileged, high-ranking state candidates.

  • Applicable to Odisha domicile candidates with family income < ₹8 Lakh/annum
  • Zero tuition fee payable for all 4 years of B.Tech study
  • Allotted purely on merit basis within the allocated TFW branch intake

OJEE 2027 Exam Pattern & Syllabus

Detailed test structure for OJEE Computer Based Tests (CBT) for Lateral Entry engineering aspirants and the JEE Main Paper-1 curriculum governing regular 1st year B.Tech state admissions.

💻 B.Tech Lateral Entry (Diploma)

Standard CBT for Polytechnic diploma graduates seeking 2nd-year direct B.Tech entry.

  • Total Questions: 120 Multiple Choice Questions
  • Total Marks: 480 Marks (4 marks per correct answer)
  • Exam Duration: 2 Hours (120 Minutes)
  • Negative Marking: -1 mark for each incorrect response
  • Subjects: Engineering Mathematics (40 Qs), Basic Electrical & Electronics (40 Qs), Engineering Mechanics (40 Qs)

🔬 B.Tech Lateral Entry (B.Sc)

Designed for B.Sc degree holders with Mathematics at 10+2 level seeking lateral entry.

  • Total Questions: 120 Multiple Choice Questions
  • Total Marks: 480 Marks
  • Exam Duration: 2 Hours (120 Minutes)
  • Negative Marking: -1 mark deduction per wrong answer
  • Subjects: Mathematics (60 Qs), Physics (30 Qs), Chemistry (30 Qs)

🎯 Special OJEE (2nd OJEE B.Tech)

Supplementary entrance examination for 1st-year vacant B.Tech seats across Odisha colleges.

  • Total Questions: 120 Questions (40 Physics, 40 Chem, 40 Math)
  • Total Marks: 480 Marks (CBT Mode in English)
  • Exam Duration: 2 Hours
  • Marking Scheme: +4 for Correct, -1 for Incorrect, 0 for Unattempted
  • Syllabus: CBSE / CHSE Odisha +2 Science Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics

OJEE Quotas, Reservations & Green Card Policy

Odisha operates a well-defined reservation matrix across its government unitary universities (VSSUT, OUTR), constituent colleges (IGIT, PMEC, GCEK), and private institutions under DTET guidelines.

📄 Green Card (GC) Verification Rule

The Green Card must be issued strictly in the name of the candidate's father or mother by the CDMO of the respective district. The candidate's name, date of birth, and parentage must match precisely across the Green Card, 10th admit card, and Aadhaar card.

💰 TFW Scheme Seat Mechanics

TFW seats are created over and above the sanctioned branch intake (e.g., 6 extra seats in a 120-seat CSE department). TFW candidates pay only university registration and exam fees; the complete tuition fee (ranging from ₹35,000 to ₹1,20,000/year) is 100% waived.

⚖️ Domicile Nativity Rule (Form-III)

Candidates claiming 85% state quota must secure the official Odisha Resident Certificate (Form-III) from the local Tahsildar. Temporary rental agreements or employment proofs of parents do NOT substitute for a statutory resident certificate.

Step-by-Step OJEE Counselling Roadmap

A comprehensive walk-through of the complete centralised admission workflow from registration to campus reporting.

1️⃣ Registration & Authentication

Visit ojee.nic.in, click on "B.Tech / Lateral Entry Counselling", and log in using your JEE Main Application Number or OJEE Application Number. Verify your profile, upload category details, and pay the ₹450 registration fee.

2️⃣ Choice Filling & Prioritization

Add college-branch pairs to your choice basket. Arrange high-demand government institutes (OUTR CSE, VSSUT CSE, IGIT) at the very top, followed by strong private universities (Silicon, CVRGU, ITER). There is no upper limit on the number of choices.

3️⃣ Mock Allotments & Locking

OJEE releases two mock allotments based on tentative submissions. Review where your rank lands, adjust your preference order if needed, and manually lock your choices using your password. Unlocked choices automatically lock at the deadline.

4️⃣ Provisional Allotment & SAF

When allotted a seat in Round 1, 2, or 3, you MUST pay the Seat Acceptance Fee (₹10,000 for General/OBC, ₹5,000 for SC/ST/PC) online. Failing to pay SAF cancels your allotted seat and disqualifies you from further regular rounds!

5️⃣ Document Verification & Queries

Upload scanned certificates on the portal. OJEE verifying officers review your documents. Check your candidate dashboard twice daily; any query raised regarding certificates must be answered within 24 hours.

6️⃣ Final Allotment & College Reporting

Download the Final Provisional Admission Letter. Report to your allotted institution on the designated reporting dates with original documents and balance college fees to finalize your B.Tech enrollment.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) — OJEE 2027

Expert answers to key queries regarding OJEE registration, JEE Main merit rank, counselling procedures, and seat rules.

Is OJEE examination mandatory for 1st-year B.Tech admissions in Odisha? +
No. Admissions to regular 1st year B.Tech in all government engineering colleges (like OUTR Bhubaneswar, VSSUT Burla, IGIT Sarang) and private universities across Odisha are conducted purely on the basis of JEE Main 2027 All India Rank (CRL) through OJEE Centralised Web Counselling. OJEE CBT is conducted only for Lateral Entry B.Tech (Diploma/B.Sc holders), B.Pharm, MCA, MBA, and Special OJEE (2nd OJEE) for vacant seats.
How is the 85% Odisha State Quota determined? +
85% of total seats in state engineering institutions are reserved for candidates who are permanent residents of Odisha. To claim this quota, candidates must produce an Odisha Resident / Nativity Certificate (Form-III) issued by a local Tahsildar or have passed their qualifying 10+2 / Diploma examination from an educational institution physically situated in Odisha.
Can Other State (Non-Odisha) candidates participate in OJEE Counselling? +
Yes. Non-Odisha candidates can participate under the 15% All India Quota (AIQ) in government universities and all open seats in private engineering colleges based on their JEE Main CRL rank. However, other state candidates are treated under the Open / General category and cannot claim state reservations like SC, ST, Green Card, or TFW.
What is the Green Card (GC) quota and what benefits does it offer? +
Odisha provides a 5% horizontal reservation across all categories for children of permanent Odisha resident parents who underwent family planning sterilization. Candidates must produce the official Green Card issued by the Chief District Medical Officer (CDMO). Because fewer aspirants hold this card, closing ranks for Green Card candidates are significantly relaxed compared to the General category.
What is the Tuition Fee Waiver (TFW) scheme and who qualifies? +
Under the AICTE TFW scheme, 5% supernumerary seats are created in each engineering department. Meritorious Odisha domicile candidates whose total family annual income from all sources is less than ₹8,00,000 are eligible. Candidates allotted a TFW seat receive a 100% waiver of tuition fees throughout their 4-year degree.
What happens if I don't pay the Seat Acceptance Fee (SAF)? +
If you are allotted a seat in any round of OJEE counselling and fail to pay the Seat Acceptance Fee (₹10,000 for General/OBC, ₹5,000 for SC/ST/PC) within the stipulated round deadline, your allotted seat is permanently cancelled and you are completely disqualified from participating in subsequent regular counselling rounds.
What is the difference between Float, Slide, and Freeze? +
Freeze: Accepts and locks your allotted seat and branch, exiting all future upgrade rounds.
Float: Accepts the allotted seat as a guaranteed fallback while remaining eligible for higher choices across ANY college in the next round.
Slide: Accepts the allotted seat while seeking an upgrade to higher choices strictly within the SAME college.
What is Special OJEE (2nd OJEE) and when is it conducted? +
Special OJEE (or 2nd OJEE) is conducted by the OJEE Board in June/July for vacant seats that remain unfilled after regular JEE Main counselling rounds. Aspirants who did not appear for JEE Main or wish to improve their options can appear. Regular JEE Main candidates always hold higher priority over Special OJEE rank holders.
Can Diploma holders change their branch in B.Tech Lateral Entry? +
Yes. Under current AICTE and SDTE Odisha guidelines, engineering diploma holders are eligible to choose any engineering branch in 2nd year B.Tech during choice filling, subject to college seat availability and meeting prerequisite bridge-course criteria.
Is the Seat Acceptance Fee refundable if I withdraw? +
Yes. Candidates who withdraw during the designated withdrawal window before the final seat allotment round receive a refund of their Seat Acceptance Fee after a nominal processing deduction of ₹1,000. Once the final round ends and classes commence, refunds follow institutional guidelines.
