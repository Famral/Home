Standard AICTE Eligibility Rules

  • Academic Qualification: Candidate must hold a Bachelor’s Degree in any discipline (B.A., B.Sc., B.Com., B.E./B.Tech, BBA, BCA, etc.) of minimum 3 years duration from a recognized university.
  • Minimum Aggregate Marks: At least 50% marks in aggregate (or equivalent CGPA). For candidates belonging to reserved categories (SC, ST, and PwD), the minimum qualifying mark is 45%.
  • Final Year Appearing Candidates: Students appearing in the final year of graduation examination whose results will be announced before commencement of 2027 admissions are fully eligible.
  • Age Limit: There is no upper age limit prescribed by NTA or AICTE to appear for CMAT 2027.
  • Nationality: Candidate must be an Indian citizen. Overseas candidates must fulfill individual B-School quota guidelines.

CMAT 2027 Admission & Selection Process

Understanding autonomous B-School admissions vs Maharashtra DTE Centralized Admission Process (CAP).

Pathway A: Autonomous AICTE B-Schools

GIM Goa, Great Lakes, BIMTECH, XIME, K J Somaiya
  1. Direct Institute Application: Apply individually on each institution's official website before their respective application deadlines (usually December 2026 – April 2027).
  2. Shortlisting: B-Schools shortlist candidates based on CMAT percentile, past academic record (10th, 12th, graduation), and diversity factors.
  3. GD / PI / WAT Evaluation: Shortlisted applicants attend Group Discussion (or Extempore), Written Ability Test, and Personal Interview.
  4. Composite Merit Offer: Final admission offers are rolled out based on composite scores: CMAT (30–50%), GD/PI/WAT (30–40%), Academic History & Work-Ex (15–20%).

Pathway B: Maharashtra DTE CAP Rounds

JBIMS Mumbai, SIMSREE, PUMBA Pune, SIESCOMS (MMS)
  1. Centralized Portal Registration: Register on the Maharashtra State CET Cell portal (cetcell.mahacet.org) for Centralized Admission Process (CAP).
  2. All India Quota Seat Pool (15%): Outside Maharashtra candidates compete for the prestigious 15% All India Quota seats in top institutes using CMAT scores.
  3. Merit Ranking & Option Form: DTE publishes provisional merit lists. Candidates submit institute and course priority preferences online.
  4. Centralized Seat Allotment: Allotment is purely merit-based with zero GD/PI rounds. Choose 'Freeze' to take admission or 'Float' to try for a higher preference in Round 2 & 3.

Step-by-Step CMAT 2027 Admission Roadmap

Your complete chronological blueprint from initial form filling to classroom enrollment.

Phase 1: Online Registration & Application Form Submission March – April 2027

Create your candidate profile at exams.nta.ac.in/CMAT using your DigiLocker or Aadhaar credentials. Complete personal, contact, and academic qualification details (10th, 12th, and Bachelor's degree). Select 4 test cities in order of preference, upload your scanned passport photo and signature, and pay the registration fee online (₹2,000 for General Male; ₹1,000 for Female/SC/ST/PwD/EWS/OBC-NCL).

Phase 2: City Intimation Slip & Admit Card Download May 2027

Check your advance exam city intimation slip to make travel arrangements. Download the official NTA CMAT 2027 Admit Card 3-4 days prior to the test. Verify shift timings (Morning: 9:00 AM – 12:00 PM or Afternoon: 3:00 PM – 6:00 PM), roll number, and exact exam lab address. Remember to affix your passport photograph and read exam instructions thoroughly.

Phase 3: Computer-Based Test (CBT) Examination Mid May 2027

Report at your allotted examination center at least 90 minutes before the test. Carry your printed Admit Card, one original government photo ID (Aadhaar, Passport, Voter ID, PAN), and transparent ballpoint pen. Attempt 100 questions across the 5 sections in 180 minutes. Manage time dynamically, avoiding reckless guessing in GK.

Phase 4: Answer Key Challenge & NTA Scorecard Publication Late May – Early June 2027

Access your recorded response sheet and compare with NTA’s provisional answer key. File challenges if any question or official key is ambiguous. Download your official CMAT 2027 Scorecard featuring section-wise raw scores, total marks out of 400, All India Rank (AIR), and official NTA Percentile Score.

Phase 5: B-School Applications & Centralized CAP Counselling June – July 2027

Shortlist target institutions based on your CMAT percentile. Apply individually to autonomous PGDM colleges (e.g. GIM Goa, Great Lakes, BIMTECH, XIME). Simultaneously, if aspiring for top Maharashtra colleges (JBIMS, SIMSREE, PUMBA), register for the DTE Maharashtra CAP rounds under the 15% All India Quota.

Phase 6: GD/PI/WAT, Document Verification & Seat Acceptance July – August 2027

Attend GD-PI-WAT rounds for PGDM colleges or freeze your CAP seat allotment for Mumbai/Pune MMS institutes. Undergo physical document verification with original marksheets, category documents, and CMAT scorecards. Pay the admission confirmation fees to secure your enrollment.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) — CMAT 2027

Clear, authoritative answers to the most common queries regarding NTA CMAT dates, JBIMS cutoffs, exam pattern, and counselling.

What is CMAT and who conducts the examination? +
The Common Management Admission Test (CMAT) is a premier national-level entrance exam conducted annually by the National Testing Agency (NTA) on behalf of AICTE and the Ministry of Education, Govt. of India. CMAT scores are accepted by more than 1,000 AICTE-approved management institutions, university departments, and top business schools nationwide for admission into MBA and PGDM programs.
What is the CMAT 2027 exam pattern and marking scheme? +
CMAT 2027 is a 3-hour (180 minutes) Computer-Based Test (CBT) consisting of 100 multiple-choice questions for a total of 400 marks. There are 5 mandatory sections with 20 questions (80 marks) each: (1) Quantitative Techniques & Data Interpretation, (2) Logical Reasoning, (3) Language Comprehension, (4) General Awareness, and (5) Innovation & Entrepreneurship. Candidates receive +4 marks for each correct response, with -1 mark deducted for every incorrect response.
Can non-Maharashtra students apply to JBIMS and SIMSREE through CMAT? +
Yes. Candidates from outside Maharashtra (All India candidates) can seek admission to JBIMS Mumbai, SIMSREE Mumbai, and PUMBA Pune through CMAT under the 15% All India Quota through the Centralized Admission Process (CAP) conducted by the Maharashtra State CET Cell (DTE). Non-Maharashtra candidates must register for MAH CET CAP counselling using their valid CMAT scorecard.
What CMAT score or percentile is needed for JBIMS Mumbai and SIMSREE? +
Jamnalal Bajaj Institute of Management Studies (JBIMS) Mumbai typically requires a 99.9+ percentile (raw score around 340+ out of 400) for its flagship MMS program under the All India quota. SIMSREE Mumbai generally closes between 99.80 and 99.90 percentile (raw score 325-338 out of 400).
Are final year graduation students eligible to appear for CMAT 2027? +
Yes. Candidates in the final year of their Bachelor's degree (10+2+3 pattern or equivalent in any discipline) whose results will be declared prior to the commencement of admission for the academic year 2027 are eligible to apply. They must secure a minimum of 50% aggregate marks (45% for SC/ST/PwD candidates) upon final degree completion.
Is there any sectional time limit or sectional cutoff in CMAT? +
No. CMAT does not impose any sectional time limits. Candidates are free to allocate the 180 minutes across all 5 sections in any order. Furthermore, NTA does not prescribe sectional cutoffs, although select autonomous B-Schools (such as GIM Goa) may review sectional performance or require minimum benchmark percentiles during their independent evaluation.
Is the Innovation & Entrepreneurship section compulsory in CMAT? +
Yes. The Innovation & Entrepreneurship section is completely mandatory and forms Section 5 of CMAT with 20 questions carrying 80 marks. Performance in this section is computed directly into the candidate's total raw score and All India Percentile.
How long is a CMAT score valid for MBA / PGDM admissions? +
CMAT scores are strictly valid for one academic year only (the upcoming 2027-2029 batch). A score obtained in CMAT 2027 cannot be carried forward to subsequent academic years.
