Assam CEE 2027 Overview & Key Highlights

The Assam Combined Entrance Examination (CEE) is the premier single-window state entrance examination for admission into 4-year undergraduate B.Tech degree programs across Assam's prestigious government engineering colleges and affiliated technical universities.

Exam Full Name Assam Combined Entrance Examination (Assam CEE 2027)
Conducting Authority Assam Science and Technology University (ASTU), Jalukbari, Guwahati
Counselling & Allotment Body Directorate of Technical Education (DTE), Assam, Kahilipara, Guwahati
Courses Offered 4-Year Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) & Bachelor of Engineering (B.E.)
Mode of Examination Offline Pen & Paper (OMR-Based Test)
Exam Medium Bilingual: English & Assamese
Number of Questions 120 MCQs (Mathematics: 40, Physics: 40, Chemistry: 40)
Total Marks & Scoring 480 Marks (+4 for correct response, -1 for incorrect response, 0 for unattempted)
Test Duration 3 Hours (180 Minutes) — 11:00 AM to 02:00 PM
Participating Government Colleges Assam Engineering College (AEC), Jorhat Engineering College (JEC), JIST Jorhat, BBEC Kokrajhar, BVEC Karimganj, Golaghat Engineering College (GEC), Dhemaji Engineering College (DEC), Bongaigaon Engineering College (BEC)

Assam Domicile & PRC Norms

Candidates must be Indian citizens and permanent residents of Assam. A valid Permanent Resident Certificate (PRC) issued by the Deputy Commissioner (DC) or Sub-Divisional Officer (Civil) is compulsory during counselling. Candidates whose parents are Assam state government employees or defense personnel stationed in Assam are granted specific relaxation.

🎓

Qualifying Academic Marks

Candidates must have passed 10+2 (AHSEC / CBSE / ICSE) in a single sitting with Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and English. General category candidates must score at least 45% aggregate in PCM, while reserved categories (SC, ST-P, ST-H, OBC/MOBC) require a minimum of 40% aggregate in PCM. English must be passed independently.

🎂

Age Limit & Relaxations

The applicant must not be below 17 years and not above 21 years of age on 1st August 2027. The maximum age limit is relaxed by 3 years (up to 24 years) for SC and ST candidates. For B.Sc. degree passed candidates, the upper age limit is relaxed up to 22 years.

ASTU conducts Assam CEE as a 480-mark offline pen-and-paper exam based on the latest Class 11 and Class 12 syllabi prescribed by the Assam Higher Secondary Education Council (AHSEC) and NCERT.

Comprehensive Topic-Wise Syllabus Breakdown (AHSEC & NCERT Alignment)

📐 Mathematics (160 Marks)

  • Sets, Relations & Functions: Domain, range, inverse functions, composite functions.
  • Algebra: Quadratic equations, Complex numbers, Binomial theorem, Sequences & series (AP, GP).
  • Matrices & Determinants: Inverse of matrices, Cramer's rule, consistency of linear equations.
  • Calculus: Limits, continuity, differentiability, maxima/minima, tangents/normals, definite & indefinite integrals, areas under curves, differential equations.
  • Coordinate & 3D Geometry: Straight lines, circles, parabola, ellipse, hyperbola, direction cosines, lines & planes.
  • Vectors & Probability: Dot & cross products, conditional probability, Bayes' theorem.

⚡ Physics (160 Marks)

  • Mechanics: Units & dimensional analysis, kinematics, laws of motion, friction, work, power & energy.
  • Rotational Motion & Gravitation: Moment of inertia, torque, Kepler's laws, escape velocity.
  • Properties of Matter: Elasticity, fluid pressure, viscosity, surface tension, heat & thermodynamics.
  • Electrodynamics: Coulomb's law, electric potential, capacitance, Ohm's law, Kirchhoff's laws, magnetic field, Biot-Savart, electromagnetic induction, AC circuits.
  • Optics: Reflection, refraction, lens formula, interference, diffraction, polarization.
  • Modern Physics: Photoelectric effect, Bohr's model, de Broglie wavelength, nuclear reactions, p-n junction diode.

🧪 Chemistry (160 Marks)

  • Physical Chemistry: Mole concept, atomic structure, states of matter, thermodynamics, chemical & ionic equilibrium, solutions, electrochemistry, chemical kinetics.
  • Inorganic Chemistry: Periodic classification, chemical bonding (VSEPR, hybridization), hydrogen, s-block, p-block elements, transition metals (d & f block), coordination compounds.
  • Organic Principles: Nomenclature (IUPAC), isomerism, inductive & resonance effects, electrophiles & nucleophiles.
  • Hydrocarbons & Functional Groups: Alkanes, alkenes, alkynes, haloalkanes, alcohols, phenols, ethers, aldehydes, ketones, carboxylic acids, amines.
  • Biomolecules: Carbohydrates, amino acids, proteins, polymers, chemistry in daily life.

⚖️ Official ASTU Tie-Breaking Rules for Merit Ranking

When two or more candidates secure identical total marks out of 480 in Assam CEE 2027, their inter-se merit rank is resolved strictly according to the following statutory hierarchy:

  1. Higher Marks in Mathematics: The candidate with higher raw marks in Mathematics is ranked higher.
  2. Higher Marks in Physics: If tie persists, higher marks in Physics determines the superior rank.
  3. Higher Marks in Chemistry: If still tied, higher marks in Chemistry is evaluated.
  4. Aggregate Marks in Qualifying 10+2 Examination: Higher aggregate percentage in PCM in the qualifying 10+2 board exam.
  5. Age Criterion (Seniority): If tie remains unresolved, the candidate older in age as per the HSLC/Class 10 certificate is placed higher.

DTE Assam Centralized Counselling Decision Simulator

The Directorate of Technical Education (DTE), Assam conducts centralized online admission counselling for all state government colleges. Master the choice locking rules, round progression, seat confirmation, and upgradation strategies.

🔒

Option A: Accept & Freeze

Completely satisfied with your allotted college and engineering branch. You confirm this allotment and formally exit all subsequent rounds.

Next Actions: Pay the provisional seat acceptance fee online, download Provisional Allotment Letter, and physically report to the college for document verification.
🔄

Option B: Accept & Float (Upgrade)

Retain your current seat as guaranteed security while actively seeking a higher-order college or branch preference in Round 2 or Round 3.

Upgradation Rule: If a higher preference is allotted in the subsequent round, your previous seat is automatically cancelled and reallocated. If no upgrade occurs, your current seat remains intact.
❌

Option C: Reject / Non-Reporting

Failing to pay the acceptance fee or failing to upload documents results in total seat forfeiture and exclusion from regular online rounds.

Caution: Candidates who forfeit seats can only re-enter counselling during the physical Institutional Spot Round subject to residual vacancies.

💡 Master Strategy: Golden Preference Order for Assam B.Tech Choice Filling

In DTE Assam web options filling, candidates can submit unlimited college-plus-branch preferences. Always organize choices strictly in order of true institutional quality, regardless of your rank:

1. Top Tier: Assam Engineering College (AEC), Guwahati → CSE → ETE → Electrical → Mechanical → Civil • Jorhat Engineering College (JEC), Jorhat → CSE → Electrical → Mechanical → Civil → Instrumentation.
2. Specialized & Emerging Govt Tech: Barak Valley Engineering College (BVEC) CSE • Dhemaji Engineering College (DEC) CSE • JIST Jorhat (ETE / PE&I) • AEC Chemical / Instrumentation / IPE.
3. Core Disciplines in Regional Colleges: Bineswar Brahma Engg. College (BBEC Kokrajhar) • Golaghat Engg. College (GEC) • Dhemaji Engg. College (Civil/Mech) • Bongaigaon Engg. College.
4. Supernumerary TFW: Always place TFW options for your dream branches directly above the non-TFW counterparts to claim 100% tuition waiver without risking seat loss.

Understanding the 5% Assam Government School Quota

Instituted to promote merit among rural and underprivileged students, this 5% reservation is reserved across branches for students who attended state government schools from Class 7 through Class 12 without interruption. Private school attendees, even under RTE, are ineligible for this specific quota.

North Eastern Neighboring States Quota in AEC & JEC

A specified pool of seats in Assam Engineering College (AEC) and Jorhat Engineering College (JEC) is historically reserved for official student nominees from neighboring North Eastern states (Nagaland, Meghalaya, Manipur, Mizoram, and Arunachal Pradesh) nominated directly by their state education directorates.

Step-by-Step Assam Engineering Admission Roadmap

Follow the sequential pathway from application form submission to final physical reporting at your allotted engineering college in Assam.

Step 1: ASTU CEE 2027 Online Application & Centre Selection

Register on astu.ac.in during March – April 2027. Provide accurate personal details, 10th and 12th board roll numbers, and reservation claims. Upload clear passport photograph and signature, select your preferred examination zone (Guwahati, Jorhat, Dibrugarh, Silchar, Tezpur, Bongaigaon, Nagaon, North Lakhimpur), and remit the examination fee online.

Step 2: Admit Card Download & Offline Pen-Paper Examination

Download the official admit card in May 2027. Arrive at your test center with printed admit card, valid photo identity proof (Aadhaar / Voter ID / Passport), and black ballpoint pens. Attempt 120 questions across Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry within the 3-hour duration.

Step 3: Answer Key Review & Official Rank Card Declaration

Verify your responses with the provisional answer key published by ASTU. Once final results are declared in June, download your rank card displaying your total marks out of 480, subject marks, and All-Assam State Merit Rank (SMR).

Step 4: DTE Assam Centralized Online Web Registration

Transition from ASTU to the Directorate of Technical Education (DTE) portal (dte.assam.gov.in / dte-assambtech.samarth.edu.in). Create your counselling login using your CEE Application Number. Upload your Permanent Resident Certificate (PRC), caste/category certificate, 10+2 marksheet, and submit unlimited college and branch choices before the choice locking window closes.

Step 5: Multi-Round Seat Allotment & Online Acceptance

DTE releases Round 1, Round 2, and Round 3 seat allotment results based on State Merit Rank and choice priorities. If allotted a seat, decide whether to Freeze (confirm seat) or Float (seek higher upgrade). Pay the provisional acceptance fee online to validate your admission.

Step 6: Document Verification & Physical Reporting

Report physically to the allotted engineering college with original documents, PRC, marksheets, medical fitness certificate, and passport photographs. Complete institutional fee payment and finalize enrollment.

Frequently Asked Questions (Assam CEE 2027)

Clear answers to the most common queries regarding Assam CEE exam dates, syllabus, PRC domicile rules, reservation entitlements, and college cutoffs.

What is Assam CEE and who is eligible to appear? +
Assam Combined Entrance Examination (CEE) is the centralized entrance test conducted annually by Assam Science and Technology University (ASTU) for admissions into 4-year B.Tech / B.E. programs across state government engineering colleges in Assam. To be eligible, candidates must be Indian citizens, permanent residents of Assam (possessing a valid PRC), aged between 17 and 21 years (with 3-year relaxation for SC/ST), and have passed 10+2 in a single sitting with Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and English with minimum 45% aggregate in PCM (40% for reserved categories).
Is Assam CEE conducted online (CBT) or offline? +
Assam CEE is conducted in offline pen-and-paper mode using OMR response sheets. Candidates must mark their answers using a black ballpoint pen. The exam comprises 120 multiple choice questions (40 in Mathematics, 40 in Physics, 40 in Chemistry) for a total of 480 marks over a 3-hour duration.
What is the negative marking policy in Assam CEE? +
Each correct response carries +4 marks. For each incorrect response, 1 mark is deducted (-1). Darkening more than one bubble for a question is treated as an incorrect response and penalized with -1 mark. Unattempted questions receive 0 marks.
Is Permanent Resident Certificate (PRC) compulsory for admission? +
Yes. A valid Permanent Resident Certificate (PRC) of Assam issued by the competent revenue authority (Deputy Commissioner or Sub-Divisional Officer) is strictly mandatory for admission into all 100% state quota seats in government engineering colleges (AEC, JEC, JIST, BBEC, BVEC, GEC, DEC, BEC). Without an Assam PRC, a candidate cannot be admitted to state quota seats regardless of their entrance rank.
What is the difference between ASTU and DTE Assam in the admission process? +
ASTU (Assam Science and Technology University) is strictly responsible for conducting the entrance examination — releasing notifications, accepting application forms, issuing admit cards, conducting the test, and declaring rank cards. Once ranks are published, the entire counselling, choice filling, document verification, and seat allocation process is conducted independently by the Directorate of Technical Education (DTE), Assam.
What rank in Assam CEE guarantees CSE at AEC Guwahati or JEC Jorhat? +
For General (Unreserved) candidates, Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) at Assam Engineering College (AEC), Guwahati typically closes around Rank 1 – 220. At Jorhat Engineering College (JEC), Jorhat, CSE closes between Rank 180 and 420. For OBC/MOBC candidates, closing ranks are approximately 380 (AEC) and 650 (JEC). For SC and ST categories, closing ranks range between 900 and 1,750.
Who qualifies for the 5% Assam Government School Quota? +
The 5% government school quota is reserved exclusively for candidates who have studied continuously from Class 7 to Class 12 in state government-run schools of Assam. Candidates must submit a dedicated certificate from their school principal countersigned by the District Inspector of Schools or District Elementary Education Officer. Students from private schools, KV, Navodaya, or CBSE institutions are not eligible for this reservation.
What is the Tuition Fee Waiver (TFW) Scheme in Assam CEE? +
The Tuition Fee Waiver (TFW) scheme provides 5% supernumerary seats in every branch across all participating engineering colleges. Eligible candidates must have a valid Assam PRC and an annual parental income from all sources strictly below ₹8,00,000 certified by the Revenue Circle Officer. Candidates admitted under TFW pay zero tuition fees for all 4 years of engineering.
Can other state candidates get admission into Assam Engineering College (AEC)? +
Candidates from other states cannot directly claim state quota seats in AEC or JEC through Assam CEE. However, candidates from neighboring North Eastern states (Meghalaya, Nagaland, Manipur, Mizoram, Arunachal Pradesh) that historically lack sufficient engineering seats can apply for designated nominee quota seats through their respective state education directorates. Other state candidates can also seek admission into private technical universities in Assam like GCU, Royal Global, and Don Bosco University.
What happens if I get allotted a seat in Round 1 of DTE counselling? +
If allotted a seat in Round 1, you must log in to the DTE counselling portal and choose either Accept & Freeze (finalize this seat and exit future rounds) or Accept & Float (retain this seat while competing for a higher preference in Round 2/3). In both cases, paying the online provisional seat acceptance fee is mandatory. Failing to pay the fee or failing to report within the prescribed window leads to automatic cancellation of your allotment.
