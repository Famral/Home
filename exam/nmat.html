GMAC & AICTE Core Eligibility Rules

  • Academic Qualification: Bachelor's Degree (minimum 3 years duration) in any discipline from a recognized University or AICTE-approved institution.
  • Minimum Aggregate Marks: Minimum 50% marks or equivalent CGPA in graduation.
  • Final Year Undergraduates: Students in the final year of bachelor's degree are completely eligible to register and sit for NMAT.
  • No Age Restrictions: GMAC does not prescribe any upper or lower age boundary for taking NMAT.
  • No Attempt Age Cap: Professionals with any years of work experience or career gaps can take NMAT.

Strict NMIMS Mumbai Institutional Guidelines

SVKM's NMIMS mandates a full-time Bachelor's degree with 50%+ marks in the first attempt. Candidates who passed graduation via part-time, correspondence, or with pending backlogs are ineligible. Furthermore, NMIMS considers only your first NMAT test attempt.

Language Skills

36 Qs • 28 Mins
  • Reading Comprehension: 2 to 3 passages (400-500 words each), testing direct inferences, central theme, title, and tone.
  • Grammar & Syntax: Identifying sentence errors, subject-verb agreement, modifiers, tenses, and parallelism.
  • Vocabulary in Context: Synonyms, Antonyms, Analogies, and Contextual word meanings.
  • Sentence Completion: Single and double blank cloze tests focusing on prepositions and idioms.
  • Para Jumbles: Ordering 4-5 jumbled sentences into coherent logical paragraphs.

Quantitative Skills

36 Qs • 52 Mins
  • Arithmetic (Core Weightage): Percentages, Profit & Loss, Ratio & Proportion, Time-Speed-Distance, Work-Time, Simple & Compound Interest.
  • Algebra & Numbers: Linear & Quadratic Equations, Inequalities, Logarithms, Number System properties.
  • Modern Mathematics: Permutations & Combinations, Probability distributions, Set Theory.
  • Geometry & Mensuration: Triangles, Circles, Polygons, Coordinate Geometry basics.
  • Data Interpretation: Bar Charts, Line Graphs, Tables, Pie Charts, and Data Sufficiency (2 statements).

Logical Reasoning

36 Qs • 40 Mins
  • Critical Reasoning (Heavy Weightage): Statement & Assumptions, Strengthening/Weakening Arguments, Course of Action, Inferences.
  • Analytical Puzzles: Linear & Circular Seating Arrangements, Matrix matchings, Ranking & Ordering.
  • Deductive Logic: Syllogisms, Truth-tellers & Liars, Venn Diagram logic.
  • Relational & Sequence Logic: Blood Relations, Direction Sense, Coding-Decoding, Alphanumeric Series.
  • Input-Output: Machine arrangement sequences and step-by-step pattern logic.

NMAT Adaptive Algorithm Pacing Strategy: Never Leave Questions Blank

Because NMAT is a computer-adaptive test with zero negative marking, the algorithm dynamically adjusts subsequent question difficulty based on your answers. If a sectional timer is about to expire, make educated guesses on all remaining questions before the clock hits zero. Leaving questions unattempted severely penalizes your final scaled score.

NMAT 2027 Admission & Selection Process

Understanding the two-stage admission funnel: NMAT CBT Exam followed by CD/PI/WAT or Case Discussion rounds.

Pathway A: SVKM's NMIMS Admission Process

Mumbai, Bengaluru, Navi Mumbai, Hyderabad, Indore
  1. Mandatory Dual Registration: Register on both GMAC portal (mba.com) and NMIMS portal (nmims.edu) before their individual deadlines.
  2. Score Cutoff Screening: NMIMS shortlists applicants based on 1st Attempt overall score (232+) AND sectional benchmarks.
  3. Stage 2 Evaluation (CD & PI / Watson Glaser): Shortlisted candidates undergo Written Ability Test (WAT) / Watson Glaser Critical Thinking appraisal and Personal Interview (PI) or Case Discussion.
  4. Final Composite Merit List: Offers rolled out based on: NMAT Score (70%), WAT/PI (20%), Work Experience & Academic Records (10%).

Pathway B: Other 50+ Participating B-Schools

K J Somaiya, XIMB, TAPMI, SDA Bocconi, Great Lakes
  1. Score Dispatch: Select up to 5 institutes free during GMAC registration or send additional score reports at ₹400/school.
  2. Best of 3 Attempts Considered: Unlike NMIMS, institutes like K J Somaiya, TAPMI, and XIMB evaluate the highest score across all attempts taken.
  3. Independent Application Forms: Fill individual institute application forms on their respective college portals before deadlines.
  4. GD / PI / Case Analysis: Attend institute-level selection rounds. Final offers reflect composite weightage of NMAT score and interview performance.

Step-by-Step NMAT 2027 Admission Roadmap

Six essential milestones from online registration to securing your MBA seat at NMIMS or top partner B-schools.

Step 1: GMAC & NMIMS Portal Registration August – October 2026

Create an account on mba.com/exams/nmat. Complete personal, contact, and academic sections. Select 5 free business school recipients. Crucial: Also register on nmims.edu if targeting SVKM's NMIMS campuses before their individual registration window closes.

Step 2: Pay Fees & Schedule Your Test Slot August – October 2026

Pay the test registration fee (₹3,000 + taxes). Choose your preferred exam delivery mode: Test Center based (at 75+ Pearson VUE centers) or Online Proctored at home. Select your specific exam date and time slot across the 75-day test window.

Step 3: Appear for the 120-Minute Computer-Adaptive Test October – December 2026

Carry two valid photo IDs and printed Admit Card to the center. Select your preferred section order before beginning. Answer 108 questions across Language (28m), Quants (52m), and Logic (40m). Preview your unofficial scaled score on screen immediately upon submission.

Step 4: Download Official Scorecard & Evaluate Retake Within 48–72 Hours

Access your official scaled scorecard (36 to 360) on your mba.com dashboard. If your score is below expectations, book a retake (up to 2 retakes allowed with a mandatory 15-day gap) for colleges like K J Somaiya, XIMB, and TAPMI.

Step 5: Shortlisting & Stage 2 Selection (CD / PI / WAT) January – February 2027

Participating B-Schools announce their cutoff lists. Shortlisted candidates attend Case Discussion, Personal Interview, Written Ability Test, or Watson-Glaser Critical Thinking assessments at designated campuses.

Step 6: Final Composite Merit List & Seat Confirmation March – April 2027

Institutes release final merit rankings based on NMAT scaled scores, interview performance, academic background, and work experience. Selected candidates verify original documents and pay the tuition deposit to confirm MBA admission.
