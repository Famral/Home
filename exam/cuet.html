CUET UG 2027 Examination & Admission Overview

Administered by the National Testing Agency (NTA) under the Ministry of Education, CUET UG is India's single largest undergraduate admission gateway, democratizing higher education entrance across Central, State, and leading Private Universities.

Full Examination Name Common University Entrance Test [CUET (UG) - 2027]
Conducting Authority National Testing Agency (NTA) on behalf of UGC & Ministry of Education
Official Examination Portals Portal: exams.nta.ac.in/CUET-UG/ & cuetug.ntaonline.in
Frequency & Academic Cycle Annual examination for Undergraduate Admissions (Academic Session 2027–2028)
Examination Delivery Mode Hybrid Mode: Pen & Paper (OMR-based) for high-registration domain subjects and Computer-Based Test (CBT) for other specialized disciplines & optional languages
Test Mediums / Languages 13 Languages: English, Hindi, Assamese, Bengali, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Odia, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu, and Urdu
Test Components / Sections Section IA & IB: 33 Languages (40 Qs to attempt out of 50)
Section II: 29 Domain Specific Subjects based on Class 12 NCERT (40 Qs out of 50)
Section III: General Test for multidisciplinary & vocational courses (50 Qs out of 60)
Marking Scheme +5 Marks for each correct answer; -1 Mark for each wrong response; 0 Marks for unattempted questions
Max Test Papers Permitted Up to 6 test papers across Languages, Domain Subjects, and General Test
Participating Institutions 250+ Universities: 45 Central Universities (DU, BHU, JNU, Jamia, AU, BBAU, Visva-Bharati, etc.), 40+ State Universities, 30+ Deemed Universities, and 140+ Private Universities
Degrees & Courses Offered B.A. (Hons), B.Com (Hons), B.Sc (Hons), B.Tech (CIC DU, BHU, AU, CUH), BBA, BMS, B.Voc, BCA, 5-Year Integrated BA LLB / BBA LLB, B.Pharm, and Integrated M.Sc

⚖️ Elimination of Board Bias

Historically, students from state boards faced massive disadvantages against students scoring 99%+ in liberal-marking CBSE/ISC boards. CUET standardizes assessment across India on a uniform national scale.

🌐 Single Unified Gateway

Instead of buying dozens of individual university application forms and traveling across cities, students write one standardized test and apply to 250+ top universities with their NTA scores.

📖 100% Class 12 NCERT Based

Section 2 domain tests strictly adhere to the Class 12 NCERT syllabus. No Class 11 questions are included, allowing students to prepare seamlessly alongside their board examinations.

CUET UG 2027 Eligibility & Class 12 Domain Mapping Rules

Understanding minimum educational qualifications, age parameters, and the critically important subject mapping regulations enforced by top institutions like Delhi University.

🎓 Academic Qualifications

Candidates must have passed Class 12 or equivalent examination from a recognized Board (CBSE, CISCE, State Boards, NIOS). Candidates appearing in Class 12 in 2027 are fully eligible to apply on a provisional basis.

🎂 Age Criteria

NTA does not specify any minimum or upper age limit for appearing in CUET UG. However, candidates must fulfill specific age limits stipulated by individual universities or regulatory councils (e.g., BCI for Law).

⚠️ Critical Golden Rule: Delhi University (DU) Class 12 Subject Mapping Delhi University mandates that candidates MUST appear in CUET UG only in those domain-specific subjects that they have studied and passed in Class 12. If you appear in a subject you did not have in Class 12, DU CSAS will reject that score during merit computation!

CUET UG 2027 Revised Exam Pattern & Marking Scheme

Technical breakdown of Sections 1A, 1B, 2, and 3, including question counts, time durations, paper limits, and marking criteria.

Section Subject Coverage Total Questions Questions to Attempt Duration Max Marks
Section IA 13 Modern Indian Languages (English, Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, etc.) 50 MCQs 40 MCQs 45 Minutes 200 Marks
Section IB 20 Optional Languages (French, Spanish, German, Sanskrit, Arabic, etc.) 50 MCQs 40 MCQs 45 Minutes 200 Marks
Section II 29 Domain Subjects (Class 12 NCERT curriculum) 50 MCQs 40 MCQs 45 Mins (60 Mins for Quantitative Subjects*) 200 Marks each
Section III General Test (GK, Current Affairs, Mental Ability, Numerical & Reasoning) 60 MCQs 50 MCQs 60 Minutes 250 Marks
*60-Minute Extended Duration Domain Subjects: To account for extensive problem-solving calculations, NTA grants a 60-minute duration (instead of 45 minutes) for: Mathematics / Applied Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Accountancy, Economics, and Computer Science / Informatics Practices.

✅ Correct Response (+5)

Each question answered correctly awards exactly +5 marks towards the aggregate score for that specific test paper.

❌ Incorrect Response (-1)

A penalty of -1 mark is deducted for each incorrect answer chosen. Guessing without elimination can severely impact percentiles.

⚪ Unattempted (0)

Unattempted or flagged questions incur zero penalty (0 marks). If a question is dropped by NTA, +5 is awarded to all candidates who attempted it.

DU CSAS & Central University Admission Roadmap

Master the step-by-step seat allocation workflow: how Delhi University’s Common Seat Allocation System (CSAS UG) operates across its 3 distinct phases.

1️⃣ Phase 1: Registration

Apply on ugadmission.uod.ac.in using your CUET application number. Enter personal profile details, upload category/caste certificates, and submit Class 12 board marksheet details.

2️⃣ Phase 2: Preference Filling

Map your Class 12 subjects to corresponding CUET test papers. Select and arrange Program + College combinations (e.g. SRCC B.Com Hons, Hindu Eco Hons) in strict priority order.

3️⃣ Phase 3: Seat Allocation

DU releases simulated ranks allowing 48 hours for preference re-ordering. Official Round 1, Round 2, and Round 3 allocations occur based purely on merit and preference list rank.

Mandatory 'Accept' Action If allotted a seat in any round, you MUST click 'Accept Allocation' within 48 to 72 hours. Failing to accept results in permanent expulsion from all subsequent CSAS counselling rounds!
Document Verification The allotted college's admission committee reviews uploaded certificates and marks online. Once approved, you pay the online admission fee on the dashboard.
Freeze vs Upgrade Decision Upgrade: Keeps your allotted seat secure while allowing you to compete for higher preferences in subsequent rounds.
Freeze: Confirms your currently allotted seat permanently and withdraws you from further upgrade rounds.
Mid-Entry Provision DU provides a mid-entry window (usually in Round 3) allowing candidates who failed to register or complete Phase 1/2 to enter the counselling process upon paying a ₹1,000 non-refundable fee.
Spot Admission Rounds Conducted for vacant seats after regular rounds. Candidates with no confirmed seat can participate. In spot rounds, candidates cannot upgrade; the seat allotted is final.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) — CUET UG 2027

Authoritative answers to the most crucial queries regarding NTA application, subject selection, DU CSAS counselling, cutoffs, and normalization.

CUET UG (Common University Entrance Test - Undergraduate) is a nationwide standardized entrance examination conducted annually by the National Testing Agency (NTA) under the Ministry of Education and UGC. It provides a single-window application and testing platform for undergraduate admissions across 250+ Central, State, Deemed, and Private Universities in India, including University of Delhi (DU), BHU, JNU, Jamia, and University of Allahabad.
Delhi University enforces a strict rule: a candidate must appear in CUET UG only in those domain-specific subjects that they have studied and passed in their Class 12 board examination. If you choose and appear in a domain subject that was not on your Class 12 marksheet, DU CSAS will reject that score during merit computation. For subjects not directly offered in CUET (e.g. Biotechnology), students must select the nearest related subject (e.g. Biology) permitted by DU guidelines.
Candidates can select up to a maximum of 6 test papers across all three sections. Recommended combinations include 1 Language + 4 Domain Subjects + 1 General Test, or 2 Languages + 3 Domain Subjects + 1 General Test. This choice covers the eligibility prerequisites of over 95% of undergraduate degree courses nationwide.

The syllabus for all 29 Section 2 Domain-Specific subjects is strictly mapped to the Class 12 NCERT curriculum. Class 11 chapters are not tested. Candidates can download detailed, unit-wise syllabus PDFs for each subject directly from exams.nta.ac.in/CUET-UG/.
NTA conducts CUET UG in hybrid format: High-registration core domain subjects (such as Chemistry, Physics, Mathematics, Biology, English, and General Test) are conducted in Pen & Paper (OMR-based) mode in a single nationwide shift, eliminating the need for normalization. Specialized domain subjects and foreign languages with lower applicant volumes are administered via Computer-Based Test (CBT).
For Central Universities, candidates claiming OBC-NCL or Gen-EWS reservation must provide certificates issued on or after April 1, 2026 (for the financial year 2026–2027) strictly in the Central Government format. State-level certificates or certificates issued prior to April 1, 2026 are not accepted during university document verification.

You would only be able to re-enter during the designated Mid-Entry window by paying a ₹1,000 penalty fee.
Supernumerary quotas are extra seats created over and above the sanctioned strength for specific candidate categories. These include: Sports Quota (5% per college), Extra-Curricular Activities (ECA), Armed Forces personnel children/widows (CW Quota), Prime Minister's Special Scholarship Scheme (PMSSS for J&K students), Kashmiri Migrants, Single Girl Child quota (1 seat per program in each college), and Orphan quota (1 male and 1 female per college with 100% fee waiver).
