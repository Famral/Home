🚀 Pathway 1: B.Tech 1st Year Admissions via UKTech

In accordance with Uttarakhand Department of Technical Education norms, admission to first-year B.Tech seats in all state government institutions (GBPIET, BTKIT, THDC-IHET, IT Gopeshwar, SIT, WIT) does not require a separate state entrance test. Candidates are admitted via centralized online counselling based strictly on their JEE Main 2027 All India CRL Rank.

  • ✓ Eligibility: 10+2 with Physics, Mathematics & Chem/CS with minimum 45% marks (40% for SC/ST).
  • ✓ Merit Metric: JEE Main CRL Rank mapped to Uttarakhand State Merit List.
  • ✓ Counselling: 3 Online Rounds followed by Institute-Level Spot Round.
⚡ Pathway 2: B.Tech Lateral Entry via UKSEE Exam

Diploma holders in engineering or B.Sc graduates seeking direct admission into the 2nd year (3rd semester) of B.Tech must appear for the official UKSEE Lateral Entry Entrance Test conducted directly by VMSB UTU.

  • ✓ Eligibility: 3-Year AICTE Diploma in Engineering or B.Sc (with Math) with ≥ 45% (40% SC/ST).
  • ✓ Exam Pattern: 100 MCQs (100 Marks, 2 Hours duration, No Negative Marking).
  • ✓ Seat Matrix: 10% supernumerary seats over sanctioned intake in all branches.

Matrices & Determinants, Differential Calculus, Integral Calculus, Differential Equations of First & Second Order, Vector Calculus, Partial Differentiation, Fourier Series, and Probability Distributions.

Round Decision Simulator: What Should You Do When Allotted a Seat?

Select your current seat allotment situation to see the recommended strategic decision, financial obligations, and upgrade risks:

FREEZE Seat Lock

Choose Freeze if you are completely satisfied with the allotted college and engineering branch. You pay the provisional seat confirmation fee, download your Provisional Admission Letter, and exit subsequent rounds.

FLOAT College Upgrade

Choose Float to retain your current allotment as a confirmed backup while participating in subsequent rounds to compete for higher-preference colleges and branches. If upgraded, your earlier seat is automatically vacated.

SLIDE Branch Upgrade

Choose Slide if you love the institution (e.g., GBPIET Pauri) but wish to upgrade to a higher-ranked engineering discipline within the same college (e.g., ECE to CSE/AI).

SURRENDER Exit / Spot

Surrendering a seat forfeits your claim over the allotted institution. You can either withdraw with applicable refund rules or register afresh for the physical Institute Spot Mop-Up Round at VMSB UTU / GBPIET.

The Government of Uttarakhand enforces a structured statutory reservation framework ensuring equitable access to technical education across vertical categories, hill communities, and women applicants.

Important Domicile Clarification (Home State vs All India)

• 85% Home State Quota: Strictly reserved for candidates holding an authentic Uttarakhand Permanent Resident Certificate (PRC / Domicile). Children of regular employees of the Uttarakhand Government, or Central Government / Defense personnel posted in Uttarakhand at the time of admission, also qualify under specific state provisions.
• 15% All India Quota: Candidates who completed 10+2 outside Uttarakhand and do not possess PRC can compete under the 15% All India Quota in participating government colleges and open seats in private engineering institutions without domicile restrictions.

Step-by-Step UKTech 2027 Admission Roadmap

A comprehensive, chronological visual roadmap guiding candidates through every phase from entrance qualification to campus commencement.

Stage 01

Entrance Test & Rank Acquisition

Appear for JEE Main 2027 (for 1st Year B.Tech) or UKSEE 2027 (for Lateral Entry / B.Pharm). Secure your scorecard indicating your All India CRL Rank or State Merit Rank.

Stage 02

UKTech Portal Registration & Fee

Register on uktech.ac.in. Enter personal, category, and domicile credentials. Upload required scanned certificates and pay the non-refundable online counselling fee (₹2,000).

Stage 03

Web Choice Filling & Locking

Browse the institutional seat matrix. Arrange colleges (GBPIET, BTKIT, THDC, IT Gopeshwar, WIT, COER) and branches in descending order of true preference. Lock choices before the cutoff.

Stage 04

Seat Allotment & Decision Matrix

Check allotment results using your roll number. Exercise your choice: Freeze (confirm seat) or Float/Slide (upgrade in next round). Pay the seat confirmation fee online.

Stage 05

Document Verification & Reporting

Download the Provisional Allotment Letter. Report physically to the allotted engineering college with original documents, photocopies, passport photos, and medical certificate.

Stage 06

Institutional Fee & Orientation

Deposit the balance college tuition and hostel charges, complete biometric registration, obtain your college identity card, and attend the academic orientation program.
