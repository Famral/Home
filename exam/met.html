MAHE Institution of Eminence

MIT Manipal was established in 1957 as one of India's earliest private engineering institutions. Today, MAHE holds the prestigious Institution of Eminence (IoE) status, ensuring autonomous updated curriculum, world-class labs, and globally valid degree credentials.

Zero Negative Marking on NATs

Unlike conventional engineering tests with blanket penalties, MET features 15 Numerical Answer Type (NAT) questions (5 each in Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry) with zero negative marking, rewarding calculation accuracy without penalty.

Best-of-Two Phase Policy

Candidates who register for both Phase 1 and Phase 2 benefit from MAHE's candidate-friendly rule: the system automatically adopts the higher of the two examination marks for rank compilation.

MET 2027 Eligibility Criteria & Interactive Evaluator

Verify academic prerequisites, compulsory subjects, board percentage benchmarks, and age regulations mandated by MAHE for B.Tech admission across all campuses.

Qualifying Examination

Passed 10+2, A-Level, IB, American 12th grade, or any equivalent examination recognized by the Association of Indian Universities (AIU).

  • Compulsory subjects: Physics, Mathematics, and English
  • Optional subject (any one): Chemistry, Biotechnology, Biology, Computer Science or any vocational subject

Minimum Aggregate Marks

Candidates must have secured a minimum of 50% aggregate marks in Physics, Mathematics, and the chosen optional subject taken together in their Class 12 (or equivalent) qualifying exam.

  • Must pass English as a separate qualifying subject
  • Board marks directly impact 50% of the final merit rank

Age & Nationality Rules

There is no minimum or upper age restriction prescribed by MAHE for appearing in MET 2027 or participating in B.Tech counselling.

  • Open to Indian Citizens and Non-Resident Indians (NRI)
  • Separate NRI quota seats available with direct sponsorship

The MET 50:50 Band System & Normalization Explained

How MAHE combines your MET entrance exam marks with your Class 12 board marks to compile the official All India Merit Rank list.

The Mathematical Composite Formula

Unlike exams that rely solely on entrance scores, MAHE implements an institutional 50:50 composite normalization system. The final ranking index (out of 100 points) is the direct arithmetic sum of two components:

Composite Score (out of 100) = [ (MET Exam Marks / 240) × 50 ] + [ Board Band Score (out of 50) ]

Board marks are computed across 4 subjects: Physics, Mathematics, English, and one optional subject (Chemistry, Computer Science, Biology, or Electronics). The candidate's aggregate percentage is mapped into a discrete Band System.

Centralized B.Tech Common Counselling Roadmap (Rounds 1 to 5)

Step-by-step navigation of MAHE's online counselling portal: registration, choice preference submission, seat allotment, fee installments, sliding, and spot admission rules.

Step 1: Counselling Registration & ₹10,000 Waitlist Deposit

Candidates who have qualified with a valid MET 2027 rank must log in to the official counselling portal (counseling.manipal.edu). A non-refundable registration fee or refundable waitlist deposit of ₹10,000 is required to unlock option filling. This amount is fully adjusted toward your first installment course fee upon accepting a seat.

Step 2: Choice Filling & Preference Ordering

Candidates can fill an unlimited number of campus and branch combinations across MIT Manipal, MIT Bengaluru, MU Jaipur, and SMIT Sikkim. It is crucial to arrange preferences in true descending order of priority, as higher choices are automatically upgraded during subsequent rounds.

Step 3: Round 1 Seat Allotment & First Fee Installment

Seats are allotted based on MET Rank, reservation category, and candidate choices. If allotted a seat, you must pay the 1st installment of course fees (typically ~₹1,65,000 to ₹2,00,000) within the stipulated 3 to 4 days window to confirm the provisional seat and remain eligible for higher-preference upgrades in Round 2.

Step 4: Subsequent Counselling Rounds (Rounds 2 to 5)

Candidates who did not receive their top choice in Round 1 are automatically considered for upward sliding in Rounds 2, 3, 4, and 5 without any penalty. Fresh choice modification is permitted prior to each round's seat allotment freeze.

Step 5: Intra-Institute Sliding & Spot Round

Conducted physically or online right before the orientation week. Admitted students within a specific campus (e.g. MIT Manipal) can slide into vacant computer science or core branches before seats are surrendered to the final spot round.

MET 2027 Scholarships & Tuition Fee Concessions

MAHE awards over ₹15 Crores annually in financial aid, merit-based freeships, and fee concessions to high-performing MET rank holders.

Kalam-Pai Freeship (Top 100 Ranks)

Awarded to candidates securing an All India MET Rank between 1 and 100. Provides a 100% waiver of tuition fee for all four years of B.Tech study at MIT Manipal or MIT Bengaluru, subject to maintaining minimum CGPA criteria.

Scholar Scholarships (Ranks 101 to 5,000)

Candidates with ranks ranging from 101 to 5,000 are eligible for substantial course fee concessions ranging between 20% and 50% across selected core and computing disciplines, substantially reducing net tuition expense.

Merit-Cum-Means Scholarship

Designed for meritorious candidates whose total parental annual income from all sources is less than ₹8 Lakhs per annum. Up to 75% tuition fee waiver depending on candidate merit and financial assessment.

Academy of General Education (AGE)

Special scholarship quotas for wards of MAHE staff, alumni children, and Konkani linguistic minority applicants, granting 10% to 25% recurring academic fee concessions throughout the degree program.
