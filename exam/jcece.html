JCECE 2027 Overview & Key Highlights

The Jharkhand Combined Entrance Competitive Examination Board (JCECEB) is the statutory examining authority constituted by the Government of Jharkhand. JCECEB governs admissions into engineering (B.Tech), agriculture, horticulture, forestry, veterinary, fishery, and lateral entry technical degree courses across state universities and private engineering colleges.

Exam & Board Name Jharkhand Combined Entrance Competitive Examination Board (JCECEB) — JCECE 2027
Conducting Authority Government of Jharkhand • Higher and Technical Education Department
Official Portals Official Board: jceceb.jharkhand.gov.in • Centralized Counselling Portal: counseling.jceceb.org.in
B.Tech 1st Year Admission Mode No Separate State Entrance Exam — 100% Merit evaluated through JEE Main 2027 All India CRL Rank. JCECEB prepares the official Jharkhand State Merit List (SML).
Entrance Exam Streams (Conducted by JCECEB) 1. JCECE Agriculture & Allied: B.Sc (Hons) Agriculture, B.Sc Forestry, B.Sc Horticulture, B.V.Sc & A.H., B.F.Sc, B.Tech Dairy Technology, B.Tech Agricultural Engineering.
2. B.Tech Lateral Entry (DECE LE): Direct admission into 2nd year B.Tech (3rd Semester) for Diploma / B.Sc holders.
Premier Institutes BIT Sindri (Birsa Institute of Technology, Dhanbad), UCET Hazaribagh (VBU), Government Engineering College Ramgarh, Government Engineering College Dumka, Government Engineering College Chaibasa, Cambridge Institute of Technology (CIT) Ranchi, RTC Institute of Technology Ranchi, BAU Ranchi (Birsa Agricultural University).
Domicile & Quota Policy 100% Seats in Government Engineering Colleges (BIT Sindri, UCET) reserved strictly for Local / Permanent Residents of Jharkhand. 15% to 25% Open / Management seats available in select private institutions for all-India candidates.
Key Reservation Categories UR (40%), BC-I (8%), BC-II (6%), SC (10%), ST (26%), EWS (10%), TFW (5% Supernumerary Tuition Fee Waiver), PwD (5% Horizontal), SMQ (Servicemen Quota).
Counselling Architecture Online Centralised Web Counselling — Round 1, Round 2, Round 3, followed by Open / Mop-Up Spot Counselling for residual vacant seats. Seat acceptance fee: ₹1,000.

JCECE 2027 Pattern & State Merit List Mechanics

Understanding Jharkhand's dual-pathway admission structure: JEE Main All India CRL Rank evaluation for 1st Year B.Tech versus the offline/CBT JCECE entrance exam for Agriculture, Forestry, Veterinary, and Lateral Entry streams.

Pathway 1: B.Tech 1st Year (via JEE Main CRL)

JEE Main Merit

JCECEB does not conduct an engineering entrance paper. Instead, it invites online registration from candidates who have appeared in JEE Main 2027 (Session 1 or 2).

  • • Rank Parameter: All India Common Rank List (CRL) Rank is the primary ranking criterion. Category ranks from JEE Main are not used directly; JCECEB assigns its own State Category Ranks based on Jharkhand reservation rules.
  • • Tie-Breaking Rules: (1) Higher NTA score in Mathematics, (2) Higher NTA score in Physics, (3) Higher NTA score in Chemistry, (4) Older in age as per 10th certificate, (5) Alphabetical order of candidate name.
  • • State Merit List (SML): JCECEB issues a combined State CRL Rank and individual State Category Ranks (BC-I, BC-II, SC, ST, EWS).

Pathway 2: JCECE Agriculture & Allied Sciences

JCECE Entrance Test

Conducted annually by JCECEB for professional admissions into Birsa Agricultural University (BAU) Ranchi and affiliated state colleges.

  • • Exam Groups: PCM (Physics, Chemistry, Math for B.Tech Dairy / Agri Engg), PCB (Physics, Chemistry, Biology for B.Sc Agriculture, Forestry, Veterinary), and PCMB (combined).
  • • Marks & Questions: 50 MCQs per subject (50 marks). PCM = 150 Marks (150 Mins), PCB = 150 Marks (150 Mins), PCMB = 200 Marks (200 Mins).
  • • Marking Scheme: +1 mark for each correct response; -0.25 negative marking for incorrect attempts. Bilingual question paper (English & Hindi).

JCECEB Counselling Decision Simulator

Jharkhand e-counselling follows strict round progression, seat locking, and seat acceptance fee rules. Explore how your decision in Round 1 impacts your upgradation chances in Round 2, Round 3, and Open Spot Rounds.

🔒

Option A: Accept & Freeze

Satisfied with your allotted institute and branch. You confirm this seat and exit future counselling rounds.

Mandatory Steps: Pay ₹1,000 seat acceptance fee, download Provisional Allotment Letter, and report to college within the specified verification window.
🔄

Option B: Accept & Upgrade (Float)

Accept the current allotment as a safety cushion while participating in Round 2 or 3 for higher-order preferences.

Rule: If a higher choice is allotted in the next round, the previous seat is instantly forfeited. If not, your Round 1 seat remains intact.
❌

Option C: Reject & Exit / Spot Round

Do not pay the ₹1,000 fee. You forfeit this seat entirely and will only be eligible to participate in fresh registration during the Special Spot Round.

Warning: The forfeited seat will be released into the open vacancy pool for subsequent round applicants.

💡 Pro-Tip: Golden Rule for Jharkhand Engineering Choice Filling

In JCECEB web options, never place a college or branch you are unwilling to join ahead of a preferred option. Top choices should always begin with BIT Sindri (CSE → IT → ECE → Electrical → Mechanical → Civil → Metallurgy → Chemical → Mining → Production), followed by UCET Hazaribagh, Govt Engineering College Ramgarh, Dumka, Chaibasa (CSE/ECE), and reputable private institutions like Cambridge Institute of Technology Ranchi.

🏡 Domicile Norms & Special Sub-Quotas

1. Permanent vs Local Resident of Jharkhand

Resident Certificate must be issued by Circle Officer (CO), Sub-Divisional Officer (Civil) (SDO), or Deputy Commissioner (DC) in Jharkhand. Only candidates holding valid residential status are eligible for state quota seats in government institutions (BIT Sindri).

2. Tuition Fee Waiver (TFW Scheme - 5%)

5% supernumerary seats in all AICTE-approved engineering branches. Provides 100% tuition fee waiver for Jharkhand residents whose family annual income from all sources is below ₹8,00,000.

3. PwD & Servicemen Quota (SMQ)

5% horizontal reservation for benchmark disabilities (minimum 40% disability with UDID). Priority reservation for children/dependents of defense personnel under the SMQ quota.

Step-by-Step JCECEB Application & Counselling Roadmap

A visual walkthrough of the entire journey from JEE Main application to JCECEB state registration, choice locking, and classroom reporting in Jharkhand.

Step 1: Appear in JEE Main 2027 / JCECE Entrance Exam

Candidates aspiring for 1st Year B.Tech must appear in JEE Main 2027 Paper 1 (Session 1 and/or Session 2) and obtain an All India Common Rank List (CRL) rank. Candidates aspiring for B.Sc Agriculture or Lateral Entry must register for the JCECE entrance examination on jceceb.jharkhand.gov.in.

Step 2: JCECEB Online Counselling Registration & Document Upload

When JCECEB opens the application portal, register using your JEE Main Application Number and personal details. Upload scanned certificates: JEE Main Scorecard, 10th & 12th marksheet, Jharkhand Local/Permanent Resident Certificate, and Category/Income Certificate. Pay the application-cum-counselling fee online (₹500 for General/EWS/BC-I/BC-II; ₹250 for SC/ST/Female).

Step 3: State Merit List (SML) Publication & Verification

JCECEB compiles and publishes the Provisional State Merit List (SML). Verify your State CRL Rank, Category Rank, and qualifying marks. If there is any discrepancy in your uploaded details, submit a grievance before the final deadline to ensure an accurate Final SML rank.

Step 4: Online Web Choice Filling & Preference Locking

Log into the counselling portal with your credentials. Browse participating institutes (BIT Sindri, UCET Hazaribagh, Ramgarh, Dumka, Chaibasa, CIT Ranchi, etc.) and add choices in descending order of your preference. Lock your choices before the deadline; unlocked choices are auto-locked at the closing cutoff time.

Step 5: Seat Allotment Result & Acceptance Fee Payment

JCECEB releases the Seat Allotment List based on SML rank and submitted preferences. Candidates who secure a seat must pay the partial admission seat acceptance fee (₹1,000) online to download their Provisional Seat Allotment Letter. Choose between Freeze (confirm) or Float (upgrade in next round).

Step 6: Physical Document Verification & Institute Reporting

Report physically to the allotted college campus within the scheduled verification dates with all original documents, photographs, medical fitness certificate, and balance tuition fees. Once documents are physically verified by the institute scrutiny committee, your admission is confirmed.

Frequently Asked Questions (JCECE 2027)

Clear answers to the most common queries regarding Jharkhand engineering admissions, BIT Sindri cutoffs, domicile rules, and JCECE Agriculture entrance.

How is admission into 1st Year B.Tech conducted in Jharkhand engineering colleges? +
In Jharkhand, 1st Year B.Tech admissions are conducted centrally by JCECEB based entirely on JEE Main All India CRL Rank. JCECEB prepares a Jharkhand State Merit List (SML) using JEE Main CRL, and candidates participate in web counselling without taking a separate engineering entrance exam.
What courses are covered under the JCECE 2027 entrance examination? +
JCECEB conducts the JCECE entrance examination specifically for B.Sc. (Hons) Agriculture, B.Sc. Forestry, B.Sc. Horticulture, B.V.Sc & A.H., B.F.Sc, B.Tech Agriculture Engineering, and B.Tech Dairy Technology. Lateral entry B.Tech admissions are handled through the Diploma to Degree entrance test.
Can non-Jharkhand (Other State) students apply for BIT Sindri? +
No. 100% of seats at BIT Sindri and other government engineering institutions under JCECEB are reserved strictly for Local / Permanent Residents of Jharkhand possessing a valid resident certificate issued by a competent authority (CO, SDO, or DC).
What is the difference between BC-I and BC-II categories in Jharkhand? +
Jharkhand classifies Other Backward Classes into two distinct sub-quotas: BC-I (Extremely Backward Classes) with 8% reservation and BC-II (Backward Classes) with 6% reservation. Candidates must submit a valid Non-Creamy Layer (NCL) caste certificate issued in the Jharkhand state government format.
What is the expected JEE Main cutoff for BIT Sindri Computer Science (CSE)? +
For the General / Unreserved (UR) category, BIT Sindri CSE typically closes around JEE Main CRL Rank 28,000 to 38,000 (State Merit List rank 1 to 110). For BC-I and BC-II categories, closing ranks generally range between 42,000 and 54,000 CRL, while SC/ST cutoffs extend up to 1,15,000 to 2,20,000 CRL.
What is the TFW (Tuition Fee Waiver) Scheme in Jharkhand B.Tech? +
Under AICTE norms, 5% supernumerary seats are allocated as TFW seats in all technical colleges. Jharkhand domicile candidates with an annual parental income of less than ₹8,00,000 are eligible for a 100% waiver of tuition fees throughout the 4-year degree.
What is the marking scheme for JCECE Agriculture entrance test? +
For PCM and PCB groups, the exam comprises 150 questions (50 each in Physics, Chemistry, and Math/Biology) worth 150 marks. Each correct answer awards +1 mark, while each wrong answer deducts 0.25 marks. PCMB group has 200 questions worth 200 marks.
What is the seat acceptance fee for JCECEB online counselling? +
Candidates allotted a seat in Round 1, Round 2, or Round 3 must pay a seat acceptance / partial admission fee of ₹1,000 online to download their Provisional Seat Allotment Letter. This fee is adjusted against the final college admission fees.
Can I upgrade my allotted seat in Round 2 or Round 3? +
Yes. Candidates who accept their allotted seat in Round 1 can opt for Upgradation (Float) to participate in subsequent rounds. If a higher preference choice is allotted, the previous seat is automatically vacated; if not, the original seat remains preserved.
Who is eligible for Jharkhand B.Tech Lateral Entry? +
Candidates who have completed a 3-year AICTE-approved Diploma in Engineering/Technology with at least 45% marks (40% for reserved categories), or a B.Sc. degree with Mathematics, and hold Jharkhand local residential status are eligible for 2nd Year B.Tech admissions.
Is there any negative marking in JEE Main for Jharkhand admissions? +
Yes. Since 1st Year B.Tech merit is based on JEE Main, the standard NTA marking scheme applies: +4 marks for correct answers and -1 mark for incorrect answers in both MCQs and Numerical Value Questions.
Which authority issues the residential certificate in Jharkhand? +
Candidates must obtain a Local Resident / Permanent Resident Certificate from Circle Officer (CO), Sub-Divisional Officer (Civil) (SDO), or Deputy Commissioner (DC) of Jharkhand, framed in compliance with Department of Personnel, Administrative Reforms and Rajbhasha guidelines.
