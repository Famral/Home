XAT 2027 Eligibility Criteria & Academic Norms

Academic degrees, final year candidate regulations, work experience norms, and our interactive eligibility evaluator.

Academic Qualifications

  • Recognized Bachelor's Degree: Candidates must possess a recognized bachelor's degree of minimum three years duration in any discipline from a university recognized by UGC/AIU.
  • No Minimum Percentage Threshold for Registration: XLRI does not prescribe a minimum graduation aggregate cutoff (such as 50%) merely to register or appear for XAT. However, participating institutes typically require ≥50% marks (45% for SC/ST).
  • Final Year Appearing Students: Candidates appearing for their final year graduation examination are fully eligible to apply, provided they complete all graduation requirements by June 10, 2027.

Age, Nationality & Work Experience

  • No Upper Age Limit: There is no age bar for registering or appearing for XAT 2027. Candidates of any age fulfilling educational prerequisites may apply.
  • Work Experience Policy: Work experience is not mandatory for standard PGDM (BM) or PGDM (HRM) programmes. Fresh graduates are welcome. Candidates with relevant post-qualification full-time work experience receive merit weightage during the PI round.
  • NRI & Foreign Candidates: NRI/Foreign candidates can apply through GMAT scores (minimum valid score usually 650+) or appear directly for XAT 2027.

XAT Computer Test

Stage 2 • January-Feb

Shortlisting Calls

Final Merit Offers

XAT 2027 Strategic Preparation Roadmap

Proven section-wise tactical blueprints for mastering Decision Making, managing unattempted question penalties, and acing the 210-minute CBT.

Phase 1 • Months 1–2

Core Fundamentals

Build rigorous mathematical foundations in Geometry, Algebra, and Modern Math. Cultivate active reading habits with philosophical and economic essays for VALR.

Phase 2 • Months 3–4

Decision Making & DI Mastery

Solve past 15 years' XAT Decision Making sets. Internalize the principle: balanced, ethical, pragmatic solutions trump extreme or emotionally reactive choices.

Phase 3 • Months 5–6

Full Mock Rigor & Strategy

Take 20+ full-length 210-minute mocks in the 2:00 PM – 5:30 PM slot. Fine-tune time allocation across VALR (50m), DM (45m), and QA-DI (65m) to clear all sectional cutoffs.

Golden Rules for Decision Making (DM) in XAT

  • Never Choose Illegal or Unethical Options: Even if a solution maximizes short-term business profits, any option violating law, fairness, or human dignity is automatically incorrect.
  • Stakeholder Balancing: In business caselets, look for solutions that address the core concerns of employees, customers, shareholders, and community harmoniously rather than zero-sum outcomes.
  • Avoid Extreme Reactions: Immediate dismissals, strikes, abrupt cancellations, or radical boycotts are rarely the correct answer. Prefer consultative, evidence-based, phased measures.
  • Stick Strictly to Stated Facts: Do not import outside business knowledge or personal biases into the caselet. Base your logical inference purely on the text provided.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Comprehensive answers to the most common queries regarding XAT 2027 registration, exam pattern, cutoffs, and admissions.

XAT (Xavier Aptitude Test) is a prestigious national post-graduate management entrance test conducted annually by XLRI Xavier School of Management, Jamshedpur on behalf of the Xavier Association of Management Institutes (XAMI). It is the gateway to XLRI Jamshedpur and XLRI Delhi-NCR, as well as over 250 top associate management institutes across India including XIMB, IMT Ghaziabad, TAPMI, GIM Goa, and Great Lakes.

This rule discourages indiscriminate skipping and rewards disciplined question selection.

Decision Making is exclusive to XAT and tests real-world managerial judgment. Rather than rote formulaic math or grammar, it presents multi-stakeholder corporate, ethical, environmental, and interpersonal dilemmas where test-takers must select the most balanced, legally sound, and pragmatic course of action.
XLRI Business Management (BM) typically demands higher mathematical aptitude, setting the QA-DI cutoff at ≥85%ile for male engineers and ≥96%ile overall. Human Resource Management (HRM) places higher weight on linguistic and logical capabilities, setting VALR at ≥80%ile and QA-DI at ≥75%ile, with an overall cutoff around ≥95%ile. Female candidates and non-engineers receive relaxation of 2–5 percentile points.
The standard XAT application fee is ₹2,200 for all candidates. Candidates interested in applying to XLRI Jamshedpur programmes (BM / HRM) pay an additional ₹200 per programme chosen. Payment is processed completely online via debit card, credit card, net banking, or UPI.
XLRI opens a brief limited application correction window after the registration deadline. However, core personal identifiers such as candidate name, primary mobile number, email address, and chosen test city preferences cannot be altered once finalized. Always review all entries before submitting fee payment.
The XAT 2027 score is valid for one academic year only, applicable for admissions to the 2027–2029 post-graduate management batch. Scores cannot be carried over to subsequent academic years.
