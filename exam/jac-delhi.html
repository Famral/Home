JAC Delhi 2027 Overview & Key Highlights

Joint Admission Counselling Delhi (JAC Delhi 2027) is the single centralized admission portal conducted by the JAC Delhi Committee for undergraduate engineering (B.Tech) and architecture admissions across 5 premier autonomous state government universities in the National Capital Territory of Delhi.

Eligibility Criteria, 85% Delhi Quota & Category Rules

Unlike many state counselling bodies where residence certificates or parent voter cards dictate domicile, JAC Delhi operates on a strictly territorial school-location rule established by statutory Delhi government enactments.

📍 85% Delhi Region Quota Rule

To qualify under the 85% Delhi Region Quota, a candidate must have passed their qualifying examination (Class 12th or equivalent) from a recognized school, college, or institution physically situated within the National Capital Territory (NCT) of Delhi.

Crucial Note: Residence address, parental domicile, or voter ID has ZERO bearing on the Delhi quota. Even if you reside in Noida, Gurugram, or Faridabad, if your Class 12 school was in Delhi, you are 100% eligible for the 85% Delhi Quota!

🌏 15% Outside Delhi Quota (AIQ)

Candidates who have passed their qualifying examination from a school located outside the National Capital Territory of Delhi fall under the 15% Outside Delhi Quota.

Outside Delhi candidates compete for 15% of seats across all categories (General, OBC, EWS, SC, ST, Defense). Closing cutoffs for Outside Delhi are extremely competitive due to the national brand value of DTU, NSUT, and IIIT-D.

DTU, NSUT & DSEU Academic Threshold Minimum 60% aggregate marks in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM) in Class 12th examination, and must have passed English as a subject of study. (Relaxation: 55% for OBC-NCL / Defense, 50% for SC / ST / PwD).
IIIT-Delhi Academic Threshold Minimum 70% aggregate marks in best of five subjects including Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics, AND must have secured at least 70% marks in Mathematics specifically in Class 12th. (Relaxation: 65% for OBC-NCL / Defense, 60% for SC / ST / PwD).
IGDTUW (Women Only) Academic Threshold Minimum 60% aggregate marks in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM) and passed English in Class 12th. Exclusively for female candidates. (Relaxation: 55% for OBC-NCL / Defense, 50% for SC / ST / PwD).

🎖️ Defense Personnel (CW) Priority Hierarchy (Priority I to IX)

Seats reserved under the 5% Defense Sub-Category (CW) in JAC Delhi are allotted strictly on the basis of priority order, NOT rank alone. A Priority I candidate with a 3,00,000 rank will be allotted ahead of a Priority VI candidate with a 15,000 rank:

  1. Priority I: Widows / Wards of Armed Forces personnel killed in action.
  2. Priority II: Wards of Armed Forces personnel disabled in action and boarded out from service.
  3. Priority III: Widows / Wards of Armed Forces personnel who died while in service with death attributable to military service.
  4. Priority IV: Wards of Armed Forces personnel disabled in service with disability attributable to military service.
  5. Priority V: Wards of Ex-Servicemen and serving personnel who are in receipt of Gallantry Awards (Param Vir Chakra, Ashok Chakra, Maha Vir Chakra, Kirti Chakra, Vir Chakra, Shaurya Chakra, Sena Medal, Mention-in-Despatches).
  6. Priority VI: Wards of Ex-Servicemen (ESM).
  7. Priority VII: Wives of defense personnel disabled in service with 50% or more disability.
  8. Priority VIII: Wards of Serving Defense Personnel.
  9. Priority IX: Wives of Serving Defense Personnel.

JAC Delhi 2027 Tentative Timeline & Key Milestones

Mark these critical dates on your admissions calendar. Missing the online registration or the strict ₹95,000 SAF fee deadline results in immediate cancellation of your allotted seat and complete forfeiture of admission rights in subsequent regular rounds!

Phase 1 • Late May – Mid June 2027

Online Registration, Document Upload & Choice Filling

Online registration opens at jacdelhi.admissions.nic.in. Pay non-refundable registration fee of ₹1,500. Enter 10+2 marks, school region (Delhi / Outside Delhi), category documents, and fill preferred university-branch choices in prioritized order.

Phase 2 • Mid June 2027

Special Document Verification for CW, PwD & KM Categories

Mandatory in-person document authentication for Defense personnel wards (CW), Benchmark Disability (PwD), and Kashmiri Migrants (KM) conducted at DTU Campus or designated medical boards.

Phase 3 • Late June 2027

Round 1 Seat Allotment & Mandatory ₹95,000 SAF Payment

Round 1 seat allocation published online. Allotted candidates MUST pay ₹95,000 Seat Acceptance Fee (SAF) online within 3 days and report physically to the allotted university for document verification.

Phase 4 • Early July – Late July 2027

Rounds 2, 3, 4 & 5 Seat Allocation & Seat Withdrawal Window

Subsequent allotment rounds conducted for vacant seats. Admitted candidates who selected 'Float' are considered for upgrades. Crucial Seat Surrender / Withdrawal Deadline closes at the end of Round 4/5 for refund of ₹95,000 SAF (minus ₹1,000 admin charge).

Phase 5 • Early August 2027

Internal Upgradation Round (Post-Round 5)

Automated branch upgradation conducted for all admitted candidates holding a floating seat. No physical reporting or fresh choice filling required. All upgraded seats automatically freeze.

Phase 6 • Mid August 2027

Physical Spot Round(s) at DTU / NSUT Campus

Open spot admissions conducted physically at DTU or NSUT campus for all remaining vacant seats. Open to all registered candidates and fresh spot entrants on payment of registration fees and immediate Demand Draft / full fee payment.

Step-by-Step JAC Delhi 2027 Counselling Roadmap

A comprehensive 6-stage chronological roadmap detailing every procedural checkpoint from JEE Main CRL rank qualification to final campus orientation.

1️⃣ Step 1: NIC Registration

Log on to jacdelhi.admissions.nic.in using your JEE Main Application Number. Fill personal particulars, enter Class 12 school address (validating 85% Delhi vs 15% Outside Delhi), category details, and deposit ₹1,500 registration fee online.

2️⃣ Step 2: Strategic Choice Locking

Add university-branch combinations in strictly prioritized order. Keep dream choices (DTU COE, NSUT CSAI, IIIT-D CSE) at the top followed by balanced circuit and core branches. Remember: locking is irreversible after the deadline.

3️⃣ Step 3: SAF ₹95,000 Online Payment

Upon receiving a seat allotment, pay the non-negotiable ₹95,000 Seat Acceptance Fee online immediately. Missing this deadline cancels your seat permanently and expels you from all subsequent regular rounds!

4️⃣ Step 4: Physical Document Verification

Visit the designated campus of your allotted university (DTU, NSUT, IIITD, IGDTUW, or DSEU) on the assigned day with original 10th & 12th certificates, category documents, medical fitness certificate, and registration slip.

5️⃣ Step 5: Freeze or Float Willingness

Choose 'Freeze' if you are 100% satisfied with your allotment. Choose 'Float' to retain your verified seat as insurance while automatically competing for higher choices in Rounds 2 through 5 and the Upgradation Round.

6️⃣ Step 6: Upgradation & Spot Entry

Floating candidates receive automated internal branch upgrades without fresh registration. Vacant seats remaining after Round 5 are auctioned in open Spot Rounds based strictly on JEE Main CRL order.

IIIT Delhi Bonus Points & Delhi Govt Fee Waivers (FFS)

Learn how Olympiad winners, competitive coders, and sports champions secure up to 2 extra bonus percentile points at IIIT Delhi, and explore the Delhi Government's 100% Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme.

⭐ IIIT Delhi Bonus Percentile Scheme

Unique to IIIT Delhi in JAC counselling, candidates with verifiable co-curricular distinctions receive bonus percentile points added to their JEE Main score for IIIT-D merit list generation:

  • Olympiads (IMO, INOI, INPhO, INChO, INBO, IOI): 1 to 2 Bonus Percentile points for Stage 2 qualifiers / national camp attendees.
  • National Talent Search / KVPY: 1 Bonus Percentile point for NTSE Scholars or KVPY Fellows.
  • Sports & Chess: Up to 2 Bonus points for National / State medalists or FIDE rating > 1800.
  • Coding & Innovation: Top-tier distinctions in Progate, Google Summer of Code, or National Science Exhibitions.

💰 Delhi Govt Financial Assistance Scheme (FFS)

The Government of NCT of Delhi guarantees that no deserving student admitted to DTU, NSUT, IIIT-D, IGDTUW, or DSEU is denied engineering education due to financial hardship:

  • 100% Tuition Fee Waiver: For students holding National Food Security (Antyodaya / BPL) cards or family income ≤ ₹2.5 Lakhs/year.
  • 50% Tuition Fee Waiver: For students with gross family annual income between ₹2.5 Lakhs and ₹6.0 Lakhs.
  • 25% Tuition Fee Waiver: For students with gross family annual income between ₹6.0 Lakhs and ₹10.0 Lakhs (subject to 60%+ academic performance).

Frequently Asked Questions — JAC Delhi 2027

Clear, authoritative answers to the most frequently asked questions regarding JAC Delhi admission rules, cutoffs, fee structures, and upgradation rounds.

Joint Admission Counselling Delhi (JAC Delhi) is the centralized single-window admission authority for B.Tech and B.Arch programs across 5 government technical universities in Delhi: DTU, NSUT, IIIT Delhi, IGDTUW, and DSEU. Admissions are governed strictly by JEE Main All India Common Rank List (CRL) merit through the official portal jacdelhi.admissions.nic.in.
Eligibility for the 85% Delhi Quota depends exclusively on the physical geographic location of the school or college where you completed your Class 12th qualifying examination. If your school was located within the National Capital Territory (NCT) of Delhi, you are entitled to the 85% Delhi Quota, irrespective of whether your permanent home is in Delhi, UP, or Haryana.
The Seat Acceptance Fee (SAF) is ₹95,000. It is mandatory for every allotted candidate in any round, regardless of whether you choose 'Freeze' or 'Float'. If you choose 'Float', paying the ₹95,000 guarantees that your currently allotted seat remains secure in your pocket while you compete for higher branch preferences in subsequent rounds and the Upgradation Round.
You can withdraw your allotted seat online through the JAC Delhi portal before the announced Seat Withdrawal Deadline (typically at the end of Round 4 or Round 5). Upon online surrender, the entire ₹95,000 is refunded to your bank account after deducting a nominal administrative fee of ₹1,000. No refund is granted if you withdraw after the deadline!
IIIT-Delhi awards up to 2 extra bonus percentile points added to your JEE Main percentile for verified achievements in Olympiads (IMO, INOI, INPhO), KVPY, NTSE, Sports (national/state), Chess (FIDE rating), Progate, or coding competitions. These bonus points apply exclusively to IIIT Delhi seat allocation and can significantly jump your rank!
Yes! Indira Gandhi Delhi Technical University for Women (IGDTUW) Kashmere Gate is exclusively for female candidates and is one of the 5 participating universities in JAC Delhi. Female candidates can include IGDTUW branches (CSE, AI-DS, IT, ECE, MAE) alongside DTU, NSUT, and IIIT-D in their choice list.
Both DTU (formerly DCE, Rohini) and NSUT (formerly DIT, Dwarka) are world-class institutions with virtually identical placements and curriculum. NSUT CSAI and COE traditionally close at marginally higher cutoffs due to its slightly smaller batch size and elite coding culture in Dwarka. DTU boasts a vast 164-acre campus, massive technical alumni network in tech leadership, and exceptional college festivals (Engifest). Students generally pick whichever campus is closer to home!
The Upgradation Round takes place after Round 5 for all admitted candidates who maintained a 'Float' status. The portal automatically upgrades candidates into higher unfilled choices based on vacant seats without requiring physical reporting or fresh choice filling. It is completely risk-free—your existing seat is 100% safeguarded.
The Spot Round is conducted physically after the Upgradation Round at DTU or NSUT campus to fill remaining vacant seats. Any candidate who did not register earlier, withdrew earlier, or was not allotted any seat can register. Candidates must attend in person with original certificates and a Demand Draft / online payment for full first-year admission fees. Seats are awarded in descending order of CRL rank on the spot.
For DTU, NSUT, and IGDTUW, the candidate's caste must be included in the official Delhi Government OBC list, AND the Non-Creamy Layer (NCL) certificate must be issued by a competent Delhi authority (SDM/Tehsildar) on or after April 1, 2026/2027. If your caste is only on the Central OBC list and not on the Delhi list, you will be treated as General for DTU/NSUT (though IIIT-Delhi accepts Central OBC list certificates).
