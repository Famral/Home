TG EAPCET 2027 Overview & Key Highlights

The Telangana Engineering, Agriculture and Pharmacy Common Entrance Test (TG EAPCET 2027) is the state’s apex entrance examination conducted annually by Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University Hyderabad (JNTUH) under the aegis of the Telangana State Council of Higher Education (TGCHE). It governs centralized undergraduate engineering admissions (B.Tech / B.E.) across premier university colleges and accredited private autonomous institutions in Telangana.

TGCHE RULES 2027

🏛️ Osmania University (OU) Local Status

Under the Presidential Order (371-D), candidates who have studied in educational institutions in Telangana for at least 4 consecutive academic years ending with the qualifying examination (or 4 out of 7 consecutive years) are declared OU Local candidates.

  • 85% of Convenor Seats in JNTUH, OU, and all Telangana private colleges reserved for OU locals.
  • 15% Unreserved seats open on merit to both local Telangana and non-local AP/Other state students.
  • Local Status certificate established via continuous Class 6 to 12 study certificates.

🎓 100% TS ePASS Fee Reimbursement

Telangana offers one of India’s most generous higher education scholarship schemes through TS ePASS Post-Matric scholarships.

  • Rank < 10,000 Privilege: 100% full tuition fee reimbursed in any private or university engineering college, irrespective of income.
  • SC & ST Candidates: 100% full tuition fee reimbursed across all colleges and ranks.
  • BC & EWS Ranks ≥ 10,000: Fixed ₹35,000/year reimbursement in private colleges (any excess tuition borne by student).

⚖️ Scrapping of 25% Board Marks Rule

Earlier, 25% weightage of Intermediate board marks was combined with 75% entrance marks. TGCHE permanently abolished this practice.

  • Merit ranks are derived 100% from normalized CBT marks out of 160.
  • Students from CBSE, ICSE, NIOS, or other state boards compete on an absolutely level playing field.
  • Intermediate marks serve purely as a basic qualification requirement (45% for General, 40% for Reserved).

TG EAPCET 2027 Exam Pattern & CBT Normalization

The examination is administered in Computer Based Test (CBT) format across 4 to 6 sessions. Candidates answer 160 objective multiple-choice questions within 180 minutes. There is no negative marking for incorrect attempts.

📐 Mathematics

80 MCQs • 80 Marks

Carries 50% of the total paper weightage. Crucial primary tie-breaker for state merit ranking.

  • Algebra: Functions, Matrices, Complex Numbers, Quadratic Equations, Binomial Theorem, Permutations & Combinations.
  • Trigonometry: Trigonometric Ratios, Compound Angles, Multiple/Submultiple, Inverses, Hyperbolic functions.
  • Coordinate Geometry: Straight Lines, Circles, System of Circles, Parabola, Ellipse, Hyperbola, 3D Geometry.
  • Calculus: Limits, Continuity, Differentiation, Applications of Derivatives, Indefinite & Definite Integrals, Differential Equations.

⚡ Physics

40 MCQs • 40 Marks

Carries 25% of the total paper weightage. Secondary tie-breaker in TG EAPCET rank formulation.

  • Mechanics: Units & Measurements, Motion in a Plane, Laws of Motion, Work Energy Power, Rotational Dynamics, Gravitation.
  • Thermal Physics: Thermal Properties of Matter, Kinetic Theory of Gases, Thermodynamics.
  • Electromagnetism: Electrostatics, Current Electricity, Moving Charges & Magnetism, Electromagnetic Induction, AC Circuits.
  • Optics & Modern Physics: Wave Optics, Ray Optics, Dual Nature of Radiation, Atoms, Nuclei, Semiconductor Devices.

🧪 Chemistry

40 MCQs • 40 Marks

Carries 25% of the total paper weightage. High-scoring section for fast raw score accumulation.

  • Physical Chemistry: Atomic Structure, Chemical Bonding, States of Matter, Thermodynamics, Chemical Equilibrium, Solutions, Electrochemistry.
  • Inorganic Chemistry: Periodic Classification, p-Block, d & f Block Elements, Coordination Compounds.
  • Organic Chemistry: General Principles (GOC), Hydrocarbons, Haloalkanes, Alcohols, Phenols & Ethers, Aldehydes & Ketones, Amines, Biomolecules.
  • Applied Chemistry: Polymers, Chemistry in Everyday Life, Principles of Qualitative Analysis.

Official Multi-Session Normalization Formula

TGCHE EQUATING ALGORITHM

Because candidates take the CBT across distinct morning and afternoon shifts with varying difficulty levels, raw marks are equated using the globally established equi-percentile standard deviation formula:

Normalized Mark = GASD + [ (GTA - GASD) / (STA - SASD) ] × (Raw Mark - SASD)
GASD: Sum of Average and Standard Deviation of all candidates across all sessions combined.
GTA: Average mark of the top 0.1% candidates across all sessions combined.
STA: Average mark of the top 0.1% candidates in the candidate's specific session.
SASD: Sum of Average and Standard Deviation of the candidate's specific session.

📍 85% OU Local vs 15% Unreserved Quota

Under the A.P. Educational Institutions (Regulation of Admissions) Order, 1974 (Presidential Order):

  • 85% Local Quota: Strictly allocated to candidates holding Local status in Osmania University area (Telangana districts).
  • 15% Unreserved (UR) Quota: Open to all eligible Indian citizens. Candidates from Telangana and Andhra Pradesh compete side by side on pure merit.
  • A local candidate has dual advantage: they can compete for both the 15% UR pool and the 85% exclusive local pool.

💰 TS ePASS Income Criteria & Full Waivers

To claim tuition fee reimbursement under the Post-Matric Scholarship Scheme:

  • Under 10,000 Rank: 100% fee waiver granted automatically in convenor quota without income restrictions.
  • Income Limit for Others: Annual parental income must not exceed ₹2,00,000 for SC/ST and urban BC/EWS; ₹1,50,000 for rural BC/EWS.
  • Income certificate must be issued by MeeSeva (Tahsildar) on or after January 1 of the admission calendar year.

TGCHE Centralized Web Counselling Roadmap

Follow the authoritative 6-phase journey from TG EAPCET rank card release to physical reporting at your allotted engineering campus.

1️⃣ Slot Booking & Processing Fee

Begin counselling at tgeapcet.nic.in. Enter Hall Ticket Number, Registration Number, and Date of Birth. Pay non-refundable processing fee online (₹1,200 for OC/BC, ₹600 for SC/ST). Select your preferred Help Line Centre (HLC), date, and time slot for certificate verification.

2️⃣ Certificate Verification at HLC

Attend physical verification at chosen HLC with original documents and two sets of photocopies. Verification officers examine Aadhar, SSC Memo, Inter Memo, Study Certificates (Class 6–12), Caste Certificate, and MeeSeva Income Certificate. Obtain your Receipt of Certificates (ROC).

3️⃣ Candidate Registration & Password

After HLC verification, receive your Login ID via SMS. Visit the portal, click "Candidate Registration", enter Registration Number, Hall Ticket Number, Rank, and Date of Birth to generate a secure password and generate your OTP for web option sessions.

4️⃣ Strategic Web Options Entry

Log in and enter an unlimited sequence of college-plus-branch preferences. Prioritize dream university colleges (OUCE, JNTUH) and elite autonomous institutions (CBIT, Vasavi, VNR VJIET) at the top. Modify priorities dynamically and freeze choices before deadline.

5️⃣ Seat Allotment & Fee Payment

TGCHE processes options based on rank, category, local region quota (OU vs UR), and gender reservations. Download your Provisional Seat Allotment Letter. Pay required tuition fee online (zero payment if candidate qualifies for 100% TS ePASS fee reimbursement).

6️⃣ Self-Reporting & College Joining

Perform mandatory Online Self-Reporting on the portal to generate your official Joining Report. Report physically to the allotted college before the specified deadline with original documents, allotment letter, and joining report to finalize admission.

Frequently Asked Questions — TG EAPCET 2027

Essential answers to critical queries regarding Telangana entrance scoring, 10,000 rank fee reimbursement privilege, web counselling procedures, and seat allotment rules.

Is the 25% Intermediate board marks weightage added to TG EAPCET 2027 ranks? +

No. The Telangana State Council of Higher Education (TGCHE) and State Government have permanently abolished the 25% Intermediate board marks weightage rule. In TG EAPCET 2027, the State Merit Rank is determined 100% on the normalized marks secured in the CBT examination out of 160. Intermediate board marks are used solely as a baseline qualifying threshold (minimum 45% aggregate in group subjects for General, 40% for reserved categories).

How does the 10,000 rank rule for TS ePASS 100% fee reimbursement work? +

Under the Telangana Post-Matric Scholarship (TS ePASS) guidelines, any student who secures a rank below 10,000 in TG EAPCET receives 100% full tuition fee reimbursement for seats allotted under the convenor quota. This applies to both private engineering colleges and university colleges, regardless of family income or caste category. If your rank is 10,001 or above, full fee waiver is guaranteed only for SC/ST students, while BC and EWS students receive a fixed ₹35,000 per annum (provided family income is ≤ ₹2.00 Lakhs in urban or ₹1.50 Lakhs in rural areas).

How does the multi-shift CBT normalization formula work in TG EAPCET? +

Because the examination is held over 4 to 6 sessions, variations in paper difficulty are mathematically neutralized using the formula: Normalized Marks = GASD + [(GTA - GASD) / (STA - SASD)] × (Candidate Raw Score - SASD). This ensures that a candidate who solved a tough session is not penalized compared to a candidate who got an easier paper.

What is the minimum qualifying mark in TG EAPCET 2027? +

Candidates belonging to Open Category (OC), EWS, and Backward Classes (BC-A/B/C/D/E) must secure at least 25% of the total aggregate marks, which translates to 40 marks out of 160. For Scheduled Caste (SC) and Scheduled Tribe (ST) candidates, there is no minimum qualifying mark; scoring any non-zero mark grants eligibility for rank list generation and counselling.

What is the difference between Local (OU) and Non-Local (Unreserved) seats in Telangana? +

In accordance with the Presidential Order 371-D, 85% of convenor quota seats across all colleges in Telangana are reserved exclusively for Osmania University (OU) local candidates (who have studied 4 out of 7 consecutive years in Telangana). The remaining 15% seats are Unreserved (UR), where local Telangana students and non-local students from Andhra Pradesh or other states compete purely on merit.

Can students from Andhra Pradesh or other states participate in TG EAPCET? +

Yes. Students from Andhra Pradesh and other states can appear for TG EAPCET and participate in TGCHE counselling. They are eligible for the 15% Unreserved (UR) seats in top government, university, and private engineering institutions. However, outside-state students are not eligible for the TS ePASS state fee reimbursement scheme.

What happens if I accept an allotted seat in Phase 1 and participate in Phase 2? +

If you complete tuition fee payment and online self-reporting in Phase 1, your seat is 100% secure. You can then submit fresh web options for higher-preference branches or colleges in Phase 2. If a higher choice is allotted in Phase 2, your Phase 1 seat is automatically surrendered and transferred to another student. If no new seat is allotted in Phase 2, you safely retain your Phase 1 seat.

What is the tie-breaking policy in TG EAPCET 2027? +

When two or more candidates achieve the same normalized aggregate score, ties are resolved in the following sequential order: (1) Higher normalized marks in Mathematics, (2) Higher normalized marks in Physics, (3) Higher percentage in aggregate marks in Intermediate/Class 12th qualifying examination, and (4) Date of birth (the older candidate is ranked higher).

Is slot booking mandatory for certificate verification at Help Line Centres (HLC)? +

Yes. Walk-in verification without slot booking is not permitted. Candidates must visit tgeapcet.nic.in, pay the counselling processing fee, and book a specific date, time slot, and Help Line Centre (HLC). Only candidates with an official slot booking receipt will be allowed into the HLC for verification.

What is Internal Sliding in Telangana engineering counselling? +

Internal Sliding is conducted after the Final Phase of web counselling within individual colleges. It allows students who have already joined a particular college to upgrade their branch (for example, from ECE to CSE or AI/ML) strictly within that same college against vacancies, based on their TG EAPCET rank and category quota.
