CG PET 2027 Examination Blueprint & Authority Overview

The Chhattisgarh Pre-Engineering Test (CG PET) is the premier state-level competitive entrance gateway administered by the Chhattisgarh Professional Examination Board (CG Vyapam) for admission into undergraduate Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) and Bachelor of Engineering (B.E.) programs across prestigious government and private engineering institutes affiliated with Chhattisgarh Swami Vivekanand Technical University (CSVTU), Bhilai.

Chhattisgarh Domicile Norms

Candidates must possess a valid Niwas Praman Patra (Chhattisgarh Domicile Certificate) issued by a competent revenue authority (Tahsildar / Sub-Divisional Magistrate) to claim state quota seats in government colleges (GEC Raipur, Bilaspur, Jagdalpur, CSVTU UTD) and 75% subsidized quota seats in private institutions.

🎓

Academic PCM Criteria

Candidates must pass Class 12 / Higher Secondary with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects along with Chemistry, Biotechnology, Biology, or a Technical Vocational subject. Minimum aggregate marks in PCM: 45% for General (UR) and 40% for SC, ST, OBC (Non-Creamy Layer), and PwD of CG domicile.

⏳

Age Limit & Relaxations

The upper age limit is 30 years as on July 1, 2027 for General category male candidates. A statutory relaxation of 3 years (up to 33 years) is granted to SC, ST, OBC candidates, female candidates across all categories, and Persons with Disabilities (PwD). The minimum age limit is 16 years.

CG Vyapam conducts CG PET as a 150-mark offline pen-and-paper examination aligned with the Class 11 and Class 12 curriculum prescribed by the Chhattisgarh Board of Secondary Education (CGBSE) and NCERT.

Comprehensive Topic-Wise Syllabus Breakdown (CGBSE & NCERT Aligned)

📐 Mathematics (50 Marks)

  • Algebra: Complex numbers, quadratic equations, arithmetic & geometric progressions, permutations and combinations, binomial theorem.
  • Matrices & Determinants: Operations on matrices, adjoint & inverse of matrices, solving linear equations by Cramer's rule.
  • Trigonometry: Trigonometric functions, identities, addition & subtraction formulae, inverse trigonometric functions.
  • Coordinate Geometry: 2D straight lines, circles, standard parabola, ellipse, hyperbola.
  • Calculus: Limits, continuity, derivatives, tangents & normals, maxima & minima, definite & indefinite integrals, areas under curves, first order differential equations.
  • Vector & 3D Geometry: Dot & cross products, direction cosines, equations of lines and planes in 3D space.

⚡ Physics (50 Marks)

  • General Physics & Mechanics: Units & dimensions, dimensional analysis, motion in 1D and 2D, Newton's laws of motion, friction, work, energy and power.
  • Rotational Motion & Gravity: Moment of inertia, conservation of angular momentum, Kepler's laws, acceleration due to gravity, escape velocity.
  • Thermal Physics: Kinetic theory of gases, heat conduction, first and second laws of thermodynamics, Carnot engine.
  • Electromagnetism: Coulomb's law, Gauss's law, capacitors, Ohm's law, Kirchhoff's rules, Biot-Savart law, Ampere's law, Faraday's laws of induction, AC circuits.
  • Optics: Reflection, refraction, thin lens formula, interference of light, Young's double slit experiment, diffraction.
  • Modern Physics: Photoelectric effect, Bohr atom, radioactivity, semiconductor diode (p-n junction), logic gates.

🧪 Chemistry (50 Marks)

  • Physical Chemistry: Atomic structure (quantum numbers, orbital configurations), states of matter, solutions, Raoult's law, chemical kinetics, rate equations, chemical & ionic equilibrium, thermodynamics, electrochemistry.
  • Inorganic Chemistry: Periodic classification of elements, chemical bonding (VSEPR, hybridization, molecular orbital theory), s-block, p-block, transition metals (d & f block), coordination compounds and isomerism.
  • Organic Chemistry: IUPAC nomenclature, inductive and resonance effects, reaction intermediates (carbocations, carbanions, free radicals).
  • Hydrocarbons & Functional Groups: Alkanes, alkenes, alkynes, aromatic hydrocarbons, haloalkanes, alcohols, phenols, ethers, aldehydes, ketones, carboxylic acids, amines.
  • Applied Chemistry: Biomolecules (carbohydrates, amino acids, proteins), polymers, environmental chemistry and water treatment.

⚖️ Official CG Vyapam & CG DTE Tie-Breaking Rules

When two or more candidates obtain identical aggregate scores out of 150 marks in CG PET 2027, inter-se merit ranking is determined strictly according to the statutory hierarchy established by the Directorate of Technical Education:

  1. Higher Marks in Mathematics: The candidate with the higher score in the Mathematics section is ranked higher.
  2. Higher Marks in Physics: If tie remains unresolved, the candidate with higher marks in Physics receives priority.
  3. Higher Marks in Chemistry: If the tie persists, higher marks in Chemistry determines the superior rank.
  4. Seniority in Age: If all sectional marks are identical, the candidate older in age (as documented on their Class 10 Certificate) is placed higher on the merit list.

CG DTE Centralized Counselling Decision Simulator

The Directorate of Technical Education (CG DTE), Chhattisgarh conducts centralized online web counselling for all government, autonomous, and participating private institutes. Understand choice locking rules, round progression, seat confirmation, and upgradation strategies.

🔒

Option A: Accept & Freeze

Completely satisfied with your allotted college and engineering branch. You confirm this allotment and formally exit subsequent counselling rounds.

Next Actions: Download the Provisional Allotment Letter from cgdte.cgstate.gov.in, report to the allotted college within the deadline, verify original documents, and pay the admission fee.
🔄

Option B: Accept & Float (Upgrade)

Retain your current seat as guaranteed security while actively seeking a higher-order college or branch choice in Round 2 or the Upgradation Round.

Upgradation Rule: If a higher preference is allocated in the next round, your previous seat is automatically cancelled and reallocated. If no upgrade occurs, your current seat remains 100% secure.
❌

Option C: Reject / Non-Reporting

Failing to complete document verification or non-reporting within the designated schedule cancels your allotted seat.

Caution: Candidates who forfeit their seat can only participate in the subsequent Institutional Spot Round conducted at respective college campuses subject to vacancies.

Understanding Chhattisgarh's 32% ST Reservation

In accordance with the demographic structure of Chhattisgarh, 32% of all state quota seats are statutory reserved for Scheduled Tribe candidates. In institutions located in tribal regions like Government Engineering College (GEC) Jagdalpur in Bastar division, extensive facilities and scholarship programs are provided to support indigenous technical education.

30% Horizontal Reservation for Women

To foster gender diversity in STEM disciplines, Chhattisgarh mandates a 30% horizontal compartmentalized reservation for female candidates across every social category (UR-Female, ST-Female, OBC-Female, and SC-Female). If female seats remain vacant, they are converted to male candidates of the same category in the subsequent round.

Step-by-Step Chhattisgarh Engineering Admission Roadmap

Follow the sequential pathway from CG Vyapam registration to offline test attendance, CG DTE online choice locking, and final enrollment at your allotted college in Chhattisgarh.

Step 1: CG Vyapam Online Profile Creation & Application

Register on vyapam.cgstate.gov.in during March – April 2027. Create a candidate profile using your mobile number, upload scanned passport photograph and signature, provide Class 10 and 12 marks, select your preferred examination district in Chhattisgarh, and submit the online application form without examination fee for Chhattisgarh domicile candidates.

Step 2: Admit Card Download & Offline OMR Examination

Download your CG PET Admit Card in late May 2027. Arrive at your examination center with two printed copies of the admit card, original photo ID proof (Aadhaar / Voter ID), and blue/black ballpoint pens. Solve 150 questions across Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry within 3 hours.

Step 3: Answer Key Verification & State Merit Rank Publication

Cross-check your answers against the provisional answer key issued by CG Vyapam. Once final results are published in late June, download your rank card displaying your raw marks out of 150 and your All-Chhattisgarh State Merit Rank (SMR) and Category Rank.

Step 4: CG DTE Centralized Counselling Registration & Web Options

Log in to the Directorate of Technical Education counselling portal (cgdte.cgstate.gov.in) using your CG PET Application Number. Upload required documents (Niwas Praman Patra, caste certificates, marksheets) and enter your ordered list of college and branch preferences.

Step 5: Multi-Round Seat Allotment & Freeze/Float Decision

Review Round 1 or Round 2 allotment results. If satisfied with your allotted college, choose Freeze. If you desire a higher-preference branch or college in subsequent rounds, select Float to protect your current seat while participating in upgradation.

Step 6: Document Verification & Physical Reporting

Report to the designated Document Verification Center (DVC) or allotted college with original certificates, Niwas Praman Patra, caste/income certificates, medical fitness certificate, and passport photos to finalize admission and pay institutional fees.

Frequently Asked Questions — CG PET 2027

Find authoritative, straightforward answers to key queries regarding CG Vyapam PET examination, CG DTE counselling rules, domicile eligibility, cutoffs, and seat reservation.

What is CG PET and who conducts the examination and counselling? +
Chhattisgarh Pre-Engineering Test (CG PET) is the state-level entrance examination for admission into undergraduate Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) and Bachelor of Engineering (B.E.) degree programs across government, autonomous, and private engineering institutions in Chhattisgarh. The entrance test is conducted by the Chhattisgarh Professional Examination Board (CG Vyapam / CPEB Raipur), while the centralized online counselling and seat allocation process is conducted separately by the Directorate of Technical Education (CG DTE), Chhattisgarh.
Is Chhattisgarh domicile mandatory for CG PET admission? +
Chhattisgarh Domicile is mandatory for 100% of seats in State Government Engineering Colleges (GEC Raipur, GEC Bilaspur, GEC Jagdalpur, and CSVTU UTD) and 75% of state quota seats in private engineering colleges. Candidates from other states (Non-CG domicile) can apply for the 15% All India Quota in private colleges or participate in CG DTE counselling using their JEE Main All India Ranks.
What is the exam pattern, total marks, and marking scheme for CG PET 2027? +
CG PET is conducted in offline pen-and-paper mode using OMR response sheets. The question paper comprises 150 multiple choice questions (50 in Mathematics, 50 in Physics, and 50 in Chemistry) for a total of 150 marks. Each correct answer carries +1 mark, and there is NO negative marking for incorrect or unattempted responses. The total exam duration is 3 hours (180 minutes).
What are the minimum qualifying marks in 10+2 for CG PET? +
Candidates must have passed Class 12 / Higher Secondary examination with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects along with Chemistry, Biotechnology, Biology, or a Technical Vocational subject. General (UR) category candidates must score at least 45% aggregate marks in compulsory subjects. Reserved categories (SC, ST, OBC Non-Creamy Layer, and PwD of CG domicile) require a minimum of 40% aggregate marks. Candidates must also pass English as an individual subject.
How does the reservation policy work in Chhattisgarh engineering admissions? +
Statutory reservations in Chhattisgarh technical colleges are: 32% for Scheduled Tribes (ST), 14% for Other Backward Classes (OBC Non-Creamy Layer), 12% for Scheduled Castes (SC), and 10% supernumerary for Economically Weaker Sections (EWS). In addition, a 30% horizontal reservation is provided for female candidates within each category, alongside 5% for Persons with Disabilities (PwD) and 3% for Freedom Fighter and Sainik wards.
What is the Tuition Fee Waiver (TFW) scheme in CG DTE counselling? +
Under the AICTE / Government of Chhattisgarh Tuition Fee Waiver (TFW) scheme, 5% supernumerary seats are created above the sanctioned intake in every branch in participating engineering colleges. Chhattisgarh domicile candidates whose annual family income from all sources is below ₹8,00,000 are eligible. Students admitted under the TFW scheme are completely exempt from paying tuition fees for all 4 years of their B.Tech degree.
Can candidates get admission into Chhattisgarh colleges through JEE Main? +
Yes. CG DTE conducts centralized counselling through two merit channels: CG PET State Merit Rank and JEE Main All India Rank. Candidates possessing a valid JEE Main score can apply for Chhattisgarh quota seats (if they hold CG domicile) or All India quota seats in private engineering institutes without having appeared for CG PET.
What score or rank in CG PET is required for CSE at GEC Raipur or BIT Durg? +
At Government Engineering College (GEC) Raipur, Computer Science & Engineering (CSE) is the most competitive branch in the state. For General (UR) category candidates, the closing rank generally closes within Rank 1 to Rank 350 (raw score 105+ out of 150). At Bhilai Institute of Technology (BIT) Durg, General closing rank for CSE closes around Rank 600. For ST candidates, CSE closing ranks range between 2,000 and 3,500.
