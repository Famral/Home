JEECUP 2027 Overview & Key Highlights

The Joint Entrance Examination Council, Uttar Pradesh (JEECUP) conducts UPJEE (Polytechnic) annually as a centralized Computer-Based Test (CBT). It serves as the gateway to technical diploma, vocational, and pharmacy education across Uttar Pradesh under the Board of Technical Education Uttar Pradesh (BTEUP).

JEECUP 2027 Eligibility Criteria & Group Evaluator

JEECUP categorizes diploma admissions into multiple examination groups (Group A through K). Review mandatory academic qualifications, age limits, and domicile requirements, or use the interactive eligibility tool below.

JEECUP 2027 Group Eligibility & Quota Evaluator

Select your intended course group, academic scores, and domicile to check admission eligibility, state quota, and UP scholarship entitlement.

Group A (Engineering)

Must pass Class 10th (High School) with at least 35% aggregate marks. Mathematics and Science are compulsory subjects with minimum passing grades.

Group E (Pharmacy D.Pharm)

Must pass 10+2 (Intermediate) with Physics, Chemistry, and either Mathematics (Group E2) or Biology (Group E1) with minimum 50% marks (45% for SC/ST).

Group K (Lateral Entry)

Direct admission to 2nd Year (3rd Semester) diploma. Requires 10+2 with Science / Vocational stream OR Class 10th plus a recognized 2-year ITI trade certificate.

Step-by-Step JEECUP 2027 Admission Roadmap

A comprehensive walk-through of the six critical phases from initial registration to final class orientation in Uttar Pradesh Government Polytechnics.

Phase 1: Online Registration & Examination Group Selection

Register on jeecup.admissions.nic.in using your Aadhaar number, email, and mobile number. Select your target group (Group A for 3-Year Engineering Diploma, Group E for Pharmacy, or Group K for Lateral Entry). Upload scanned photograph, signature, and pay the fee online (₹300 for Gen/OBC, ₹200 for SC/ST).

Phase 2: Computer-Based Test (CBT) Across 75 UP Districts

Download the CBT admit card in late May 2027. Report to your assigned examination center with admit card and original government photo ID (Aadhaar Card). The exam consists of 100 questions (400 marks) with zero negative marking.

Phase 3: State Open Rank (SOR) Card Publication

BTEUP & JEECUP publish the official result scorecard. Candidates receive a normalized score out of 400 along with their All-UP State Open Rank (SOR) and Category Rank. Candidates with non-zero positive marks qualify for counselling.

Phase 4: Centralized Online Choice Filling & Locking

Log in to the counselling portal, pay the counselling participation fee (₹250), and select your desired polytechnic colleges and branches in strict order of preference. There is no restriction on the number of choices; candidates are advised to lock 30+ choices.

Phase 5: Seat Allotment, Freeze/Float & ₹3,250 Deposit

When seat allotment is declared, log in to view your allotted college and branch. Choose Freeze (final acceptance) or Float (opt for upgrade in Round 2). In both cases, deposit the mandatory ₹3,250 seat acceptance fee online before the round cutoff.

Phase 6: Physical DV at District Help Center & Final Admission

Report in person to your nearest designated District Help Center (Government Polytechnic) with original academic marksheets, caste/domicile certificates, medical certificate, and 6 passport photos. After successful verification, pay the remaining tuition fee online and download your Final Admission Confirmation Letter.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) — JEECUP 2027

Answers to the most common queries regarding UP Polytechnic eligibility, negative marking rules, fee structures, and counselling choices.

What is JEECUP and which courses are offered through it?
JEECUP (Joint Entrance Examination Council Uttar Pradesh), also known as UPJEE (Polytechnic), is the premier state-level entrance exam conducted for admission into 3-year Diploma in Engineering & Technology (Group A), 2-year Lateral Entry Engineering Diploma (Group K), Diploma in Pharmacy (Group E1/E2), and various post-diploma and PG diploma programs across 150+ Government, 19 Aided, and 1,200+ Private polytechnics affiliated with BTEUP Lucknow.
Is there any negative marking in JEECUP 2027?
No, JEECUP has officially removed negative marking. Candidates receive +4 marks for every correct answer, while zero (0) marks are deducted for incorrect or unattempted questions. The test consists of 100 questions for a total of 400 marks.
What is the minimum eligibility criteria for Group A Engineering Diploma?
For Group A (3-Year Engineering Diploma), candidates must have passed Class 10th (High School) examination from a recognized board with a minimum of 35% aggregate marks, with Mathematics and Science as compulsory subjects. There is no upper age limit, but the candidate must be at least 14 years old as of July 1, 2027.
Can candidates from states outside Uttar Pradesh apply for JEECUP 2027?
Yes, candidates from other Indian states are eligible to apply under the All India (Open) Quota, which accounts for 15% of total seats in Government and Aided polytechnics, and 100% of seats in Private polytechnic colleges. Other state candidates generally participate from Round 4 onwards in centralized counselling.
What is the difference between Freeze and Float options in JEECUP counselling?
'Freeze' means you accept the allotted seat as your final choice, pay the seat acceptance fee, complete document verification at the Help Center, and proceed for final admission. 'Float' means you accept the current seat as a security backup while opting to upgrade to higher preference choices in subsequent counselling rounds. For both options, paying the ₹3,250 seat acceptance fee and reporting to the Help Center in Round 1 is mandatory.
What is the annual fee structure for Government Polytechnics in Uttar Pradesh?
Government Polytechnics in UP have a highly subsidized annual tuition fee of approximately ₹10,370 per year. Aided polytechnics charge around ₹19,000 per year, while private polytechnic institutes charge between ₹30,000 to ₹45,000 per year. Eligible UP domicile candidates can receive 100% fee reimbursement under the UP Post-Matric Scholarship Scheme (Saksham Portal).
Which are the top 5 Government Polytechnics in Uttar Pradesh?
The top 5 Government Polytechnics in UP based on closing cutoff ranks and placement records are: 1) Government Polytechnic, Lucknow (Code 2201), 2) Government Polytechnic, Kanpur (Code 3301), 3) Government Polytechnic, Ghaziabad (Code 1101), 4) Government Polytechnic, Gorakhpur (Code 4401), and 5) Hewett Polytechnic, Lucknow (Aided, Code 2204) followed by IERT Prayagraj.
What is the qualifying marks requirement to get a rank in JEECUP 2027?
There is no fixed minimum cutoff score required to obtain a JEECUP State Rank. Any candidate who appears in the CBT exam and scores at least 1 mark (positive non-zero score) is assigned a State Open Rank (SOR) and category rank, making them eligible for centralized counselling rounds.
