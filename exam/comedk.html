The Four Post-Allotment Choices Explained

Every candidate allotted a seat in COMEDK must exercise one of four designated choices within the specified time window. Failure to exercise a choice results in automatic cancellation and permanent disqualification.

Accept & Freeze Option 1
100% Satisfied — Final Admission

Candidate accepts the allotted seat unconditionally and does not wish to participate in subsequent rounds. Pay full tuition fee online, print Online Allotment Letter & Fee Receipt, and physically report to the allotted college within the reporting deadline.

Mandatory Action: Full Fee Payment + College Campus Reporting
Accept & Upgrade Option 2
Hold Backup Seat + Try Higher Options

Candidate is satisfied with the current seat as a guaranteed backup, but wishes to be considered for higher preferred choices in subsequent rounds. Pay the full tuition fee online to hold the seat. If upgraded in the next round, previous seat is automatically transferred; if not upgraded, current seat is retained!

Mandatory Action: Full Fee Payment to Lock Seat + Participate in Next Round
Reject & Upgrade Option 3
Forfeit Current Seat + Aim Higher

Candidate rejects the currently allotted college/course and releases it back to the general seat pool. Candidate participates in the next round for strictly higher-preference choices only. If no higher seat is allotted later, candidate has NO claim on the rejected seat.

Mandatory Action: Seat Forfeited + Moves to Next Round (High Risk)
Reject & Withdraw Option 4
Exit COMEDK Counselling Completely

Candidate is completely unsatisfied with the seat and does not want to participate in any subsequent rounds of COMEDK counselling. Candidate exits the admission process permanently. No fees are paid, and the candidate cannot re-enter counselling in later rounds.

Mandatory Action: Complete Exit + Zero Further Participation

⚠️ Critical Warning Regarding Round 3 "Seat Blocking" Penalties

COMEDK implements stringent anti-seat blocking regulations. In Round 3 (the Final Round), there is NO "Accept & Upgrade" or "Reject & Upgrade" option. Any candidate participating in Round 3 who is allotted a seat MUST compulsory join the college. Seat cancellations or surrenders after Round 3 commencement are completely forbidden; the entire fee paid is forfeited, and COMEDK reserves the statutory right to impose an additional penalty of ₹2.0 Lakhs and file legal proceedings.

COMEDK 2027 Eligibility Criteria & Seat Categories

Review academic qualifications, PCM subject requirements, All India open seats, and designated linguistic and religious minority provisions.

📚

Academic & Subject Requirements

Candidates must have passed 10+2 / 2nd PUC or equivalent examination recognized by State/Central government. Physics and Mathematics are compulsory subjects, along with Chemistry, Bio-Technology, Biology, Computer Science, or Electronics as an optional subject. English must be passed as a compulsory language.

📊

Minimum Qualifying Marks in PCM

General Merit candidates must score a minimum aggregate of 45% marks in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics. For SC, ST, and OBC candidates of Karnataka state, the minimum qualifying mark is 40% aggregate. Outside-state reserved category students are evaluated under General Merit (45% threshold).

🇮🇳

All India Open Merit (GM)

COMEDK has no domicile restrictions for General Merit (GM) seats. Students from Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Delhi, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, Kerala, Andhra Pradesh, West Bengal, and all Indian states compete equally on the basis of their COMEDK UGET CBT rank for top private college seats.

🗣️

Linguistic Minority Quotas

Specific member institutions offer designated reservation for linguistic minorities: Tulu Minority (TMR) in colleges like NMAMIT Nitte and Sahyadri; Telugu Minority (LMR) in colleges like CMRIT Bengaluru, MVJ College, and East Point. Valid mother tongue documentation is mandatory.

✝️

Religious Minority Quota

Institutions established by Christian religious trusts (such as St. Joseph Engineering College, Mangaluru, and Father Muller) offer reserved Christian Minority (CHR/CMR) seats for Christian candidates holding a valid baptism certificate and community declaration.

📍

HKR (Article 371-J) Karnataka Quota

Candidates from the 7 backward districts of Kalyana-Karnataka (Bidar, Kalaburagi, Raichur, Koppal, Yadgir, Ballari, and Vijayanagara) holding a valid Annexure-A certificate issued by the Assistant Commissioner can claim Hyderabad-Karnataka Region (HKR) reservation seats.

ℹ️ Notice for Diploma Holders (Lateral Entry)

Candidates with a Diploma in Engineering / Technology are NOT eligible for 1st-year B.E. admission through COMEDK UGET. Diploma holders seeking 2nd-year lateral entry admissions into Karnataka engineering colleges must participate in the Karnataka Diploma Common Entrance Test (DCET) conducted separately by the KEA.

6-Stage COMEDK Counselling Roadmap

Follow the sequential roadmap from registration to physical campus reporting without missing vital deadlines.

Stage 1: Online Registration & Document Upload Registration Phase

Access candidate portal at comedk.org using your application number and password. Complete personal and academic details, upload clear scanned PDF/JPG copies of Class 10th marks card, Class 12th marksheet, original ID proof, and minority/HKR certificates (if applicable).

Stage 2: Centralized Online Verification Status Verification Phase

COMEDK verification officers audit each uploaded document against regulatory guidelines. Constantly monitor your dashboard for "Document Approved". If any document is flagged as "Resubmit", immediately upload the corrected document within the query resolution window to prevent cancellation.

Stage 3: Interactive Option Entry (Choice Filling) Choice Filling

Search through 190+ colleges by college code, name, or branch. Add as many choices as you wish (recommended: 70-120+ combinations). Arrange your dream colleges at the top, realistic choices in the middle, and guaranteed safe choices at the bottom.

Stage 4: Mock Allotment & Choice Refinement Trial Round

Review the indicative mock allotment result published by COMEDK. Use this simulation to gauge your real admission odds. You get a dedicated window to re-order, add, or delete choices before preferences are permanently locked for Round 1.

Stage 5: Real Seat Allotment & Mandatory Decision Seat Allocation

Check Round 1 real allotment. If allotted, select one of the four choices: Accept & Freeze (finalize admission), Accept & Upgrade (hold seat and try for higher choices), Reject & Upgrade, or Reject & Withdraw.

Stage 6: Online Fee Payment & Campus Reporting Final Admission

Complete the full online tuition fee payment using net banking, UPI, or credit/debit card. Download the official Online Allotment Letter & Fee Receipt. Visit the college campus in person with all original documents for physical verification and formal enrollment.

Smart Option Entry & Choice Ordering Tactics

Tactical advice from expert counsellors to maximize your chances of securing the best possible college and branch in COMEDK 2027.

🎯

The "Dream-Realistic-Safe" 3-Tier Rule

Divide your option list into three distinct segments: Tier 1 (Choices 1-30): Aspirational colleges and branches (RVCE CSE/ECE, BMSCE CSE, Ramaiah CSE); Tier 2 (Choices 31-70): Realistic options matching your actual rank range; Tier 3 (Choices 71-110+): Safe backup colleges to ensure you never go unallotted.

🔢

Add At Least 80–120 Choices

Unlike some entrance exams, COMEDK has no limit on the number of choices you can fill. Submitting only 5-10 choices is the most common reason qualified students end up with zero seat allotment. Take full advantage of unlimited choice filling by including all reputable private institutions.

⚖️

College Brand vs Branch Dilemma

Evaluate whether you prioritize college brand prestige (e.g. Mechanical/Chemical at RVCE or BMSCE) or branch specialization (e.g. CSE/AI at BIT, DSCE, or BMSIT). Top tech companies often hire across circuital branches (CSE, ISE, AI&DS, ECE), making circuital branches in top-10 colleges high-value targets.

💾

Save Regularly & Check Order Hierarchy

COMEDK system does not automatically re-sequence based on cutoff. If you accidentally place a lower-tier college as Choice #3 and RVCE as Choice #10, the system will permanently allocate Choice #3 if eligible! Always verify that every higher row is strictly more preferred than every lower row.
