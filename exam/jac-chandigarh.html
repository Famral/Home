JAC Chandigarh 2027 Overview & Key Highlights

Eligibility Criteria, Quota Rules & Domicile Verification

Admission eligibility in JAC Chandigarh is bifurcated into two distinct frameworks: Panjab University constituent teaching departments (open across India) and Chandigarh UT Administration institutions (featuring 85% Home State reservation).

🏛️ UIET, UICET & UIET Hoshiarpur (Panjab University)

Academic Requirement: Candidates must have passed 10+2 (Class 12) examination conducted by CBSE or any recognized state/central board with Physics, Mathematics, and Chemistry/Biotechnology/Biology/Technical Vocational subject along with English.

  • Minimum Aggregate: At least 60% marks in 10+2 aggregate (55% for SC/ST/PwD/BC).
  • Quota Structure: 100% All India Quota. There is no Chandigarh UT domicile reservation at UIET/UICET.
  • Merit Ranking: Based strictly on JEE Main 2027 Paper 1 All India CRL Rank.

🏢 CCET Chandigarh & CCA (Chandigarh Administration)

Academic Requirement: Candidates must have passed 10+2 with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects along with one of Chemistry/Biotechnology/Computer Science/Biology.

  • 85% Chandigarh UT Quota: Strictly determined by school location. Candidates who passed Class 12 from a recognized school located within the National Capital / Union Territory of Chandigarh qualify.
  • 15% All India Quota: Candidates passing Class 12 from schools outside Chandigarh compete under this quota.
  • Minimum Aggregate: Minimum 60% marks in 10+2 (55% for SC/ST/PwD).

📍 Chandigarh UT Quota (85%)

Exclusively available at CCET and CCA Chandigarh. You qualify if and only if your Class 12 examination school is situated within the territorial jurisdiction of Chandigarh UT. Domicile certificates, native ancestry, or parental residence are irrelevant.

🇮🇳 All India Quota (100% / 15%)

UIET Chandigarh, UICET, and UIET Hoshiarpur allocate 100% of seats to the All India pool. Additionally, CCET reserves 15% of seats for candidates from outside Chandigarh, creating a highly competitive nationwide admissions arena.

🎖️ PU Supernumerary Seats

Panjab University reserves special supernumerary seats: 2 additional seats per branch for Single Girl Child, 5% for Rural Area Students (RAS), 5% for Border Area Students (BAS), and dedicated sports seats evaluated via physical performance trials.

JAC Chandigarh 2027 Important Dates & Timeline

Projected schedule based on official Joint Admission Committee guidelines for the 2027 academic session. All deadlines on jacchd.admissions.nic.in are strictly time-bound.

Phase 1 • May 20 – June 18, 2027

Online Registration & Fee Submission

Portal opens at jacchd.admissions.nic.in. Candidates fill JEE Main details, academic scores, upload documents (caste, sports, defence, rural area), and pay non-refundable registration fee of ₹2,800 (₹1,400 for SC/ST/PwD).

Phase 2 • June 19 – June 24, 2027

Document Verification & Sports Trials

Online verification of uploaded certificates for reserved categories. Physical sports performance trials conducted by the Panjab University Directorate of Sports at the PU Gymnasium, Sector 14, Chandigarh, for sports quota applicants.

Phase 3 • June 26 – July 2, 2027

Online Choice Filling & Choice Locking

Eligible candidates submit ordered choices of participating institutes and engineering branches. Choices must be locked prior to deadline; system automatically auto-locks submitted choices at closing hour.

Phase 4 • July 4 – July 8, 2027

Round 1 Seat Allotment & SAF Fee Payment

Round 1 allotment published online. Allotted candidates must pay ₹40,000 Seat Acceptance Fee (SAF) and submit online willingness as FREEZE or FLOAT.

Phase 5 • July 11 – July 22, 2027

Round 2 & Round 3 Allotments & Seat Surrender

Progressive allotment rounds with branch upgrades for candidates on 'Float'. Final online withdrawal window to surrender seat and claim refund before commencement of the Special Round.

Phase 6 • July 25 – August 10, 2027

Special Round & Physical Spot Rounds

Special Round conducted online for remaining vacancies. Followed by in-person physical Spot Rounds at UIET Chandigarh campus, Sector 25, to fill any leftover seats.

Step-by-Step JAC Chandigarh 2027 Admission Roadmap

A comprehensive 6-stage chronological blueprint from JEE Main qualification to college orientation.

1️⃣ JEE Main Qualification

Appear for JEE Main 2027 Paper 1 (for B.E.) or Paper 2 (for B.Arch). Obtain your All India Common Rank List (CRL) score. JAC Chandigarh admissions are executed strictly based on CRL merit rank.

2️⃣ Centralized Registration

Register on jacchd.admissions.nic.in. Complete personal, demographic, and educational profile. Select qualifying examination school location (Chandigarh UT vs Other State) and pay registration fee online.

3️⃣ Strategic Choice Filling

Add preferred colleges and branches in exact descending order of preference. UIET Chandigarh CSE, IT, and CCET CSE are traditionally top choices. Review and lock your list before the cutoff deadline.

4️⃣ Seat Allotment & SAF Payment

Upon receiving a seat, pay the mandatory Seat Acceptance Fee (SAF) of ₹40,000 online. Failure to pay within the designated window will result in permanent seat cancellation and debarment from regular rounds.

5️⃣ Document Verification & Willingness

Submit required documents online for verification. Select Freeze to confirm your current allotment or Float to stay eligible for branch/college upgrades in higher rounds.

6️⃣ Physical Reporting / Spot Round

Report physically to the allotted institute at the end of counselling with original certificates and balance fees. If unallotted, attend the physical Spot Round conducted at UIET Chandigarh South Campus.

Panjab University Special Reservation Quotas & Fee Concessions

Panjab University features unique progressive reservation categories designed to empower rural students, border residents, single girl children, and distinguished sports athletes.

👧 Single Girl Child Quota

2 Supernumerary Seats per Branch: Panjab University reserves 2 additional seats in each B.E. branch at UIET Chandigarh and UICET exclusively for the only girl child of their parents (no siblings, or only one daughter with no other children).

Requires affidavit attested by Executive Magistrate.

🌾 Rural Area Students (RAS)

5% Reserved Seats: Candidates who have passed both Class 10 and Class 12 from recognized schools situated in designated rural areas of Punjab/UT. The school must not be within municipal or city limits.

Requires Rural School Certificate signed by District Education Officer.

🛡️ Border Area Students (BAS)

5% Reserved Seats: Eligible for candidates who completed both 10th and 12th schooling from educational institutions located within 20 kilometers of the international border.

Requires Border Area Certificate issued by Tehsildar/SDM.

🏅 Sports Category Trials

Mandatory PU Trials: Candidates claiming sports reservation must participate in mandatory physical performance trials conducted by the Directorate of Sports, Panjab University Gymnasium, Sector 14.

Gradation points are awarded based on trials and recognized tournament levels.

🎖️ Defence & Freedom Fighters

Priority Ranking: Available across UIET and CCET under standard Ministry of Defence priority levels (Priorities 1 to 9, covering widows/wards of armed forces personnel killed in action to ex-servicemen).

Requires Dependent Certificate from Zila Sainik Welfare Board.

🎓 Tuition Fee Waiver (TFW)

5% AICTE Supernumerary Seats: 100% tuition fee waiver for meritorious candidates whose family annual gross income from all sources is less than ₹8.0 Lakhs. Applicable at CCET and UIET.

Income certificate verified during registration.

Ensure you possess self-attested photocopies along with original documents ready for online verification and final institutional reporting.

📋 Category & Quota Specific Certificates

  • Chandigarh School Verification (for CCET 85% Quota): Certificate from the school principal confirming Class 12 was completed in UT Chandigarh.
  • UT Chandigarh OBC Certificate (for CCET): Must be in the UT Chandigarh OBC list and issued by SDM on or after April 1, 2026/2027 with valid Non-Creamy Layer (NCL) status.
  • EWS Certificate: Issued on or after April 1, 2026/2027 by a competent revenue authority.
  • SC / ST Caste Certificate: In the candidate's name issued by the competent district authority.
  • Single Girl Child Affidavit: Executed on judicial stamp paper attested by First Class Magistrate.
  • Rural Area / Border Area Certificate: Formats signed by DEO and SDM/Tehsildar.
  • Medical Fitness Certificate: Issued by a registered Government Medical Officer.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) — JAC Chandigarh 2027

Clear, authoritative answers to the most common queries regarding counselling procedures, cutoffs, reservations, and refund policies.

All admissions are based strictly on JEE Main All India CRL merit.
No. UIET Chandigarh, UICET, and UIET Hoshiarpur are constituent teaching departments of Panjab University and operate under a 100% All India Quota (AIQ). Candidates from Chandigarh, Punjab, Haryana, and all other states compete on an equal merit standing without state domicile reservations. Only CCET Chandigarh and CCA (under Chandigarh UT Administration) operate the 85% UT Chandigarh Quota and 15% All India Quota.
Eligibility for the 85% Chandigarh UT Quota at CCET and CCA is determined strictly by the location of the school or junior college where the candidate passed their qualifying Class 12 examination. If the school is situated inside the Union Territory of Chandigarh, the candidate is eligible for the 85% Chandigarh Quota, regardless of parental permanent residence.
The Seat Acceptance Fee (SAF) is ₹40,000, payable online upon being allotted a seat in any round. It is mandatory to pay this fee to maintain your allotment, even if you select 'Float' to seek higher branch upgrades. The ₹40,000 is adjusted towards your first-year tuition fee. If you wish to withdraw, you must submit a seat surrender request online through the portal before the designated withdrawal deadline (before Round 3 / Special Round) to receive a refund after deducting processing charges.
For General category candidates in the All India Pool, Computer Science & Engineering (CSE) at UIET Chandigarh typically closes between 48,000 and 53,000 CRL. Information Technology (IT) closes around 68,000 to 74,000 CRL. At CCET Chandigarh, CSE closes around 34,000 to 38,000 CRL for Other State candidates and around 68,000 to 74,000 CRL for Chandigarh Home State candidates. At UIET Hoshiarpur, CSE closes around 1,35,000 to 1,55,000 CRL.
Panjab University reserves 2 additional supernumerary seats in each B.E. branch at UIET Chandigarh and UICET exclusively for the single girl child of their parents (i.e. only one daughter with no other children). Candidates must submit a legal affidavit on non-judicial stamp paper attested by a First Class Magistrate affirming their single girl child status.
Panjab University provides 5% reserved seats for Rural Area Students (RAS) who have completed both Class 10 and Class 12 from recognized schools located in designated rural areas outside municipal limits. Similarly, 5% seats are reserved for Border Area Students (BAS) who passed 10th and 12th from schools situated within 20 kilometers of the international border. Official certificates signed by the DEO and Tehsildar/SDM are mandatory.
Applicants seeking sports reservation must upload valid tournament certificates and attend mandatory physical performance trials conducted by the Directorate of Sports at the Panjab University Gymnasium, Sector 14, Chandigarh. Candidates are evaluated on athletic trials and certified tournament levels (National/State/District) to generate a graded sports merit rank.
After paying the ₹40,000 SAF fee, selecting 'Freeze' locks your allotted college and branch permanently, withdrawing your application from further allotment rounds. Selecting 'Float' guarantees your currently allotted seat while permitting you to compete for higher preferences in subsequent rounds and the Special Round. If upgraded, your previous seat is automatically vacated; if not upgraded, your existing seat is 100% safe.
The Spot Round is conducted physically after Round 3 and the Special Round to fill leftover vacant seats across UIET, CCET, UICET, UIET Hoshiarpur, and CCA. Candidates who were not allotted seats earlier, withdrew earlier, or did not register earlier can participate by paying the spot registration fee. Candidates must report in person with original documents and a Demand Draft or online fee payment mode at the UIET Chandigarh campus, Sector 25, where seats are allocated strictly in order of JEE Main CRL rank.
