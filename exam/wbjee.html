WBJEE 2027 Overview & Key Highlights

The West Bengal Joint Entrance Examination (WBJEE) is the apex state-level gateway for undergraduate admissions into Engineering & Technology, Pharmacy, and Architecture programs across universities, government colleges, and self-financed institutes in West Bengal.

Exam Full Name West Bengal Joint Entrance Examination (WBJEE 2027)
Conducting Body West Bengal Joint Entrance Examinations Board (WBJEEB) (Statutory Board under Dept. of Higher Education, Govt. of West Bengal)
Official Portals wbjeeb.nic.in & wbjeeb.in
Courses Offered B.Tech / B.E. (4 Years), B.Pharm (4 Years), B.Arch (5 Years)
Exam Mode & Format Offline OMR-Based Pen-and-Paper Test (Two distinct sessions: Paper I & Paper II)
Total Marks & Duration 200 Marks (Mathematics: 100 Marks / 2 Hours; Physics & Chemistry: 100 Marks / 2 Hours)
Ranks Produced GMR (General Merit Rank): Based on Paper I + Paper II (200 marks) for all Engineering & JU Pharmacy.
PMR (Pharmacy Merit Rank): Based on Paper II only (100 marks) for Pharmacy colleges.
Participating Colleges 115+ Colleges (10 State Universities, 10 Govt Engineering Colleges, 95+ Private Unaided Institutes)
Flagship Institutions Jadavpur University (JU Kolkata), University of Calcutta (Rajabazar & Tech Campus), Kalyani Govt Engineering College (KGEC), Jalpaiguri Govt Engineering College (JGEC), IEM Kolkata, Heritage Institute of Technology
Admission Process Single-window centralized e-Counselling administered exclusively by WBJEEB via online choice filling, merit allotment, and seat confirmation
⚠️ Crucial Rule for Outside West Bengal (All India) Candidates
Candidates without West Bengal Domicile are strictly considered under the All India Quota (Open Category). They are eligible for 10% All India seats at Jadavpur University and 100% of seats in private self-financed engineering institutions (Heritage, IEM, Techno Main, Haldia, etc.). However, 100% state government engineering colleges (KGEC Kalyani, JGEC Jalpaiguri, GCELT, GCECT, GCETT) and state constitutional reservations are legally restricted to WB domicile holders.

Official WBJEEB Norms
Proforma A1

10-Year Continuous Residence

For candidates who have resided in the State of West Bengal continuously for at least 10 (ten) years as of 31st December 2026. Must be certified by an authorized administrative officer: District Magistrate (DM), Additional DM, SDO, BDO, or Commissioner of Police.

Proforma A2

10-Year Residence + 10+2 from WB

For candidates residing continuously in West Bengal for at least 10 years AND who have passed or appeared in the 10+2 Examination from any recognized school/institution in West Bengal. Certified directly by the Head of the Institution from where the candidate passed 10+2.

Proforma B

Parents Permanent Residents of WB

For candidates who have not resided in West Bengal continuously for 10 years, but whose father or mother is a permanent resident of West Bengal having a permanent address within the state. Must be certified by an administrative officer (DM, SDO, BDO, etc.) along with parents' domicile proof.

WBJEE 2027 Exam Pattern & Marking Scheme

WBJEE follows a unique 3-category marking scheme designed to reward conceptual depth and punish random guessing. Understanding the multi-correct category and negative marking structure is essential for rank maximization.

Paper I: Mathematics 100 Marks

Duration: 2 Hours (Morning Session: 11:00 AM – 1:00 PM) • 75 Questions

  • Category 1: 50 Questions • 1 Mark each • Negative: -0.25 mark
  • Category 2: 15 Questions • 2 Marks each • Negative: -0.50 mark
  • Category 3: 10 Questions • 2 Marks each • Multi-correct • No negative marking

Paper II: Physics & Chemistry 100 Marks

Duration: 2 Hours (Afternoon Session: 2:00 PM – 4:00 PM) • 80 Questions

  • Physics (50 Marks): 30 Qs in Cat-1 (1M), 5 Qs in Cat-2 (2M), 5 Qs in Cat-3 (2M)
  • Chemistry (50 Marks): 30 Qs in Cat-1 (1M), 5 Qs in Cat-2 (2M), 5 Qs in Cat-3 (2M)
  • Combined Time: 120 minutes for both Physics and Chemistry sections
Question Category Mark per Correct Answer Negative Marking per Incorrect Multi-Answer / Partial Marking Formula
Category 1 +1 Mark -0.25 Mark (-25%) Only one option is correct. Standard single choice MCQ.
Category 2 +2 Marks -0.50 Mark (-25%) Only one option is correct. Higher difficulty single choice MCQ.
Category 3 +2 Marks 0 (No Negative) One or more options are correct. If all correct marked: +2 Marks. If any wrong option marked: 0 Marks. Partial marks formula: 2 × (Correct Marked / Total Correct Options).
💡 Tie-Breaking Rule: Mathematics is King!
In WBJEE, if two candidates achieve the identical aggregate score out of 200, the candidate with higher positive marks in Mathematics is automatically ranked higher. If still tied, higher positive marks in Physics, followed by Chemistry, and then fewer negative marks in all subjects are used to break the tie.

Step-by-Step WBJEE e-Counselling Roadmap

A comprehensive walk-through of the complete centralized admission process from registration to physical campus reporting.

Stage 1

One-Time Online Registration

Eligible GMR/PMR rank holders register on wbjeeb.nic.in by authenticating their WBJEE Roll Number, Application Number, and Date of Birth. Provide bank account details (for refund if applicable) and pay the counselling participation fee.

Stage 2

Choice Filling & Logical Ordering

Select as many combinations of colleges and engineering branches as eligible. Arrange choices in true order of preference. Always place dream branches (e.g. Jadavpur University CSE/IT) at the top, regardless of rank, followed by safe options.

Stage 3

Mock Allotment & Mandatory Locking

WBJEEB publishes a Mock Seat Allotment showing tentative allotment trends based on submitted choices. Candidates can reorder choices before the locking window closes. Unlocked choices are automatically saved and locked at deadline.

Stage 4

Round 1 Allotment & ₹5,000 Fee

Allotted candidates must immediately download their Provisional Allotment Letter and pay the ₹5,000 online Seat Acceptance Fee. Failure to pay this fee results in instant seat cancellation and expulsion from Round 2.

Stage 5

Physical Document Verification

Report in person to the allotted college/reporting centre with original academic certificates, rank card, and Proforma A1/A2/B domicile proof. Upon successful verification, submit "NO for Upgradation" to freeze or "YES for Upgradation" to proceed.

Stage 6

Round 2 Upgradation & Mop-Up Round

Candidates who opted for Upgradation compete for higher choices in Round 2. If upgraded, old seat is relinquished; if not upgraded, old seat is safely kept. Unallotted candidates can enter the Mop-Up round with fresh choice filling.

Frequently Asked Questions (WBJEE 2027)

Authoritative answers to the most crucial questions regarding registration, cutoffs, seat acceptance, and counselling policies.

What is the official website for WBJEE 2027 Counselling? ▼
The official portal for WBJEE 2027 registration, exam schedules, and centralized e-counselling is wbjeeb.nic.in (also accessible via wbjeeb.in), maintained by the West Bengal Joint Entrance Examinations Board.
What is the difference between GMR and PMR in WBJEE? ▼
GMR (General Merit Rank) is calculated on your combined score in Paper 1 (Mathematics) and Paper 2 (Physics & Chemistry) out of 200 marks. It is mandatory for admission to all Engineering, Technology, and Architecture degree courses in all colleges, as well as Pharmacy at Jadavpur University.

PMR (Pharmacy Merit Rank) is prepared using marks scored only in Paper 2 (Physics & Chemistry out of 100 marks). PMR is used for admission to Bachelor of Pharmacy (B.Pharm) degree courses across all other pharmacy institutions in West Bengal.
How does the Jadavpur University 90% Home State Quota work? ▼
In Jadavpur University Faculty of Engineering & Technology, 90% of General category seats and 100% of all reserved category seats (SC, ST, OBC-A, OBC-B, PwD, EWS, TFW) are legally reserved for West Bengal Domicile candidates who produce valid Proforma A1, A2, or B. Only the remaining 10% of General category seats are open for All India (outside WB) candidates, resulting in exceptionally high cutoffs (typically top 250 GMR).
What happens if I do not pay the ₹5,000 Seat Acceptance Fee in Round 1? ▼
If a candidate is allotted any seat in Round 1 and does NOT pay the ₹5,000 online Seat Acceptance Fee within the prescribed window, their allotted seat is permanently cancelled AND the candidate is completely barred from Round 2. They cannot participate in Round 2 under any circumstance and can only re-enter during the Mop-Up round by paying an additional registration fee.
What is the difference between 'YES for Upgradation' and 'NO for Upgradation'? ▼
When you report for document verification after paying ₹5,000:
• NO for Upgradation: You freeze your allotted seat, decline further rounds, and take final admission at the institute.
• YES for Upgradation: You retain your currently allotted seat as a guaranteed safety fallback while competing for higher-preference choices in Round 2. If a higher choice is allotted in Round 2, your previous seat is automatically cancelled; if not, your earlier seat remains secured!
What are Proforma A1, A2, and B for West Bengal Domicile? ▼
• Proforma A1: For candidates residing continuously in West Bengal for at least 10 years as on Dec 31, 2026. Certified by administrative officers (DM, SDO, BDO).
• Proforma A2: For candidates residing in WB for 10 years AND who passed/appeared 10+2 in WB. Certified directly by the Head of the School/College.
• Proforma B: For candidates who have not lived in WB for 10 years, but whose father or mother is a permanent resident of West Bengal.
Can candidates from outside West Bengal apply through WBJEE? ▼
Yes! Outside-WB candidates participate under the All India Quota. They are eligible for: 1) 10% All India seats at Jadavpur University, 2) All seats in private self-financed engineering colleges (Heritage, IEM, Techno Main, Haldia), and 3) Self-financed university departments. However, they are NOT eligible for 100% state government engineering colleges (like KGEC, JGEC, GCELT, GCECT) or state category reservations.
What is the TFW (Tuition Fee Waiver) Scheme in WBJEE? ▼
Under the TFW scheme, 5% supernumerary seats are created across government, university, and private engineering institutions. 100% of the tuition fee is waived for candidates holding West Bengal Domicile whose annual parental income from all sources is less than ₹2.5 Lakhs. A valid TFW Income Certificate issued by an officer not below BDO/SDO is mandatory.
What are the specific 10+2 marks required for Jadavpur University? ▼
For admission to Jadavpur University engineering programs, a candidate must have passed 10+2 with at least 60% aggregate marks in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (45% for SC/ST, 54% for OBC-A/B, 45% for PwD), at least 60% in Mathematics individually (45% for SC/ST, 54% for OBC), and at least 30% marks in English.
What is the WBJEE Decentralized (Spot) Counselling Round? ▼
After WBJEEB concludes its centralized e-counselling rounds (Round 1, Round 2, and Mop-Up), any remaining vacant seats are filled through Decentralized (Spot) Counselling conducted independently by individual institutes such as Jadavpur University, KGEC, JGEC, and Calcutta University. Candidates apply directly on the college website based on their WBJEE GMR rank.
