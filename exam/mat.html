MAT 2027 Examination Blueprint & Authority Overview

The Management Aptitude Test (MAT) is an established national entrance examination administered by the All India Management Association (AIMA), New Delhi since 1988. Officially recognized by the Ministry of Education (Govt. of India) and AICTE as a national level test, MAT provides access to more than 600 AICTE-approved PGDM colleges and UGC-recognized university MBA departments nationwide.

MAT 2027 Testing Modes — CBT vs PBT vs IBT vs Double Mode

AIMA offers unique testing flexibility allowing aspirants to choose their preferred examination environment or opt for multiple modes within the same cycle to maximize scores and B-School choices.

💻

CBT Mode

Computer Based Test

Conducted at authorized digital test centers across 50+ major cities. Candidates interact with an on-screen digital interface equipped with a sectional timer and palette.

  • • Center-based standardized environment
  • • Quick question toggling & flagging
  • • Free dispatch to 5 Management Institutes
📝

PBT Mode

Paper Based Test

Traditional offline pen-and-paper format with printed test booklets and OMR response sheets administered at designated institutional centers nationwide.

  • • Ideal for candidates accustomed to paper
  • • Easy cross-referencing on physical pages
  • • Free dispatch to 5 Management Institutes
🏠

IBT Mode

Internet Based Test

Remotely proctored online test administered directly from the comfort of candidate's home using dual AI & human surveillance with a strict webcam/mic lock.

  • • No travel required • flexible slots
  • • Strict 1 Mbps net + webcam prerequisite
  • • Free dispatch to 5 Management Institutes
⚡

Double Mode

PBT+CBT / CBT+IBT

Allows candidates to take two different modes within the same testing cycle. B-Schools consider the higher composite score / percentile of the two attempts.

  • • Second shot in the same admission round
  • • Expanded free choice: 7 B-Schools
  • • Discounted combined registration fee
💡
Strategic Recommendation on Test Mode:

If you want to maximize your chances and send scores to more premier colleges without paying additional fees, choose the Double Mode (PBT + CBT or CBT + IBT). You gain an extra attempt to raise your score and 2 additional free B-School applications (7 total instead of 5).

Academic Qualification

Graduation in any discipline from a recognized University or Institute. Minimum aggregate: 50% marks for General category and 45% marks for SC, ST, and PwD categories. Final year undergraduate students are also eligible on a provisional basis.

No Age or Attempt Caps

Work Experience & Diversity

Work experience is not compulsory for taking MAT. However, candidates with 1 to 3 years of relevant post-graduation corporate experience receive up to 5–10% bonus weightage during GD/PI rounds at premier PGDM colleges like BIMTECH and XIME.

New MAT 2027 Exam Pattern & Sectional Blueprint

AIMA has transitioned MAT to a streamlined 150-question 120-minute architecture designed to evaluate managerial aptitude across 5 balanced dimensions.

New Streamlined Exam Architecture 120 Minutes

Examination Mode CBT (Online Center), PBT (OMR Center) & IBT (Home Proctored)
Total Duration 120 Minutes (2 Hours) • No Sectional Time Limits
Total Questions 150 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)
Number of Sections 5 Sections (30 Questions Each)
Marking Scheme +1 Mark for Correct • -0.25 Mark for Incorrect
Unattempted Questions 0 Marks (No negative marking for skipping)
Composite Score Scaling 199 to 801 Scale (Calculated on 120 questions from first 4 sections)
GK Section Policy Scored separately; not included in Composite Score or Percentile

5 Core Sections Breakdown 30 Qs Each

1. Language Comprehension 30 Qs • In Composite

Reading Comprehension passages, vocabulary, grammar, para jumbles, sentence correction.

2. Intelligence & Critical Reasoning 30 Qs • In Composite

Blood relations, series, analogy, syllogisms, cause and effect, assertion-reasoning, puzzles.

3. Mathematical Skills (Quantitative) 30 Qs • In Composite

Arithmetic (profit-loss, time-work, percentages, ratio), Algebra, Geometry, Mensuration.

4. Data Analysis & Sufficiency 30 Qs • In Composite

Tables, bar graphs, pie charts, line graphs, Venn diagrams, data sufficiency problems.

5. Indian & Global Environment (GK) 30 Qs • Scored Separately

Business current affairs, economy, national organizations, corporate leaders, international news.

⚠️ Crucial Strategy Insight on the Indian & Global Environment (GK) Section

Many candidates waste valuable time attempting all 30 questions in the GK section. Remember: the Indian & Global Environment section score is NOT counted in your MAT Composite Score (199–801) or your overall Percentile. Spend the majority of your 120 minutes securing maximum accuracy in the first 4 sections (120 questions) to drive your percentile above 90!

Step-by-Step B-School Admission Roadmap Through MAT

Follow the sequential pathway from AIMA MAT online registration to test day, B-School shortlisting, GD-PI evaluation, and final MBA / PGDM enrollment.

Step 1: AIMA Online Registration & Testing Mode Selection

Register on mat.aima.in during the registration window of your chosen phase. Complete your candidate profile with personal and academic details, upload clear passport photo and signature, select your desired testing mode (CBT, PBT, IBT, or Double Mode), and select up to 5 Management Institutes (7 for double mode) for automatic free score reporting.

Step 2: Admit Card Download & Test Day Execution

Download the official MAT admit card. For PBT and CBT, arrive at your test center with printed hall tickets and original photo ID. For IBT, ensure a functional webcam, microphone, and a quiet, well-lit room. Attempt 150 questions across the 5 sections in 120 minutes with zero sectional time caps.

Step 3: Scorecard Publication & Composite Score Verification

Download your AIMA MAT Scorecard. Review your sectional scaled marks (0–100), overall Composite Score (199–801), and national percentile rank. AIMA will simultaneously transmit your verified scores directly to your selected B-Schools.

Step 4: B-School Application & Shortlisting Calls

In addition to the 5 automatic reporting choices, submit individual application forms on the websites of target business schools (e.g., BIMTECH, XIME, Jaipuria, JIMS). B-Schools release their cutoff lists and call shortlisted candidates for stage 2 evaluation.

Step 5: Group Discussion (GD), WAT & Personal Interview (PI)

Attend the selection rounds conducted either on-campus or virtually. Prepare for current business affairs, abstract GD topics, case discussions, written essays (WAT), and personal interview questions touching on your career goals, graduation subjects, and work experience.

Step 6: Final Composite Merit List & Seat Confirmation

B-Schools compute a final composite merit score weighting your MAT score (30–50%), GD-PI performance (30–40%), academic consistency (10–20%), and work experience (5–10%). Receive your provisional admission offer letter and remit the seat confirmation deposit.

Frequently Asked Questions — MAT 2027

Clear, authoritative answers to common questions regarding AIMA MAT testing modes, exam pattern, Composite Score calculation, cutoffs, and B-School selection.

What is MAT and who conducts the examination? +
The Management Aptitude Test (MAT) is a national-level standardized entrance examination conducted by the All India Management Association (AIMA), New Delhi. Recognized by the Ministry of Education (Govt. of India) and AICTE, MAT scores are accepted by more than 600 business schools and universities across India for admission into MBA, PGDM, and allied management programs.
What is the new 150-question exam pattern and duration for MAT 2027? +
AIMA has streamlined MAT from the earlier 200-question format to a modern 120-minute (2 hours) test comprising 150 multiple choice questions. The exam contains 5 equal sections with 30 questions each: Language Comprehension, Intelligence & Critical Reasoning, Mathematical Skills, Data Analysis & Sufficiency, and Indian & Global Environment. Each correct response earns +1 mark, while -0.25 mark is deducted for each incorrect answer.
How is the MAT Composite Score calculated, and is the GK section included? +
The MAT Composite Score is reported on a standardized scale ranging from 199 to 801. It is computed exclusively on the basis of performance in the first 4 sections (120 questions). The 5th section (Indian & Global Environment / GK) is scored separately and is NOT included in the Composite Score or overall Percentile. However, select B-Schools may look at your GK score during the Personal Interview round.
What is the difference between PBT, CBT, and IBT modes in MAT? +
Paper Based Test (PBT) is conducted in an offline classroom setting using printed test booklets and OMR sheets. Computer Based Test (CBT) is an online computer-administered exam conducted at authorized testing labs. Internet Based Test (IBT) is an online remote-proctored examination taken from home on a personal laptop or desktop equipped with a webcam and stable internet. Candidates can also take a Double Mode (e.g., PBT + CBT) to take two tests in the same cycle and choose 7 free B-Schools.
What percentile in MAT is required for top colleges like SIMSREE, PUMBA, and BIMTECH? +
For top government university departments like SIMSREE Mumbai and PUMBA Pune, General category All India candidates generally require a 95 to 99.5+ percentile (Composite Score 700+). For premier private business schools like BIMTECH Greater Noida, XIME Bangalore, and Jaipuria Institute of Management, cutoffs generally range between 75 and 85 percentile (Composite Score 600 to 680).
Can final year graduation students appear for MAT 2027? +
Yes. Final year undergraduate students enrolled in Bachelor's degrees in any discipline (B.A., B.Sc., B.Com., B.Tech, BBA, etc.) are eligible to take MAT on a provisional basis. They must complete all degree examinations and secure at least 50% aggregate marks (45% for SC/ST/PwD) prior to final enrollment.
How many times in a year is MAT conducted and how long is the score valid? +
MAT is conducted 4 times a year in February, May, September, and December. Each MAT score remains valid for one academic year. If a candidate appears in multiple phases, B-Schools generally accept the best or most recent score as per their individual admission criteria.
What is the difference between an MBA and a PGDM through MAT? +
An MBA (Master of Business Administration) is an academic degree awarded by universities or their affiliated colleges (e.g., PUMBA Pune, Christ University). A PGDM (Post Graduate Diploma in Management) is a diploma awarded by autonomous, AICTE-approved institutions (e.g., BIMTECH Greater Noida, XIME Bangalore). Both credentials have equal corporate recognition and AIU (Association of Indian Universities) equivalence for higher studies and employment.
