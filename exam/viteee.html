Payment of remaining tuition and hostel fees, submission of physical verification certificates, and commencement of B.Tech 2027 freshmen orientation at respective campuses.

VITEEE 2027 Eligibility Criteria & Group A / Group B Rules

Review academic qualification rules, age threshold limits, nationality regulations, and determine whether your target branch falls under Group A (core) or Group B (computing/circuits).

🎓 10+2 Academic Aggregate Threshold

Candidates must pass the 10+2 Higher Secondary Board Exam with a minimum 60% aggregate in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics/Biology (PCM / PCB). For SC/ST candidates and residents of J&K, Ladakh, and North-Eastern States, the minimum required aggregate is 50%.

🎂 Age Limit & Date of Birth

Candidates whose date of birth falls on or after July 1, 2005 (for 2027 admissions, on or after July 1, 2005 / 2006) are eligible. Date of birth recorded in the High School / SSC certificate is accepted as definitive proof.

🔬 PCB Eligibility for Biotechnology

Candidates with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology (PCB) in 10+2 are eligible for admission into B.Tech Biotechnology, Biomedical Engineering, and Bioengineering at VIT Vellore and VIT-AP, provided they qualify in the BPCEA paper.

🌍 Nationality & NRI Admission

Resident Indian citizens must appear for VITEEE. Non-Resident Indians (NRIs), PIO, and Foreign Nationals can apply directly under the NRI/Foreign category based on SAT / Class 12 board merit without appearing for VITEEE.

⏱️ Time Allocation Strategy

With 125 questions in 150 minutes, candidates have 72 seconds per question. Allocate 55 mins for Maths, 40 mins for Physics, 35 mins for Chemistry, and 20 mins for Aptitude & English.

⚡ Zero Penalty Advantage

Because there is no negative marking, never leave questions blank. Spend the final 5 minutes making educated guesses on any remaining unattempted questions to maximize raw aggregate score.

🧩 Easy Pickings: Aptitude & English

The 15 questions in Aptitude (10) and English (5) are scoring gems that take less than 15 minutes and often separate rank brackets by thousands of positions.

📝 ₹5,900 Non-Refundable Fee

To unlock choice filling, candidates must pay a one-time non-refundable counselling fee of ₹5,900. Candidates eligible for Phase 1 who do not register lose priority for early category allotments.

💳 ₹1,00,000 Seat Advance Fee

Frequently Asked Questions (VITEEE 2027)

Find authoritative, legally accurate answers to the most common queries regarding VITEEE application deadlines, fee category slabs, negative marking, scholarships, and counselling.

VITEEE (VIT Engineering Entrance Examination) is the centralized common entrance exam conducted by Vellore Institute of Technology for admissions into 4-year undergraduate B.Tech programs across its 4 premier campuses: VIT Vellore (Main Campus, Tamil Nadu), VIT Chennai (Tamil Nadu), VIT-AP (Amaravati, Andhra Pradesh), and VIT Bhopal (Madhya Pradesh). Admissions to B.Tech are strictly through VITEEE rank merit.
VIT allocates seats across 5 distinct tuition fee categories based purely on VITEEE rank merit. The syllabus, classrooms, faculty, hostel, and placements are 100% identical for all categories. Category 1 offers the lowest subsidised tuition fee (~₹1.98 Lakhs/year for Group B programs like CSE), while Categories 2, 3, 4, and 5 have progressively higher fee structures up to ~₹4.93 Lakhs/year, allowing candidates with moderate ranks to secure competitive branches by opting for higher fee slabs.
No. VITEEE has zero negative marking. Candidates receive +1 mark for each correct answer, and 0 marks for unattempted or incorrect responses. The exam comprises 125 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) for 125 total marks, allowing candidates to attempt all questions safely without penalisation.
To secure B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering (CSE Core) at VIT Vellore under Category 1, a candidate generally needs a VITEEE rank between 1 and 7,500. For Category 2, cutoffs extend to approximately 14,000; Category 3 up to 20,000; Category 4 up to 24,000; and Category 5 up to 28,000.
No. For VIT Vellore and VIT Chennai campuses, admission to all B.Tech degree programs is strictly determined by VITEEE merit ranks; JEE Main scores are not considered. However, in certain years, VIT-AP and VIT Bhopal offer a separate quota or admission pathway based on JEE Main CRL percentiles or state board merit after VITEEE counselling rounds conclude.
VIT classifies engineering branches into two groups for fee determination: Group A includes foundational and traditional engineering branches such as Civil, Mechanical, Chemical, Electrical & Electronics (EEE), and Biotechnology (Category 1 fee ~₹1.73 Lakhs/year). Group B includes high-demand computer and circuit branches like Computer Science (CSE), CSE specializations (AI/ML, Data Science, Cyber Security), Information Technology (IT), and Electronics & Communication (ECE) (Category 1 fee ~₹1.98 Lakhs/year).
Candidates must secure an aggregate of at least 60% marks in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics/Biology (PCM/PCB) in their Class 12 / Higher Secondary qualifying board examination. A relaxed threshold of 50% aggregate in PCM/PCB applies for candidates belonging to SC/ST categories, as well as candidates hailing from Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh, and North-Eastern states.
The Support The Advancement of Rural Students (STARS) scheme is a prestigious philanthropic initiative by VIT that provides a 100% tuition fee waiver, along with free hostel accommodation and food, to the top-ranking boy and girl students from rural government higher secondary schools in every district of Tamil Nadu and Andhra Pradesh.
VIT conducts online counselling in 5 sequential phases strictly segregated by rank: Phase 1 (Ranks 1–20,000), Phase 2 (20,001–45,000), Phase 3 (45,001–70,000), Phase 4 (70,001–1,00,000), and Phase 5 (1,00,001–1,50,000). Candidates eligible in earlier phases have exclusive first access to available branch and fee category combinations across campuses. Higher-ranked candidates who miss their phase can participate in later phases, but cannot claim already filled seats.
Yes. VIT operates a centralized campus placement cell known as PAT (Placement and Training Centre). Students from VIT Vellore, VIT Chennai, VIT-AP, and VIT Bhopal are eligible to participate in the unified placement recruitment drives where top global recruiters (including Microsoft, Amazon, Google, Intel, TCS, and Deloitte) hire graduates based on technical competence and CGPA.
