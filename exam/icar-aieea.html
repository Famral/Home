Agriculture Admission Architecture

ICAR AIEEA 2027 Examination & National Agricultural Admission Ecosystem

The Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR) is the apex national body coordinating agricultural education and research in India. Through the ICAR All India Quota (AIQ), meritorious students secure entry into accredited Bachelor's, Master's, and Doctoral programs across 4 prestigious ICAR Deemed Universities, 3 Central Agricultural Universities, and over 65 State Agricultural Universities (SAUs).

ICAR AIEEA (UG) via CUET

Admission to 11 undergraduate agricultural degree programs (B.Sc. Agriculture, Horticulture, Forestry, B.Tech Agricultural Engineering, etc.) is conducted purely on the basis of CUET (UG) 2027 domain subject percentiles. ICAR manages the centralized seat allocation via icaraieea.net.

ICAR AIEEA (PG) CBT

For Master's degree programs (M.Sc., M.V.Sc., M.Tech, M.F.Sc.), NTA conducts an exclusive national-level Computer-Based Test (AIEEA-PG) featuring 120 specialized multiple-choice questions across 20 core major subject groups along with ICAR-PG Scholarship awards.

National Talent Scholarship (NTS)

Every candidate admitted through ICAR All-India Quota into an accredited agricultural university located outside their state of domicile automatically receives the prestigious ₹3,000/month NTS (UG) or ₹5,000/month NTS (PG) for their entire academic tenure.

Crucial Update for UG Aspirants: No Separate ICAR UG Exam

Candidates targeting B.Sc. Agriculture, B.Tech Agri Engineering, and allied science degrees for 2027 must register for CUET (UG) 2027 on the NTA portal (exams.nta.ac.in/CUET-UG/) and select "Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR)" as a participating university, choosing eligible domain subjects (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology, Agriculture).

Academic Requirements

ICAR Eligibility Criteria & Class 12 Domain Mapping Rules

Failure to comply with ICAR's strict subject mapping guidelines is the #1 reason candidates get disqualified during seat allotment. Review the mandatory rules before selecting your CUET test subjects.

Strict ICAR Rule: 10+2 Subject Alignment is Non-Negotiable

Candidates MUST appear in CUET (UG) in the same domain subjects that they passed in their Class 12 Board examination. If an applicant who passed 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology appears in Agriculture domain without studying it in Class 12, ICAR will reject their seat allotment during document verification!

General Eligibility & Age Criteria

  • Age Limit: Candidate must be at least 16 years of age as of 31st August 2027 for UG admission. No upper age limit.
  • Qualifying Exam: 10+2 / Senior Secondary examination from a recognized Indian Board (CBSE, ICSE, or State Board).
  • Minimum Aggregate Marks: At least 50% marks in aggregate for General, OBC (NCL), UPS, and EWS categories; 40% aggregate for SC, ST, PwD, and Transgender candidates.
  • Accreditation Rule (PG): For AIEEA-PG, candidates must have graduated from an ICAR-accredited college / program in an accredited university.

Permitted 3-Subject CUET Combinations

  • PCB (Physics, Chemistry, Biology): Eligible for B.Sc. Agriculture, Horticulture, Forestry, Fisheries (B.F.Sc.), Community Science, Food Nutrition, Biotechnology.
  • PCM (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics): Eligible for ALL 11 degrees including B.Tech Agricultural Engineering, B.Tech Dairy Tech, B.Tech Food Tech.
  • ABC (Agriculture, Biology, Chemistry): Eligible for B.Sc. Agriculture, Horticulture, Forestry, Fisheries (only if studied Agriculture in 10+2).
  • PCA (Physics, Chemistry, Agriculture): Eligible for B.Sc. Agriculture, Horticulture, Forestry, Community Science.

Academic Programs

Undergraduate Degrees Offered via ICAR: Curriculum & Career Scope

Explore the 11 specialized 4-year Bachelor's degree programs, their industry demand, civil services eligibility, and private sector placement opportunities.

B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture

Duration: 4 Years (8 Semesters)
Core Areas: Agronomy, soil science, plant breeding, entomology, plant pathology, agricultural economics, extension education, and the mandatory 1-semester Rural Agricultural Work Experience (RAWE).
Career Scope: UPSC IFS, IBPS Agricultural Field Officer (AFO), State Agriculture Officers (AO/AAO), seed & pesticide MNCs (Bayer, Syngenta, UPL), NABARD, and agritech startups.

B.Sc. (Hons.) Horticulture

Duration: 4 Years
Core Areas: Pomology (fruits), olericulture (vegetables), floriculture & landscaping, post-harvest handling, spices, plantation crops, and protected cultivation (greenhouse technology).
Career Scope: Horticulture Development Officers, commercial orchard management, floral export houses, landscape architects, and cold chain logistics.

B.Tech Agricultural Engineering

Duration: 4 Years (PCM Mandatory)
Core Areas: Farm machinery & power, soil & water conservation engineering, micro-irrigation systems, renewable energy, and post-harvest food engineering.
Career Scope: Tractor MNCs (Mahindra, John Deere, Tafe), drip irrigation giants (Jain Irrigation, Netafim), food processing equipment design, and GATE-Agri entry into PSUs.

B.Tech Dairy Technology

Duration: 4 Years (PCM Mandatory)
Core Areas: Dairy chemistry, dairy microbiology, dairy engineering, cheese/butter/ice cream processing technology, quality assurance, and packaging.
Career Scope: NDRI Karnal campus placements, Amul (GCMMF), Mother Dairy, Nestlé, Danone, Britannia, and State Dairy Federations (Nandini, Verka, Saras).

B.F.Sc. Fisheries Science

Duration: 4 Years
Core Areas: Aquaculture, fish genetics & breeding, aquatic animal health, fisheries resource management, fish processing, and marine biology.
Career Scope: Fisheries Development Officers, coastal aquaculture farms, seafood processing & export inspection agencies (EIA/MPEDA), and feed manufacturers.

B.Sc. (Hons.) Forestry

Duration: 4 Years
Core Areas: Silviculture, forest mensuration, wildlife management, wood science, agroforestry, forest protection, and environmental conservation policies.
Career Scope: Direct eligibility advantage in Indian Forest Service (IFS) and State Forest Service (SFS/RFO) examinations, environmental consulting, and wood product industries.

Step-by-Step Procedure

ICAR Centralized Online Counselling Roadmap: Rounds 1 to Mop-Up

A comprehensive visual guide detailing how seats are allotted across State and Central Agricultural Universities via icaraieea.net.

Step 1: Registration on Official ICAR Portal & Fee Payment

Following the declaration of CUET (UG) / AIEEA (PG) results by NTA, eligible candidates must register on the ICAR counselling portal (icaraieea.net) by paying a non-refundable registration fee (typically ₹500). Candidates generate their login credentials.

Step 2: Choice Filling & University Preference Locking

Candidates select their desired courses and university preferences in priority order. Golden Rule: Fill preferences in strict descending order of university reputation (e.g., IARI New Delhi → PAU Ludhiana → GBPUAT → BHU → TNAU → UAS Bangalore). Always lock choices before the published deadline.

Step 3: Round 1 Seat Allotment Result Declaration

ICAR processes choices based on All-India Overall Rank, reservation category, and seat availability. Allotted candidates receive an online Provisional Allotment Letter specifying the institute, campus, and degree allotted.

Step 4: Seat Acceptance Fee (₹10,000) & Document Upload

To secure the allotted seat, the candidate MUST deposit a Seat Acceptance Fee of ₹10,000 online and upload scanned copies of Class 10/12 marksheets, caste certificates, and identity proof. The admitting university's nodal officer conducts online document verification.

Step 5: Exercising Upgradation (Float) vs. Freeze

Candidates satisfied with their allotment choose "Freeze" to confirm admission. Candidates hoping for a higher-preference university in subsequent rounds select "Upgradation". If upgraded in Round 2, the earlier seat is automatically cancelled and transferred to another candidate.

Step 6: Subsequent Rounds & Mop-Up (Stray Vacancy)

Rounds 2, 3, and 4 proceed similarly. Remaining vacant seats across universities are pooled into the Final Mop-Up Round, where previously unallotted or newly registered candidates can apply for remaining vacancies.

Step 7: Physical Reporting & Balance Fee Payment

After final round allocation, students report physically to their allotted university campus with original certificates, medical fitness certificates, and settle the remaining annual tuition/hostel fees. The ₹10,000 seat acceptance fee is adjusted into the course fee.

Mandatory Documents Checklist for ICAR Verification

  • NTA CUET (UG) / AIEEA (PG) 2027 Scorecard
  • ICAR Provisional Seat Allotment Letter

Financial Assistance

ICAR National Talent Scholarship (NTS) & Fellowships

Understand how the ICAR National Talent Scholarship works, who qualifies, the critical non-home state domicile requirement, and renewal conditions.

NTS for Undergraduate (UG) Students

₹3,000 / Month

Awarded to every undergraduate student who is admitted through the ICAR All-India Quota into an accredited agricultural university located in a state other than their home state.

  • Tenure: Full 4 years (8 semesters) of the Bachelor's degree program.
  • Total Value: ₹36,000 annually (~₹1.44 Lakh over the degree).
  • Domicile Clause: If a student from Punjab gets admission in PAU Ludhiana, they do NOT get NTS. But if they take admission in GBPUAT Uttarakhand or UAS Bangalore, they are eligible!
  • Renewal Rule: Must maintain a minimum OGPA of 7.0 out of 10.0 (6.5 for SC/ST/PwD) with no failing grades or conduct infractions.

NTS & Scholarships for PG Students

₹5,000 to ₹12,640 / Month

Postgraduate scholars admitted through ICAR AIEEA-PG qualify for substantial monthly stipends to support advanced agricultural research.

  • NTS (PG): ₹5,000 per month for Master's students admitted in an agricultural university outside their home state.
  • ICAR-PG Scholarship: Awarded to top merit rank holders across 20 subject groups: ₹12,640 per month plus ₹6,000 annual contingency grant.
  • AICE JRF/SRF (Ph.D.): Doctoral scholars receive ₹31,000/month for the first 2 years (JRF) and ₹35,000/month for the 3rd year (SRF) plus contingency grants.
