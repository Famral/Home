💡 CEE Kerala Single Window System (CAP)

The Centralized Allotment Process (CAP) ensures that candidates need not apply individually to multiple colleges. Through a single unified candidate portal, applicants can register and rearrange unlimited course-college combinations. Allotments are generated purely on merit rank, category reservations, and candidate preference hierarchies.

The 4 Cardinal Rules of KEAM Centralized Allotment (CAP)

CEE Kerala operates a strict single-window algorithmic system. Understanding these four golden rules will prevent accidental seat cancellation and permanent disqualification.

⚠️

Rule 1: Irreversible Deletion of Lower Options

Most Critical Rule
When CEE allots a seat at your Option #N, all lower priority options from N+1 to the end are permanently and irreversibly deleted from your profile!
  • You can NEVER reclaim or request any option lower in serial number than your current allotment.
  • Never insert filler or unwanted college-branch choices below good ones just to have options.
  • Only options 1 through N-1 remain alive for subsequent allotment phases.
💳

Rule 2: Token Fee Remittance is Mandatory

Seat Protection
Allotment alone does NOT hold a seat. You MUST remit the prescribed token fee to CEE Kerala online or via designated post offices before the deadline.
  • Failure to pay fee before the deadline cancels the allotted seat immediately.
  • Disaster warning: Unpaid candidates lose ALL remaining options and are booted from further regular CAP rounds!
  • Once paid, the fee is adjusted against your college tuition fee at admission.
🔄

Rule 3: Higher Option Retention & Deletion

Upgrade Strategy
If you remit the token fee in Phase 1, you can choose to retain your higher preferences (1 to N-1) for Phase 2.
  • If upgraded to a higher choice in Phase 2, your earlier Phase 1 seat is automatically released to another candidate.
  • You cannot say "I want my Phase 1 college back." The upgrade is final!
  • If satisfied with your Phase 1 allotment, manually delete all higher options before Phase 2 processing begins.
🧪

Rule 4: Trial Allotment is a Practice Run

Risk-Free Sandbox
CEE Kerala publishes a simulated Trial Allotment approximately 2-3 days before Phase 1 allotment locks.
  • Trial allotment conveys NO legal right to the seat and requires NO fee remittance.
  • Use trial allotment results to analyze if your choices are positioned too high or too low.
  • You have a dedicated window post-trial allotment to add, delete, or rearrange priorities!

🎓 AICTE Tuition Fee Waiver (TFW) Scheme in KEAM

Under the AICTE TFW scheme, 5% supernumerary seats are created over and above the sanctioned intake in each branch. Candidates whose total family annual income from all sources is less than ₹8,00,000 (Rupees Eight Lakhs) are automatically considered for 100% tuition waiver seats strictly in order of their KEAM Engineering Merit Rank. Make sure to upload a valid Income Certificate during the CEE profile verification window!

6-Stage KEAM CAP Counselling Roadmap

Follow this structured step-by-step master plan from initial option entry to final physical college induction.

Stage 1

Plus Two Marks Submission & 50:50 Rank Generation

After taking the KEAM CBT entrance exam, candidates must log into the CEE portal to upload their Class 12 / Plus Two marksheets. CEE standardizes board marks across State Board, CBSE, and CISCE using statistical mean and standard deviation, combining them 50:50 with CBT entrance marks to release the official KEAM Engineering Rank List.

Stage 2

Centralized Online Option Registration (Choice Entry)

Log into candidate portal using Application Number and Password. Browse participating colleges, courses, and fee categories. Add choices and arrange them in descending order of actual preference (1 being dream choice). There is NO upper limit on the number of options you can register.

Stage 3

Trial Allotment Simulation & Hierarchy Refinement

CEE publishes a simulated Trial Allotment to demonstrate what seat you would obtain based on your registered options. Analyze whether you received an allotment. If you got a college too low or none at all, utilize the 48-hour revision window to add more backup colleges and resequence your priority list.

Stage 4

Phase 1 Seat Allotment & Mandatory Token Fee Remittance

Download your Phase 1 Allotment Memo. If allotted a seat, you MUST pay the prescribed token fee online to CEE Kerala before the declared deadline. If you do not pay the fee, your seat is cancelled AND all registered options are permanently deleted!

Stage 5

Phase 2 Option Filtering & Upgradation

All choices lower than your Phase 1 allotment are automatically erased. If you wish to upgrade, retain higher options. If satisfied with your Phase 1 seat, delete all higher options so you are not automatically shifted. Phase 2 allotment is processed, and fees are automatically adjusted for upgraded seats.

Stage 6

Phase 3 Final Allotment & Physical College Reporting

Download your final Allotment Memo and Fee Remittance Receipt. Report in person to the allotted college campus with original certificates, Transfer Certificate (TC), Conduct Certificate, and physical fitness report. Pay any remaining institutional / hostel fees to confirm enrollment.
