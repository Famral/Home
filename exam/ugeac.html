UGEAC Bihar 2027: Master Blueprint & Counselling Overview

The Under Graduate Engineering Admission Counselling (UGEAC) is the single centralized state counseling portal administered by the Bihar Combined Entrance Competitive Examination Board (BCECEB) for admission into 4-year undergraduate Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) degree programs across all 38 Government Engineering Colleges in Bihar.

UGEAC Bihar 2027 Eligibility Criteria & Domicile Norms

Admission through UGEAC requires fulfilling specific academic, entrance exam, and state residency norms set by the Department of Science & Technology, Bihar.

Academic Qualifications

  • Passed 10+2 / Intermediate examination with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects.
  • Any one optional subject: Chemistry, Biotechnology, Biology, Technical Vocational subject, Computer Science, IT, or Informatics.
  • Minimum aggregate marks: 45% for General (UR) / EWS, and 40% for SC, ST, EBC, BC, and PwD (DQ) candidates.

Bihar Domicile Rules

  • Parents are permanent residents (Mool Niwasi) of Bihar.
  • Wards of Bihar State Government employees or employees on deputation in Bihar.
  • Wards of Central Govt / Defense / Railway / PSU personnel stationed in Bihar.
  • Wards of UN / International organization officials posted in Bihar at registration.

Exam & Age Requirements

  • Mandatory JEE Main Paper-1: Must have appeared in NTA JEE (Main) 2027 and obtained a valid All India CRL rank.
  • No Upper Age Limit: Candidates of any age can apply for 4-year B.Tech admissions in Bihar state colleges.
  • Medical Fitness: Certified medically fit by an authorized government civil surgeon at admission.

Special & Horizontal Quotas

  • 33% Horizontal Reservation for Girls: One-third of seats in EVERY category (UR, EBC, BC, SC, ST, EWS) are reserved exclusively for female candidates. If female seats in a category remain vacant, they are filled by male candidates of that same category.
  • Differently Abled Quota (DQ - 5%): Horizontal reservation for candidates with 40%+ physical disability verified by a designated medical board.
  • Serviceman Quota (SMQ - 1%): Reserved for sons/daughters of military personnel from Bihar killed in action or permanently disabled.
  • Tuition Fee Waiver (TFW - 5% Supernumerary): 5% additional seats per branch offering 100% tuition waiver for students with annual parental income below ₹8 Lakhs.
Note on Leftover Seats: Any seats in Government Engineering Colleges that remain unfilled after the final round of UGEAC are transferred to BCECE (PCM) merit counseling.

Strategic Choice Filling & Upgradation Simulator

In UGEAC online choice filling, there is no limit to the number of preferences you can submit. Follow our golden sequence strategy to maximize your odds of securing high-demand branches like CSE, AI/ML, and ECE.

🏆 Golden Choice Preference Order for Bihar B.Tech

Sequence your choices strictly in decreasing order of institutional prestige, academic excellence, and campus placement records:

1. Premier Flagship Tier: MIT Muzaffarpur (CSE → ECE → IT → EE) • BCE Bhagalpur (CSE → ECE → EE).
2. Capital Region Tier 1: Bakhtiyarpur College of Engineering, Patna (CSE → AI/DS → ECE → EE).
3. Established Heritage Colleges: Gaya College of Engineering (CSE → ECE) • Darbhanga College of Engineering (CSE → ECE) • Nalanda College of Engineering, Chandi (CSE → AI/DS → ECE) • Motihari College of Engineering • LNJPIT Chhapra.
4. Modern District GECs: Saharsa CE, Purnea CE, Sasaram CE, Katihar CE, Begusarai CE, Supaul CE (CSE / AI → ECE → Civil → Mechanical).

⚠️ Auto-Upgradation Strategy: "YES" vs "NO"

When Round 1 seat allotment is declared, you must choose your upgradation status wisely:

  • Upgradation = NO (Freeze): You are 100% satisfied with your Round 1 allotted college & branch. You take final admission and do NOT participate in Round 2.
  • Upgradation = YES (Float): You want to be considered for higher preferences in Round 2 while keeping your Round 1 seat locked as a guaranteed safety backup.
  • MANDATORY DV WARNING: Even if you choose Upgradation = YES, you MUST physically report to the designated Nodal Center for Document Verification in Round 1. If you fail to verify documents, your Round 1 seat is CANCELLED and you are barred from Round 2!

Step-by-Step UGEAC 2027 Admission Roadmap

Follow this 7-stage structured roadmap to navigate the BCECEB engineering admission workflow smoothly without errors.

Stage 1: JEE (Main) Paper-1 Result & CRL Score
Appear in JEE Main 2027 Session 1 or Session 2. Obtain your NTA scorecard with All India Common Rank List (CRL) rank. Candidates without a JEE Main CRL cannot apply for the primary rounds of UGEAC.
Stage 2: Online UGEAC Registration & Fee Payment
Visit bceceboard.bihar.gov.in → Click on "Online Portal of UGEAC-2027". Register with JEE Main Application ID and Date of Birth. Fill personal info, upload photo and signature, pay the counselling fee (₹1,200 for General/EWS/BC/EBC; ₹600 for SC/ST/DQ).
Stage 3: State Merit Rank Card & Hard Copy Download
BCECEB publishes the state-level merit list mapping JEE Main CRLs to Bihar UGEAC ranks. Download your State Rank Card and print Hard Copy Part-A and Part-B of your online application. Keep multiple copies safe.
Stage 4: Web Option Choice Filling & OTP Locking
Log into your candidate portal and enter your desired combinations of college and branch. You can add unlimited choices. Sequence them by quality and verify your preference order. Lock your choices using mobile OTP before the deadline.
Stage 5: Round 1 Seat Allotment & Upgradation Decision
Download your Provisional Seat Allotment Order. If you are satisfied with the college and branch, select "Freeze (Upgradation = No)". If you want to participate in Round 2 while retaining your current seat as backup, select "Float (Upgradation = Yes)".
Stage 6: Physical Document Verification at Nodal Center
Report physically to the designated Reporting / Nodal Center with original certificates, 6 passport-size photographs (matching JEE Main admit card), biometric identification slip, and Part A & B forms. Mandatory even for Upgradation = Yes candidates.
Stage 7: Final College Reporting & Class Commencement
Report to your finalized Government Engineering College with the Document Verification Slip issued by the Nodal Center. Complete campus enrollment, pay the annual admission fee (~₹12,000), register for hostel accommodation, and begin B.Tech orientation under Bihar Engineering University (BEU).

Frequently Asked Questions About UGEAC Bihar 2027

Find authoritative answers to the most common queries regarding BCECEB engineering counseling, college choices, reservations, and admission rules.

What is UGEAC Bihar and who is eligible to apply?
UGEAC (Under Graduate Engineering Admission Counselling) is the centralized counselling conducted by the Bihar Combined Entrance Competitive Examination Board (BCECEB) for admission into 4-year B.Tech programs in 38 Government Engineering Colleges in Bihar. Candidates who have passed 10+2 with Physics and Mathematics (minimum 45% marks for UR/EWS, 40% for reserved categories), appeared in JEE Main Paper-1, and hold Bihar domicile eligibility can apply.
Is JEE Main mandatory for UGEAC 2027?
Yes, appearing in NTA JEE (Main) Paper-1 and possessing a valid All India Common Rank List (CRL) is mandatory for UGEAC registration. All regular counselling rounds for the 38 Government Engineering Colleges are conducted solely based on JEE Main CRL ranks. If seats remain vacant after all UGEAC rounds, BCECEB conducts a separate round through BCECE (PCM) merit.
How does the 33% female reservation work in Bihar engineering colleges?
Under the Bihar Government's Mukhyamantri Kanya Utthan / Saat Nischay-2 initiative, 33% horizontal reservation is legally reserved for female candidates across every category (UR, EWS, BC, EBC, SC, ST) in all 38 Government Engineering Colleges. This means girls get admission at significantly relaxed closing CRL ranks compared to male candidates in the same category.
What is the difference between 'Upgradation = Yes' and 'Upgradation = No'?
If you choose 'Upgradation = No (Freeze)', you accept your Round 1 seat as final, complete admission, and do not participate in subsequent rounds. If you choose 'Upgradation = Yes (Float)', your Round 1 seat is held securely as a backup while you are considered for higher preferences in Round 2. However, even if you choose 'Yes', you MUST report to your assigned Nodal Center for document verification in Round 1, or your seat will be cancelled.
Can other state candidates apply for Bihar Government Engineering Colleges?
Bihar Government Engineering Colleges primarily reserve 85% to 100% of seats for Bihar permanent residents and wards of government personnel stationed in Bihar. Candidates without Bihar domicile can generally apply only for private engineering colleges participating in UGEAC or unreserved vacancies if explicitly opened by BCECEB in mop-up rounds.
What is the fee structure for Government Engineering Colleges in Bihar?
Tuition fees in Bihar Government Engineering Colleges are heavily subsidized by the state government, typically ranging between ₹10,000 to ₹15,000 per year (total 4-year B.Tech fee approx ₹45,000 to ₹60,000). Students can also utilize the Bihar Student Credit Card Scheme (MNSSBY) which provides education loans up to ₹4 Lakhs at negligible interest rates (1% for girls/disabled, 4% for boys).
What are the top 3 Government Engineering Colleges in Bihar?
The top 3 state government engineering institutes in Bihar are: 1) Muzaffarpur Institute of Technology (MIT Muzaffarpur, estd 1954), 2) Bhagalpur College of Engineering (BCE Bhagalpur, estd 1960), and 3) Bakhtiyarpur College of Engineering (Patna district, estd 2016). They are followed by Gaya College of Engineering, Darbhanga College of Engineering, and Nalanda College of Engineering Chandi.
What is the UGEAC counselling registration fee?
The online registration and counseling fee is ₹1,200 for General (UR), EWS, BC, and EBC candidates, and ₹600 for SC, ST, and Differently Abled (DQ) candidates. The fee is non-refundable and payable online via net banking, credit/debit card, or UPI.
