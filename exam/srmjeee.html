SRMJEEE 2027 Examination & B.Tech Admission Architecture

The SRM Joint Engineering Entrance Examination (SRMJEEE) is the mandatory entrance channel for B.Tech aspirants seeking admission across SRM Institute of Science and Technology's premier campuses in Kattankulathur (Chennai Main Campus), Ramapuram, Vadapalani, Tiruchirappalli, as well as SRM University AP (Amaravati) and SRM University Delhi-NCR (Sonepat & Modinagar).

Remote Online Proctored Exam (RPOM)

SRMJEEE pioneered home-based online proctoring in India. Candidates appear from their home desktops or laptops equipped with webcams and microphones under real-time AI computer vision and human invigilator surveillance, eliminating travel overhead.

3 Distinct Examination Phases

Aspirants get multiple opportunities across Phase 1 (April), Phase 2 (June), and Phase 3 (July). If a student misses or underperforms in Phase 1 due to Board exams or other commitments, they can reapply in Phase 2 or 3 to improve their rank.

Global Accreditations & Tier 1 Recruiters

SRMIST holds NAAC A++ grade (CGPA 3.55/4.0), Category-I Institution status by MHRD/UGC, and select engineering programs are accredited by the international ABET (USA) Engineering Accreditation Commission, driving 8,000+ annual campus job offers.

Zero Negative Marking Advantage

Unlike JEE Main (-1 for wrong answers) or MET/BITSAT, SRMJEEE features zero negative marking. Every candidate should attempt all 125 questions to maximize their aggregate score and overall merit ranking.

Admission Prerequisites

SRMJEEE 2027 Eligibility Criteria & Direct Admission Rules

Review the mandatory educational requirements, subject combinations, campus-wise aggregate cutoffs, and direct admission provisions for national toppers.

Academic & Age Eligibility

  • Nationality: Resident Indians, Non-Resident Indians (NRIs), PIO, or OCI cardholders are eligible to apply.
  • Age Limit: Candidates must have completed 17 years of age as on December 31, 2027 (born on or before December 31, 2010).
  • Qualifying Examination: Passed 10+2 / Higher Secondary examination from CBSE, ISC, State Board, or recognized international board in 2027 or 2026.
  • KTR, Ramapuram & Vadapalani Minimum Marks: Minimum 60% aggregate in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM) or Biology (PCB for biotech).
  • SRM AP & Delhi-NCR Minimum Marks: Minimum 50% aggregate in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics.

Direct Admission & Merit Exemptions

  • JEE Main Top Rankers: Candidates with a rank under Top 1,000 in JEE Main are eligible for direct admission with full scholarships without appearing for SRMJEEE.
  • State & Central Board Toppers: First rank holders in any Indian Central or State Board are offered direct admission with the 100% Founder's Scholarship.
  • Exemplary Sports Persons: Candidates representing India or state teams in recognized National/International championships qualify for direct admission under sports quota.
  • District Toppers: Top rankers in rural Tamil Nadu districts qualify for dedicated fee concessions.

Remote Online Proctoring (RPOM) Rules

  • Device: Desktop PC or Laptop running Windows 10/11 or macOS with working camera & microphone. Smartphones/tablets NOT permitted for taking exam.
  • Dual Camera Setup: Primary webcam continuously records candidate face; secondary mobile phone camera placed behind records workspace and screen.
  • Internet Speed: Continuous high-speed broadband / mobile hotspot with at least 1 Mbps upload/download speed.
  • Proctoring AI Checks: AI algorithm flags face absence, multiple faces, gaze shifts, audio noise, screen toggling, and browser tab switches.
  • Mandatory Mock Test: Candidates MUST appear in the scheduled trial mock test 2 days before the actual exam to test hardware compatibility.

Allotment Process

SRM Centralized Online Counselling: Step-by-Step Roadmap

A clear step-by-step walkthrough of how SRMIST conducts centralized online seat allocation across all campuses through srmist.edu.in.

Step 1: Rank Card Release & Counselling Registration

Following exam conclusion, SRMIST publishes individual All-India Rank Cards. Candidates log in to the admission portal to verify eligibility and confirm their registration for online choice allocation.

Step 2: Choice Filling & Campus-Branch Prioritization

Candidates select their desired campus (KTR, Ramapuram, Vadapalani, AP, NCR) and branch combinations in order of priority. Strategy tip: Place KTR CSE and CSE specializations at the top, followed by Ramapuram/AP CSE, and KTR ECE.

Step 3: Provisional Seat Allotment Result

SRMIST generates seat allotments based on candidate SRMJEEE merit rank, submitted preferences, and seat availability in each campus. Allotted candidates receive an online Provisional Allotment Letter.

Step 4: Part Payment of Tuition Fee (₹1,10,000)

To confirm the allotted seat, candidates MUST deposit a Part Payment of Tuition Fee (typically ₹1,10,000 including registration fee) online within the stipulated 4 to 5 day window. Failure to pay leads to immediate seat forfeiture.

Step 5: Document Verification & Balance Fee Settlement

Candidates upload required 10th and 12th marksheets, conduct certificates, and identity proofs for online verification. The remaining balance tuition fee and hostel charges must be settled before physical orientation.

Step 6: Subsequent Phase Counselling & Upgradations

Unfilled or vacant seats from Phase 1 are carried forward into Phase 2 and Phase 3 counselling rounds. Candidates who appeared in later phases get allotments based on remaining vacancies.

SRMIST Scholarships, Tuition Fee Waivers & Concessions

SRMIST awards over ₹30+ Crore worth of scholarships annually to reward academic excellence and support deserving students.

Founder's Scholarship

100% Full Waiver + Hostel

The most prestigious scholarship at SRMIST. Awarded to top-tier achievers across national and state entrance examinations:

  • SRMJEEE Rank 1 to 100: 100% tuition fee waiver, 100% hostel room & mess waiver, and a monthly stipend.
  • State & Central Board Toppers: 100% full waiver for 1st rank holders of all recognized State and Central education boards.
  • JEE Main Top 1,000: 100% tuition and accommodation waiver for top 1,000 All India Rankers in JEE Main.

SRM Merit Scholarships

25% to 100% Tuition Waiver

Tiered tuition fee concessions based on candidate SRMJEEE merit ranking:

  • SRMJEEE Rank 101 to 500: 75% Tuition Fee Waiver for the entire 4-year tenure.
  • SRMJEEE Rank 501 to 1,000: 50% Tuition Fee Waiver.
  • SRMJEEE Rank 1,001 to 2,000: 25% Tuition Fee Waiver.
  • Renewal Rule: Scholars must maintain a minimum CGPA of 8.0/10.0 with zero backlogs and 85%+ attendance in every semester.
