AP EAPCET 2027 Overview & Key Highlights

The Andhra Pradesh Engineering, Agriculture and Pharmacy Common Entrance Test (AP EAPCET) is the apex state-level gateway conducted by JNTU Kakinada on behalf of the Andhra Pradesh State Council of Higher Education (APSCHE) for undergraduate admissions across government universities, constituent colleges, and private institutions.

Exam Full Name Andhra Pradesh Engineering, Agriculture and Pharmacy Common Entrance Test (AP EAPCET 2027)
Conducting University Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University Kakinada (JNTUK) on behalf of APSCHE
Governing Authority Andhra Pradesh State Council of Higher Education (APSCHE)
Official Portals Exam Portal: cets.apsche.ap.gov.in/EAPCET
Counselling Portal: eapcet-sche.aptonline.in
Mode of Examination Computer Based Test (CBT) across multiple sessions with shift-wise score normalization
Streams & Paper Structure Engineering Stream (MPC): Maths (80) + Physics (40) + Chemistry (40) = 160 Questions
Agriculture & Pharmacy (BiPC): Biology (80) + Physics (40) + Chemistry (40) = 160 Questions
Marking Scheme +1 mark for every correct response • NO Negative Marking
Minimum Qualifying Marks 25% of total marks (40 out of 160 marks) for OC / BC candidates. No minimum qualifying mark for SC / ST candidates.
Seat Quota Structure Category A (Convenor Quota): 70% seats filled via AP EAPCET merit & state reservation rules (JVD eligible)
Category B (Management/NRI): 30% seats filled via JEE Main / AP EAPCET / 10+2 marks
State Welfare Benefits Jagananna Vidya Deevena (100% Tuition Fee Reimbursement) & Jagananna Vasathi Deevena (₹20,000/yr hostel assistance) for eligible students

🎯 Convenor Quota (Category A)

Allotted exclusively through AP EAPCET centralized web counselling by APSCHE across 70% of total state intake.

  • Strictly adheres to AU & SVU 85% regional local reservations.
  • Statutory category reservations for OC, EWS, BC-A/B/C/D/E, SC, ST.
  • Full government fee reimbursement eligible for qualifying candidates.

💰 Jagananna Vidya Deevena (JVD)

Complete 100% tuition fee reimbursement credited by the Andhra Pradesh State Government.

  • Family annual income must not exceed ₹2.50 Lakhs per annum.
  • Must hold an active White Ration Card / Rice Card in Andhra Pradesh.
  • Includes Vasathi Deevena food and accommodation allowance (₹20,000/yr).

📍 AU vs SVU Presidential Order

Under the AP Educational Institutions Presidential Order 1974, seats are zoned into two local regions.

  • 85% of seats in AU region institutions reserved for coastal Andhra local students.
  • 85% of seats in SVU region institutions reserved for Rayalaseema & Nellore students.
  • 15% unreserved statewide merit quota open to all candidates.

⚖️ Shift Normalization Equation

APSCHE employs a statistical normalization formula across multi-session CBTs to ensure parity:

  • Adjusts raw marks based on global mean and session top 0.1% performance.
  • Guarantees candidates in tougher shifts are not unfairly penalized.
  • Published normalized score is used directly for rank compilation.

📊 75:25 Composite Weightage

When Class 12 board marks integration is activated by the state government:

  • 75% Weightage: Normalized score obtained in AP EAPCET entrance test.
  • 25% Weightage: Group marks obtained in PCM/PCB in Intermediate/10+2.
  • Normalized across BIEAP, CBSE, ICSE, and other recognized boards.

🎖️ Qualifying Cutoff Marks

Thresholds mandated by APSCHE to be eligible for rank card generation:

  • OC / BC / EWS: Minimum 25% (40 marks out of 160).
  • SC / ST: No minimum qualifying mark is prescribed.
  • Candidates scoring below 40 marks (unreserved) cannot access web options.

Jagananna Vidya Deevena (JVD) Scheme Rules

Under the Andhra Pradesh State Higher Education Department rules, 100% complete tuition fee reimbursement is disbursed provided the candidate fulfills all five criteria:

  • Convenor Quota Admission: Admitted strictly under Category A through AP EAPCET web counselling. Management / Category B seats are NOT eligible.
  • Income Criterion: Total annual family income from all sources must not exceed ₹2,50,000 (₹2.50 Lakhs).
  • Ration Card: Must hold a valid White Ration Card / Rice Card issued by the AP Civil Supplies Department.
  • Land Ceiling: Total family landholding should not exceed 10 acres of wetland or 25 acres of dry land.
  • No Government Employment: No family member should be a permanent government employee or pension-drawing pensioner (sanitary workers exempted), and family must not own a four-wheeler (commercial taxis, autos, tractors exempted).

Step-by-Step APSCHE Web Counselling Roadmap

A visual walkthrough of the complete admission lifecycle — from online fee payment and certificate verification to mandatory dual reporting.

1️⃣ Registration & Fee Payment

Log in to eapcet-sche.aptonline.in using AP EAPCET Hall Ticket and DOB. Pay processing fee online.

  • ₹1,200 for OC and BC candidates; ₹600 for SC and ST candidates.
  • Download payment receipt with transaction ID.

2️⃣ Certificate Verification

APSCHE verifies candidate data automatically via Integrated API connected to Civil Supplies, Intermediate, and MeeSeva databases.

  • Candidates with matching data are cleared online directly.
  • Data mismatch or Special Categories (PH, CAP, NCC, Sports) report to HLC.

3️⃣ Exercising Web Options

Log in with Hall Ticket number and OTP to fill preferences on the candidate web options portal.

  • No upper limit on choices — fill 50 to 80+ combinations.
  • Prioritize choices in strict descending order of your true preference.

4️⃣ Options Freezing & Modification

Review submitted options during the modification window. If satisfied, freeze choices before the deadline.

  • Saved options get frozen automatically at deadline if not locked manually.
  • Download and print the frozen options list for record.

5️⃣ Seat Allotment Publication

APSCHE executes automated seat allocation based on EAPCET rank, local area, gender reservation, and choice hierarchy.

  • Download the Provisional Seat Allotment Order and Joining Letter.
  • Verify fee payment details and allotted college reporting instructions.

6️⃣ Mandatory Dual Reporting

Candidates MUST complete two reporting stages to prevent seat forfeiture:

  • Online Self-Reporting: Click self-reporting online to lock seat.
  • College Reporting: Physically report with original certificates and fee challan.

Frequently Asked Questions (AP EAPCET 2027)

Clear, authoritative answers to the most critical doubts regarding APSCHE web counselling, local regions, JVD fee waivers, and seat allocation rules.

What is AP EAPCET 2027 and who conducts it? +

AP EAPCET (Andhra Pradesh Engineering, Agriculture and Pharmacy Common Entrance Test) is the state-level entrance exam conducted annually by Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University Kakinada (JNTUK) under the supervision of the Andhra Pradesh State Council of Higher Education (APSCHE) for admission into professional undergraduate degree courses across Andhra Pradesh.

What is the difference between AU and SVU regions in AP EAPCET? +

Under the AP Educational Institutions Presidential Order 1974, Andhra Pradesh is divided into two local university zones:

1. Andhra University (AU) Region: Coastal Andhra districts (Visakhapatnam, Kakinada, East Godavari, West Godavari, Krishna, NTR Vijayawada, Guntur, Bapatla, Palnadu, Srikakulam, Vizianagaram).
2. Sri Venkateswara University (SVU) Region: Rayalaseema & southern districts (Tirupati, Chittoor, YSR Kadapa, Annamayya, Kurnool, Nandyal, Anantapur, Sri Sathya Sai, SPSR Nellore, Prakasam).

85% of seats in institutions within each region are reserved for candidates who studied for a minimum of 4 consecutive years in that area. The remaining 15% are unreserved statewide seats.

How is the AP EAPCET rank calculated? Is there Intermediate weightage? +

AP EAPCET rank is based on normalized CBT entrance examination scores. When the state government implements Class 12th group marks integration, ranking is determined using a 75:25 composite formula: 75% weightage for the AP EAPCET normalized score and 25% weightage for Intermediate / 10+2 marks in Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry (or Biology) with normalization across CBSE, ICSE, and state boards.

What are the minimum qualifying marks in AP EAPCET? +

Candidates belonging to OC, BC, and EWS categories must score at least 25% of the aggregate marks, which is 40 marks out of 160 in the entrance test to qualify for a rank. There are no minimum qualifying marks for SC and ST candidates; all SC/ST candidates who appear are allotted ranks and considered under reserved vacancies.

What is Jagananna Vidya Deevena (JVD) and who receives 100% fee reimbursement? +

Jagananna Vidya Deevena is the Andhra Pradesh State Government's complete fee reimbursement program. Students admitted under Convener Quota (Category A) whose family annual income is up to ₹2.50 Lakhs (possessing an active White Ration Card / Rice Card, conforming to wetland/dryland limits, and having no government employee parents or personal four-wheelers) receive 100% full tuition fee reimbursement paid directly by the state government.

What is the mandatory dual reporting process in AP EAPCET? +

Upon receiving a seat allotment, candidates must complete two distinct actions:

1. Online Self-Reporting: Log in to the official portal (eapcet-sche.aptonline.in) and click "Self-Reporting" to confirm acceptance and generate a provisional admission receipt.
2. Physical Reporting: Visit the allotted college in person within the designated deadline with original certificates, photocopies, and fee challan.

Failure to complete both self-reporting and physical reporting results in automatic cancellation of the allotted seat.

How many web options can a candidate fill during AP EAPCET counselling? +

There is no upper limit on the number of options you can submit. Candidates can select and prioritize as many college and branch combinations as they wish. Experts recommend filling at least 40 to 70+ choices to maximize admission probability across top, target, and safety institutes.

What is the difference between Category A and Category B seats? +

Category A (Convenor Quota): Represents 70% of total sanctioned intake in private engineering colleges and 100% in university constituent colleges. Allotment is conducted centrally by APSCHE based on AP EAPCET rank and statutory reservation rules at government-fixed tuition fees. Eligible candidates receive JVD fee reimbursement.

Category B (Management Quota): Represents 30% of seats in private colleges, filled directly by college managements under open merit via JEE Main, AP EAPCET, or Class 12th marks at higher fee caps (NOT eligible for JVD).
