UPTAC 2027 Overview & Key Highlights

Uttar Pradesh Technical Admission Counselling (UPTAC 2027) is the single centralized admission window conducted by Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU), Lucknow for undergraduate engineering and professional admissions across 300+ government and private institutions in Uttar Pradesh.

📜 Statutory Domicile Categories

Category A: Candidates who have completed both 10th and 10+2 from an institution situated in Uttar Pradesh. No separate domicile certificate is needed; marksheet proves domicile.

Category B: Candidates who passed 10+2 from outside UP, but at least one parent is a bona fide resident of UP possessing a state domicile certificate issued by the Tehsildar.

Category C (All India Quota): Candidates who completed 10+2 outside UP and whose parents do not reside in UP. Eligible for 15% open seats in government colleges and private institutes.

⚠️ Category Certificate Time Validity

OBC-NCL Certificate Rule: The Other Backward Class Non-Creamy Layer (OBC-NCL) certificate MUST have been issued on or after April 1, 2026 (or April 1 of the admission calendar year) by a competent UP Revenue Officer (Tehsildar). Central OBC certificates are not accepted for state quota.

EWS Certificate Rule: Economically Weaker Section certificates must be valid for the financial year 2026–2027 and issued based on gross annual family income below ₹8 Lakhs.

UPTAC 2027 Step-by-Step Counselling Roadmap

A comprehensive walkthrough of every sequential phase required to register, select preferences, and secure admission to top UP engineering colleges.

1️⃣ Portal Registration

Visit uptac.admissions.nic.in. Enter your JEE Main Application Number, password, personal profile, mobile number, and email. Pay the non-refundable registration fee of ₹1,000.

2️⃣ Document Upload & Verification

Upload scanned copies of 10th & 12th marks cards, UP domicile certificate, and category certificate (OBC-NCL after April 1 / EWS). AKTU officers verify documents online without requiring physical travel.

3️⃣ Strategic Choice Filling

Select colleges and branches from the portal menu. There is no maximum limit—fill at least 40 to 60 choices arranged strictly in order of personal preference. Lock choices before the timer expires.

4️⃣ Allotment & Willingness (Freeze/Float)

Upon allotment, pay the Seat Acceptance Fee (₹20,000 for Gen/OBC or ₹12,000 for SC/ST). Choose Freeze if you are 100% satisfied, or Float to compete for higher options.

5️⃣ Physical Institutional Reporting

Report in person to the allotted college before the designated deadline. Present original certificates, medical fitness certificate, character certificate, and pay the net balance tuition fees.

6️⃣ Internal Sliding & Spot Rounds

Admitted students in government colleges participate in online Internal Sliding for intra-college branch upgrades. Unallotted candidates can attend Special Rounds 6 & 7 for open seats.

UPTAC 2027 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Essential answers to critical queries on seat reservation, cutoff ranks, choice locking, and institutional reporting.

Uttar Pradesh Technical Admission Counselling (UPTAC) is conducted by Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU), Lucknow for undergraduate B.Tech admissions across 300+ institutions. Participants include autonomous state government colleges (IET Lucknow, KNIT Sultanpur, BIET Jhansi), state university departments (UIET CSJMU Kanpur, RMLAU Ayodhya, CCSU Meerut), 8 Rajkiya Engineering Colleges (RECs), and top-tier private colleges in Delhi NCR (JSS Noida, AKGEC, KIET, Galgotias, GL Bajaj, ABES).
Yes, non-UP residents can apply under the 15% All India Quota (AIQ) in government colleges (IET, KNIT, BIET, etc.) and open seats in private engineering colleges based on their JEE Main All India Common Rank List (CRL) in the open merit category.
IET Lucknow is the highest-ranked AKTU college. For UP Domicile General (UP_OPEN), CSE closing rank is typically around 24,000 to 36,000 CRL. For All India candidates, it closes around 18,000 to 24,000 CRL. For UP OBC-NCL, it closes around 42,000 to 52,000; for UP EWS around 35,000 to 45,000; for UP SC around 1,40,000 to 1,75,000; and for UP ST around 3,20,000 to 3,80,000 CRL.
Under the AICTE TFW scheme, 5% supernumerary seats are created in every approved engineering branch. These seats are available to UP domicile students whose gross annual family income is below ₹8,00,000. Admitted students pay zero tuition fees for all 4 years of their degree.
A 20% horizontal reservation is provided to female candidates with UP domicile across all vertical categories (General Girls, OBC Girls, SC Girls, ST Girls, and EWS Girls). This allows female candidates to secure top branches at noticeably relaxed cutoff ranks.
Paying the Seat Acceptance Fee (₹20,000 for Gen/OBC/EWS or ₹12,000 for SC/ST) is mandatory upon allotment. If a candidate fails to pay within the round deadline, their allotted seat is cancelled, and they are disqualified from participating in subsequent regular rounds.
Internal Sliding is conducted online in Round 5 exclusively for candidates who have reported physically to government engineering colleges in Round 4. Students can apply for higher branch upgrades within their allotted college without losing their already secured seat.
Special Rounds (Rounds 6 & 7) are conducted by AKTU to fill remaining vacant seats after Round 5. Unallotted candidates, fresh applicants, or those who withdrew earlier can register and compete for available seats across government and private institutions.
