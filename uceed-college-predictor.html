Understanding UCEED Joint Seat Allocation & Choice Filling

B.Des seat allocation in participating IITs (IIT Bombay IDC, IIT Delhi, IIT Guwahati, IIT Hyderabad, IIT Roorkee) and IIITDM Jabalpur is organized centrally by IIT Bombay. Seat allotment is based strictly on candidate's UCEED All India Rank (AIR), category merit, and institute choice ordering across multiple rounds.

IIT B.Des Fee Structure & Scholarships

  • Participating IITs (IIT Bombay, IIT Delhi, IIT Guwahati, IIT Hyderabad, IIT Roorkee): Tuition fee is approx. ₹1.0 - ₹1.25 Lakh per semester.
  • Full Tuition Fee Waiver (100%): 100% tuition fee waiver for SC, ST, and PwD candidates, and students whose family income is below ₹1 Lakh/year.
  • 2/3rd Tuition Remission (66.6%): 2/3rd tuition fee remission for General/OBC candidates whose family annual income is between ₹1 Lakh and ₹5 Lakhs.

UCEED B.Des Admissions & Quota Distribution

Understanding category reservation quotas and merit rank dynamics for IIT Joint Seat Allocation and Result Sharing Universities.

UCEED Reservation Structure

IIT UCEED seat allocation follows central government statutory quotas:

  • OBC-NCL Reservation (27%): 27% seats reserved for Non-Creamy Layer OBC candidates across participating IITs.
  • SC (15%) & ST (7.5%): Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe candidates receive reserved seats in rank order.
  • EWS Reservation (10%): 10% seats reserved for Economically Weaker Section candidates in central government institutes.
  • PwD Reservation (5%): 5% horizontal reservation for Persons with Disability in each category.

Joint Seat Allotment Factors

Closing ranks across rounds are governed by:

  • UCEED All India Rank (AIR): Final rank computed from Part A (Objective) and Part B (Drawing/Design Aptitude) out of 300 marks.
  • Preference Choice Order: Allotment strictly follows applicant preference hierarchy during counselling.
  • Counselling Rounds: Round 1, Round 2, and Round 3 joint seat allocation reports.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the UCEED College Predictor and how does it work?
The UCEED College Predictor is a free tool that estimates your B.Des admission chances in premier IITs (IIT Bombay IDC, IIT Delhi, IIT Guwahati, IIT Hyderabad, IIT Roorkee), IIITDM Jabalpur, DTU, NSUT, CEPT, and top design schools. Enter your UCEED rank or score out of 300, select your reservation category, and the tool compares your rank against official joint seat allocation closing ranks to show High, Medium, or Low admission probabilities.
How does UCEED Joint Seat Allocation choice filling work?
UCEED joint seat allocation allows candidates to select institute preferences in order of choice. Seat allotment occurs strictly in All India Rank (AIR) and category rank order. Once a choice is allotted, lower preference choices are discarded, making your preference list sequence critical.
Is the predicted B.Des seat guaranteed?
No. Actual seat allotments are determined by IIT Bombay UCEED seat allocation authority based on candidate rank, filled preference list, category seat availability, and actual closing cutoffs in the current admission cycle.
What is the difference between Participating and Result Sharing Institutes?
Participating Institutes (IIT Bombay, IIT Delhi, IIT Guwahati, IIT Hyderabad, IIT Roorkee, IIITDM Jabalpur) participate in the central Joint Seat Allocation process. Result Sharing Institutes (such as DTU, NSUT, CEPT, Srishti Manipal, WUD, etc.) accept UCEED scores for B.Des admission via their own separate application processes.
What fee concessions or scholarships are available in IIT B.Des programs?
IITs provide 100% tuition fee waivers for SC, ST, PwD, and candidates with family income under ₹1 Lakh/year. A 66.6% tuition fee remission is offered to candidates with family annual income between ₹1 Lakh and ₹5 Lakhs.
