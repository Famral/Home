GAS vs SFS Seats: Fee Structure Comparison

Government engineering colleges in Rajasthan (MBM, CTAE, RTU UD, ECA) offer two distinct seat categories: GAS (Govt Aided) and SFS (Self-Financing). Understanding this distinction saves lakhs in tuition fees!

Government Aided (GAS)

GAS Seat (Subsidized)

₹15,000 - ₹35,000 / Year
  • ✔ Subsidized by State Government funds.
  • ✔ Highest competition; lowest closing ranks.
  • ✔ Available in premier branches at MBM, CTAE, RTU Kota.
  • ✔ Identical faculty, labs, classrooms & degree.
*Tip: Always place GAS seats higher than SFS in your choice list!
Self-Financing (SFS)

SFS Seat (Regular Govt)

₹65,000 - ₹95,000 / Year
  • ✔ Self-financed seats in top government colleges.
  • ✔ More seats available than GAS in CSE/IT.
  • ✔ Moderate cutoffs compared to GAS.
  • ✔ 100% same degree from RTU / MBM University.
*Affordable alternative to expensive private colleges.
AICTE Fee Waiver

TFWS Seat (Zero Tuition)

₹0 Tuition (Nominal ₹5k-10k)
  • ✔ 100% Tuition Fee waived for all 4 years.
  • ✔ 5% supernumerary seats in every single branch.
  • ✔ Available across both Govt & Top Private colleges.
  • ✔ Mandatory income certificate (< ₹8.0 Lakhs/yr).
*Allocated strictly in Round 1 before general rounds!

Rajasthan Domicile Eligibility: Rule 3 Clauses

To qualify for the 85% Rajasthan state quota, candidates must satisfy at least ONE of the following official Rule 3 clauses:

Clause 3(a)

Bonafide Resident of Rajasthan

The candidate or either of the parents is a natural Bonafide Resident of the State of Rajasthan with a valid Mool Niwas certificate issued by a competent Revenue authority.

Clause 3(b)

Continuous 3 Years Schooling

The candidate has studied continuously for at least 3 preceding academic years in recognized educational institutions located in Rajasthan, including the qualifying Class 12th exam.

Clause 3(c)

Govt / PSU Employee Parents

Children of employees of the Government of Rajasthan, Rajasthan State Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs), or Central Govt/All India Services officers stationed in Rajasthan for at least 2 years.

Clause 3(d)

Defence & Ex-Servicemen

Children of serving defence personnel or ex-servicemen belonging to Rajasthan or currently posted in military cantonments within Rajasthan state borders.

REAP 2027 Counselling Schedule (Tentative)

Expected chronological timeline for registration, choice locking, merit declaration, and multi-round seat allocations.

Phase 1 • Mid June 2027

Online Registration & Application Fee Payment

Candidates register on the official REAP portal (https://reap2027.in), pay the non-refundable registration fee of ₹700, and input personal, academic (10th/12th), JEE Main CRL, and category details.

Phase 2 • Early July 2027

Document Upload & Choice Filling Window

Candidates upload digital certificates (domicile, income, caste) and fill their prioritized college-branch preference list. Candidates can fill unlimited choices across participating Rajasthan institutes.

Phase 3 • Mid July 2027

REAP 2027 State Merit List Declaration

CEG publishes the combined state merit list and category-wise ranks. Priority 1 (JEE Main CRL) candidates are positioned above Priority 2 (12th PCM) candidates.

Phase 4 • Late July 2027

Round 1 Allotment (Out of State, PwD, KM & TFWS)

Special allotment round for Outside Rajasthan (15% AI quota), Kashmiri Migrants, Persons with Disabilities, and Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme (TFWS) applicants.

Phase 5 • Early August 2027

Round 2 Allotment (Rajasthan Domicile Main Round)

General & category seat allotment for 85% Rajasthan domicile seats. Students must accept their seat online and choose whether to freeze or opt for upward sliding.

Phase 6 • Mid August 2027

Round 3: Upward Movement (Sliding Round)

Automatic vacancy-based upgradation of seats to higher filled preferences without losing the previously allotted seat.

Phase 7 • Late August 2027

Special Spot / Institutional Management Round

Direct institute-level physical reporting and spot round conducted by individual colleges to fill any remaining vacant seats.

Smart Choice Filling Strategy for REAP 2027

Master the rules of preference ordering to avoid common choice-locking mistakes made by candidates every year.

1. The 3-Tier Choice Basket Model

Divide your choice list into 3 categories: Dream / Reach Choices (top 20% - MBM CSE GAS, CTAE CSE GAS), Target / Realistic Choices (middle 50% - RTU CSE SFS, SKIT CSE, ECA CSE), and Safe / Fallback Choices (bottom 30% - core branches or private options).

2. Order GAS Seats Strictly Above SFS Seats

Never place an SFS seat above a GAS seat of the same branch in the same college! If you place RTU CSE SFS above RTU CSE GAS, the algorithm will allot you the expensive SFS seat even if your merit was high enough for the low-cost GAS seat.

3. Always Opt for Upward Movement

If allotted a seat in Round 1 or Round 2 that is not your #1 dream choice, report and pay the confirmation fee, then choose "YES" for Upward Movement. You have zero risk of losing your allotted seat while remaining eligible for an upgrade!
